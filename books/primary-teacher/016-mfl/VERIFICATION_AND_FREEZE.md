## Freeze conditions confirmed

The MFL module is frozen because:

- The framework is complete and internally consistent.
- The module contains exactly 100 workflows.
- Structural QA is complete.
- Representative execution testing is complete.
- Targeted MFL risk testing is complete.
- Target-language content is controlled through verification safeguards where the target language is unspecified or where accuracy requires checking.
- Generated language is not falsely presented as authentic material.
- Cultural content includes safeguards against stereotyping, tokenism and overgeneralisation.
- Assessment workflows distinguish language-learning constructs from confidence, personality or personal disclosure.
- Inclusion workflows preserve the intended construct rather than automatically reducing challenge.
- SEND and EAL handling does not make diagnostic claims.
- Copyright, privacy and digital-resource safeguards are included where relevant.
- School-specific procedures, local syllabus requirements and current external information are not invented.
- The agreed prompt architecture and metadata standards are retained.
- No em dash character is used in the module verification record.

## Publication note

This freeze records verification of the repository prompt workflows. Representative execution and targeted risk testing were qualitative in-model checks performed with GPT-5.6 Luna. They do not constitute automated regression testing across every supported AI platform, external API, language model, browser environment or school information system.
