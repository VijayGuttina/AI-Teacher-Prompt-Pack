# MFL Family 6: Reading, Writing, Translation & Mediation

This family contains 10 copy/paste-ready workflows for developing primary MFL reading, writing, translation and mediation through accurate, cumulative and communicative language learning.

## Family principles

- Reading and writing should have a clear communicative or curriculum purpose.
- Build reading from recognition and decoding towards comprehension, interpretation and transfer.
- Build writing from supported production towards increasingly independent communication.
- Translation should develop meaning, language awareness and accuracy rather than word-for-word substitution alone.
- Mediation should require pupils to convey meaning appropriately for a recipient or purpose.
- Distinguish language knowledge from handwriting, copying, speed and confidence.
- Use language-specific forms only when verified.
- Never invent vocabulary, grammar, quotations, authentic texts or source attributions.
- If the target language is unspecified, use `[TARGET LANGUAGE]` and mark uncertain language-specific content `[VERIFY]`.
- Do not assume a particular translation is the only acceptable rendering where several accurate choices are possible.
- Preserve intended constructs when adapting for SEND, EAL or other access needs.
- Cultural and contextual material must avoid stereotypes and overgeneralisation.
- Current factual or local information must be verified rather than invented.
- Teacher judgement remains central when interpreting pupil work.

### TASK

Design a complete reading lesson from pre-reading activation through decoding, comprehension, response and assessment.
