# Outdoor & Adventurous Activity

## OA-01 - Plan an Outdoor PE Lesson
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in outdoor and adventurous activities.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are learning [ACTIVITY OR SKILL] outdoors using [SPACE, EQUIPMENT AND CONDITIONS].
**TASK:** Create a safe, purposeful and progressive outdoor PE lesson.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the learning objective; account for the outdoor environment; maximise meaningful participation; include clear setup, boundaries, demonstrations and transitions; provide adaptation and challenge; identify relevant environmental and activity-specific safety controls.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide objective, success criteria, setup, teaching sequence, activities, adaptations, challenges, assessment checkpoints and safety considerations.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not invent site-specific hazards, emergency procedures or supervision ratios. Clearly flag anything that must follow school procedures or local site rules.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT], [CLASS SIZE] or [LESSON LENGTH].

## OA-02 - Teach Basic Orienteering Skills
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher experienced in introductory orienteering.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] need to develop [MAP READING, NAVIGATION OR ORIENTEERING SKILL] using [MAP TYPE AND SITE].
**TASK:** Create a progressive teaching sequence that develops navigation through practical activity.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Introduce only the map and navigation concepts needed; use a controlled practice area before increasing independence; include route choice and checking; establish boundaries, buddy arrangements and return procedures appropriate to the supplied school context.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide objective, key vocabulary, demonstration, practice stages, navigation challenge, adaptations, assessment indicators and safety controls.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Navigation instructions must match the stated map and environment. Do not assume pupils can safely leave a defined area without explicit school procedures.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [MAP TYPE], [SITE], [EQUIPMENT] or [TIME].

## OA-03 - Create a Navigation Challenge
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and outdoor learning designer.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will complete a navigation challenge within [DEFINED AREA] using [MAP OR CLUES].
**TASK:** Design a challenge that develops route planning, observation and decision-making.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the permitted area and boundaries; include a sequence of manageable checkpoints; provide a clear completion condition; include route verification; offer easier and harder routes; specify what happens if pupils become unsure of their location.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge brief, setup, instructions, checkpoint structure, success criteria, adaptations, extension and safety procedures.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Keep the challenge within the stated controlled environment. Do not create instructions that depend on unknown landmarks or site features.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SITE], [MAP], [CLASS SIZE] or [TIME].

## OA-04 - Develop Team Problem-Solving Outdoors
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in cooperative outdoor challenges.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] will solve [PHYSICAL OR NAVIGATION PROBLEM] in teams.
**TASK:** Create an active team challenge that requires communication, planning and movement.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the problem and constraints; give each pupil a meaningful role or contribution route; include planning before action; allow teams to test and revise strategies; avoid unsafe climbing, lifting or movement demands.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge, setup, constraints, team process, success criteria, adaptations, extension, observation criteria and safety controls.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Team success should depend on collaboration and problem-solving rather than one physically dominant pupil completing the task.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CHALLENGE], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## OA-05 - Plan an Outdoor Adventure Circuit
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs safe outdoor activity stations.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need practice in [OUTDOOR SKILLS] using [SPACE AND EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Create an outdoor circuit that maintains activity time and develops the stated skills.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include 4 to 8 stations; define movement routes and boundaries; avoid bottlenecks; provide progression and adaptation; include equipment checks and weather or surface considerations where relevant.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide circuit overview, station table, setup, timings, rotation, differentiation, assessment focus and safety checks.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Every station must be feasible in the stated environment and must not introduce unsupported hazards or specialist equipment.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [SKILLS].

## OA-06 - Adapt Outdoor Activity for Weather and Environment
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who plans resilient outdoor learning.
**CONTEXT:** The planned activity is [ACTIVITY], with environmental conditions of [WEATHER, SURFACE OR SITE CONDITION].
**TASK:** Decide how to adapt, relocate, modify or pause the activity while preserving the learning objective where possible.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify relevant environmental factors; distinguish manageable conditions from conditions requiring cancellation or relocation; consider clothing, surfaces, visibility, equipment and hydration where appropriate; avoid inventing formal thresholds without supplied policy.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide risk considerations, decision points, adapted plan, relocation option, pause or cancellation triggers and communication points.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not provide false certainty about weather safety. School policies, site rules and professional risk assessments take precedence.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [ACTIVITY], [WEATHER], [SITE], [SCHOOL POLICY] or [EQUIPMENT].

## OA-07 - Teach Outdoor Safety and Responsibility
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who teaches safe independent participation outdoors.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are beginning [OUTDOOR ACTIVITY] in [CONTROLLED SITE].
**TASK:** Create a short pupil-facing safety briefing that supports responsible independence.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Cover boundaries, staying with the agreed group, equipment handling, environmental awareness, what to do when unsure, communication with the teacher and stopping or returning when instructed; use language pupils can understand.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide a 2-minute briefing, pupil checklist, teacher confirmation questions and safety reminders.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not invent emergency procedures, first-aid arrangements or supervision ratios. Refer pupils to the teacher's established school procedure where needed.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [SITE] or [SCHOOL PROCEDURES].

## OA-08 - Assess Outdoor Decision-Making
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and formative assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are completing [OUTDOOR TASK] where success depends partly on planning and decisions.
**TASK:** Create an observation framework for assessing decision-making during active participation.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Assess observable planning, route or strategy choice, checking, adaptation, communication and safe decision-making as relevant; provide opportunities for pupils with different physical profiles to demonstrate understanding; avoid equating speed with quality of decision-making.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide assessment criteria, observation method, evidence notes, pupil reflection and next-step actions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria must match the intended construct and allow sufficient opportunity for each pupil to demonstrate it.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [TASK], [ASSESSMENT PURPOSE] or [CLASS SIZE].

## OA-09 - Inclusive Outdoor Adventure Activity
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in inclusive outdoor learning.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil or group faces [ACCESS BARRIER] during [OUTDOOR ACTIVITY], while the core objective is [CORE OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Design adaptations that preserve meaningful participation and learning.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Consider terrain, distance, pace, equipment, communication, sensory access, navigation demands, group roles and recording; preserve pupil agency; identify when an adaptation changes the physical or assessment construct; include relevant safety controls.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide barrier analysis, unchanged objective, adaptations, alternative roles or routes, teacher prompts, safety considerations and assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not remove the core cognitive or physical challenge merely because the route, equipment or response method has changed.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [BARRIER], [SITE] or [CORE OBJECTIVE].

## OA-10 - Design a Pupil-Led Outdoor Challenge
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who develops pupil agency through structured outdoor problem-solving.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will design a short outdoor challenge within [DEFINED AREA] using [AVAILABLE EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Create a pupil-led design cycle from problem identification through testing and evaluation.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Establish non-negotiable safety boundaries first; require pupils to define the challenge objective and success condition; plan, test, observe and revise; include clear site boundaries and equipment controls; require justification based on evidence from testing.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge brief, safety boundaries, design template, testing cycle, success criteria, adaptation, extension and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Pupil agency must operate within teacher-defined safety controls. Do not encourage activities involving unsafe heights, uncontrolled speed, dangerous terrain or unsupported specialist techniques.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CHALLENGE TYPE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [TIME AVAILABLE].
