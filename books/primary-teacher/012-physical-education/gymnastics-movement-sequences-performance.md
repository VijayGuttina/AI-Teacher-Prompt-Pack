# Gymnastics, Movement Sequences & Performance

## GY-01 - Teach a Gymnastics Shape or Action
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in gymnastics.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are learning [GYMNASTIC SHAPE OR ACTION] using [SPACE AND EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Create a progressive lesson that develops safe, controlled performance.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define observable success criteria; use age-appropriate teaching cues; include preparation, demonstration, progressive practice and application; provide regression and extension; specify safety boundaries, spacing and supervision considerations.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide objective, success criteria, teaching points, activity sequence, adaptations, challenges, observation points and safety controls.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not prescribe unsafe progressions or assume advanced equipment. Emphasise control and safe entry and exit where relevant.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SKILL], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [LESSON LENGTH].

## GY-02 - Build a Gymnastics Skill Progression
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary gymnastics teacher and curriculum designer.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are progressing from [STARTING SKILL] toward [INTENDED SKILL OR MOVEMENT OUTCOME].
**TASK:** Build a staged progression that develops confidence, control and technical understanding.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Change one or two demands at a time; establish readiness indicators before progression; include practice under stable conditions before adding complexity where appropriate; provide alternatives for pupils progressing at different rates.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide 4 to 6 stages with purpose, task, teaching focus, readiness indicator, adaptation and extension.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not assume every pupil should attempt the same final movement. Progression must be based on observable readiness, not pressure or comparison.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [STARTING SKILL], [INTENDED OUTCOME] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GY-03 - Compose a Movement Sequence
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in gymnastics composition.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will create an individual or group sequence using [MOVEMENT REQUIREMENTS].
**TASK:** Design a composition process that combines technical requirements with creative choice.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include planning, selection, linking, rehearsal and refinement; define non-negotiable requirements clearly; preserve multiple valid sequences; include transitions and use of space where relevant.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide composition brief, planning prompts, creation stages, rehearsal routine, success criteria, adaptation and extension.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Assess purposeful sequencing rather than similarity to a teacher-created routine.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SOLO/GROUP], [REQUIREMENTS], [SPACE] or [TIME].

## GY-04 - Teach Linking and Transitions
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary gymnastics teacher.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils can perform individual [SHAPES OR ACTIONS] but struggle to link them smoothly.
**TASK:** Create a practice sequence that develops purposeful transitions.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Focus on preparation, weight transfer, spatial awareness, timing and control where relevant; provide contrasting transition options; allow pupils to select effective links; include observation and refinement.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning focus, teaching cues, progressive practices, linking challenge, adaptations, observation criteria and reflection prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not imply that speed alone creates a successful transition. Prioritise control and continuity.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [MOVEMENTS] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GY-05 - Diagnose a Gymnastics Performance Difficulty
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who uses observation to identify teachable movement priorities.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil or group demonstrates [OBSERVABLE DIFFICULTY] during [GYMNASTICS TASK].
**TASK:** Analyse the observed evidence and propose a safe next teaching step.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observation from inference; identify plausible contributing factors; avoid medical or developmental diagnosis; suggest a simpler diagnostic task; prioritise one intervention and specify what evidence would indicate improvement.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide observation summary, plausible explanations, diagnostic task, priority response, practice activity and reassessment indicators.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not infer hidden causes as facts. The intervention must be safe and achievable within the stated context.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [TASK], [OBSERVATIONS] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GY-06 - Plan Apparatus-Based Learning
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher experienced in safe apparatus organisation.
**CONTEXT:** A class of [CLASS SIZE] pupils will use [APPARATUS] to practise [LEARNING FOCUS] in [SPACE].
**TASK:** Create a safe and active learning plan.
**REQUIREMENTS:** State setup and movement routes; minimise queues; define access and waiting expectations; include safe mounting, dismounting or use where relevant; provide station roles and teacher supervision priorities; include adaptation and progression.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide apparatus plan, setup sequence, pupil flow, station tasks, safety checks, supervision priorities, adaptations and progressions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not invent apparatus specifications or safety rules that depend on unknown equipment. Flag any requirement that must follow school or equipment-provider procedures.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CLASS SIZE], [APPARATUS], [SPACE] or [LEARNING FOCUS].

## GY-07 - Peer Observation and Feedback in Gymnastics
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who teaches constructive peer observation.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will observe [GYMNASTICS TASK] and provide feedback against [SUCCESS CRITERIA].
**TASK:** Create a concise peer-feedback process.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Limit attention to a small number of observable criteria; provide descriptive language and one actionable suggestion; include a performer response stage; avoid personal comments about bodies or inherent ability.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide criteria, observation prompts, feedback stems, recording method, response routine and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Feedback must be linked to observable performance evidence rather than general praise or preference.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [TASK] or [SUCCESS CRITERIA].

## GY-08 - Assess a Gymnastics Sequence
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and authentic assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to assess [YEAR GROUP] pupils' performance of [GYMNASTICS OUTCOME].
**TASK:** Create an assessment approach that captures control, purposeful composition and understanding where relevant.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the assessment construct; use observable criteria; distinguish movement execution from sequence design; allow different valid responses; include self-reflection and a next-step action.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide assessment task, evidence requirements, observation rubric, self-assessment and feedback prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not reward confidence, flexibility, body type or similarity to a model unless explicitly part of the intended learning.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [OUTCOME], [ASSESSMENT TIME] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GY-09 - Adapt Gymnastics for Inclusion
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in inclusive gymnastics.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil or group faces an access barrier during [GYMNASTICS ACTIVITY], while the core objective is [CORE OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Create adaptations that preserve the intended learning wherever possible.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Analyse barriers involving movement range, balance, communication, sensory demands, confidence, equipment or environment; provide alternative participation routes; preserve meaningful challenge; identify where an adaptation changes the assessment construct; maintain safety.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide barrier analysis, unchanged objective, adaptations, participation routes, teacher prompts, safety considerations and assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not assume that floor or apparatus participation must look identical for learning to be equivalent.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [CORE OBJECTIVE] or [ACCESS BARRIER].

## GY-10 - Create a Gymnastics Performance Challenge
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs inclusive performance challenges.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] will create and perform a short sequence meeting [GIVEN REQUIREMENTS].
**TASK:** Create a challenge with clear constraints and multiple valid solutions.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the required elements and purpose; include planning, practice, performance and reflection; provide adaptation and extension; establish respectful audience behaviour and safe use of space and apparatus.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge brief, requirements, process stages, success criteria, adaptation, extension, safety controls and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Success must be based on evidence of the intended learning, not costume, prior gymnastics experience or conformity to one ideal sequence.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [REQUIREMENTS], [GROUP SIZE], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].
