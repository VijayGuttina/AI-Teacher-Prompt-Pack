# Games, Tactics & Decision-Making

## GA-01 - Teach a Simple Game Principle
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in games-based learning and tactical understanding.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are learning the game principle [GAME PRINCIPLE] within [ACTIVITY OR SPORT].
**TASK:** Create a progressive lesson that develops understanding through play, guided questioning and practical application.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the principle in pupil-friendly language; use a modified game or problem first where appropriate; include concise questions that focus attention on decisions; progress task constraints gradually; avoid over-coaching technique when the objective is tactical understanding.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning objective, success criteria, entry game, teaching questions, progression, adaptations, challenges, assessment indicators and safety considerations.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The activity must create genuine decision-making opportunities rather than rehearsing a predetermined movement pattern.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GAME PRINCIPLE], [ACTIVITY], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [CLASS SIZE].

## GA-02 - Design a Modified Game
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs small-sided and modified games.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher wants pupils to practise [SKILL OR TACTICAL PRINCIPLE] using [AVAILABLE SPACE AND EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Design a modified game that increases meaningful involvement and reinforces the intended learning.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Specify players, space, equipment, rules and scoring; explain how each constraint supports the learning objective; minimise long waiting times and elimination; provide adaptation and progression options; include safe restart and transition procedures.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide game purpose, setup, rules, scoring, learning link, adaptations, progressions, teacher questions and safety controls.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Every major rule should have a learning purpose. Avoid adding complexity that does not improve the targeted skill or decision.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SKILL], [TACTICAL PRINCIPLE], [EQUIPMENT] or [SPACE].

## GA-03 - Create an Attacking Decision-Making Activity
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in invasion-game pedagogy.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to improve decisions when attacking in [GAME OR ACTIVITY].
**TASK:** Create an activity that develops recognition of space, support, timing and appropriate options.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include a realistic problem; provide multiple possible solutions; manipulate numbers, space, rules or targets gradually; use questions to help pupils notice cues; avoid prescribing one correct decision in every situation.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide tactical problem, setup, rules, key cues, teacher questions, progressions, adaptations and observation criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Success must depend on responding to changing information, not simply completing a rehearsed sequence.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GAME], [EQUIPMENT], [SPACE] or [GROUP SIZE].

## GA-04 - Create a Defending Decision-Making Activity
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in teaching defensive decision-making.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are learning [DEFENSIVE PRINCIPLE] within [GAME OR ACTIVITY].
**TASK:** Design a progressive activity that helps pupils recognise and respond to attacking threats safely and fairly.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define observable defensive behaviours; create manageable attacker-defender situations; progress complexity through space, numbers or options; include clear boundaries on contact and safe challenge; provide reflection questions.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning objective, setup, rules, teaching focus, progressions, adaptations, assessment indicators and safety or fair-play expectations.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not encourage unsafe contact or reward reckless challenge. Tactical success should be linked to reading the situation.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GAME], [DEFENSIVE PRINCIPLE], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GA-05 - Diagnose a Tactical Problem
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who analyses game play using observable evidence.
**CONTEXT:** During [GAME OR ACTIVITY], pupils repeatedly experience [OBSERVED TACTICAL PROBLEM].
**TASK:** Identify plausible causes and design a short diagnostic and teaching response.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observed behaviour from explanation; identify multiple plausible causes; suggest an observation or modified game to distinguish them; prioritise the highest-value teaching response; include a reassessment condition.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide evidence summary, plausible causes, diagnostic activity, priority intervention, teacher questions and reassessment indicators.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not attribute a tactical problem automatically to poor technique when positioning, perception, communication or task design may also contribute.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GAME], [OBSERVATIONS] or [AVAILABLE EQUIPMENT].

## GA-06 - Teach Support and Communication
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in collaborative game play.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to improve [SUPPORT, COMMUNICATION OR TEAM COORDINATION] during [GAME].
**TASK:** Create a practical activity that develops useful off-the-ball behaviour and communication.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define observable behaviours; ensure communication has a clear game purpose; include movement and decision-making; provide alternatives for pupils who communicate differently; avoid requiring constant shouting as evidence of teamwork.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide objective, activity setup, rules, communication prompts, adaptations, progression, observation checklist and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Assess the usefulness and timing of communication, not simply its volume or frequency.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GAME], [CLASS PROFILE] or [SPACE].

## GA-07 - Progress From Skill Practice to Game Application
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who connects isolated practice with meaningful game play.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have practised [TECHNICAL SKILL] but struggle to use it effectively in [GAME CONTEXT].
**TASK:** Create a bridge from controlled practice to increasingly authentic decision-making.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve the skill focus while gradually introducing perception, timing, movement, opposition or choice; identify when pupils are ready to progress; provide regression options; include assessment of transfer rather than repetition alone.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide progression stages, activity purpose, conditions, teaching cues, progression triggers, adaptations and transfer assessment.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not assume technical competence automatically transfers to game performance. Each stage must add a meaningful contextual demand.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [SKILL], [GAME], [YEAR GROUP], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GA-08 - Create a Game Observation Tool
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and formative assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to observe [TACTICAL OR GAME BEHAVIOUR] during active play.
**TASK:** Create a concise observation tool that can be used without repeatedly stopping the game.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Select no more than 5 observable behaviours; define each clearly; distinguish evidence from interpretation; include a simple recording method and next-step action; account for pupils who may have fewer opportunities to demonstrate the behaviour.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide observation criteria, recording grid, observation guidance, opportunity notes and follow-up teaching actions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not equate low frequency of an action with poor understanding unless the pupil had sufficient opportunity to demonstrate it.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [GAME], [YEAR GROUP], [BEHAVIOUR] or [NUMBER OF PUPILS].

## GA-09 - Inclusive Competitive Game
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs inclusive competition and meaningful challenge.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] will play a competitive [GAME OR ACTIVITY] with a wide range of prior experience.
**TASK:** Create a competitive format that preserves challenge while supporting participation and learning.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the competitive element; avoid elimination that removes pupils from activity for long periods; provide multiple roles or entry points where appropriate; include adaptation and progression; establish fair-play expectations; ensure success is not determined solely by pre-existing physical advantage.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide game format, setup, rules, scoring, inclusion adaptations, extension, fair-play prompts and safety controls.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Competition should reinforce the learning objective rather than replacing it with a sole focus on winning.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GAME], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## GA-10 - Pupil-Led Game Adaptation
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who develops tactical understanding through pupil-designed rule changes.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have played [BASE GAME] and will adapt it to emphasise [LEARNING FOCUS].
**TASK:** Create a structured pupil-led design activity.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Establish non-negotiable safety boundaries; ask pupils to identify the current game problem; generate and test rule or space changes; predict likely effects; compare outcomes; justify the final adaptation using evidence from play.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge brief, design questions, adaptation template, testing cycle, evidence prompts, safety boundaries, reflection questions and assessment criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Preserve genuine pupil agency while preventing rule changes that create unsafe conditions or remove the intended learning problem.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [BASE GAME], [LEARNING FOCUS], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].
