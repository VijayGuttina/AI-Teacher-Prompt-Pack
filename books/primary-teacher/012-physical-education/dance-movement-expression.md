# Dance, Movement & Expression

## DA-01 - Teach a Dance Motif
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in dance and creative movement.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are developing a short movement motif based on [THEME OR STIMULUS].
**TASK:** Create a progressive lesson that develops purposeful movement and expression.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the movement focus; introduce a clear stimulus; model possibilities without prescribing a single sequence; develop actions, space, dynamics and relationships as appropriate; include rehearsal, refinement and reflection.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide objective, success criteria, entry activity, teaching sequence, teacher prompts, adaptation, challenge, assessment indicators and safety considerations.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Preserve creative choice and avoid assessing conformity to the teacher's preferred choreography.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [THEME], [LESSON LENGTH], [SPACE] or [MUSIC/STIMULUS].

## DA-02 - Create a Movement Exploration
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary dance teacher.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will explore [MOVEMENT CONCEPT] through [THEME OR STIMULUS].
**TASK:** Design a structured exploration with multiple valid responses.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Provide progressive prompts involving body actions, space, direction, level, pathway, timing or dynamics where relevant; establish safe boundaries; allow individual and collaborative exploration; include observation and reflection.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide stimulus, exploration prompts, teacher questions, progression, adaptation, challenge and reflection.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Prompts must generate purposeful exploration rather than vague instruction to simply be creative.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CONCEPT], [SPACE] or [TIME].

## DA-03 - Develop Actions, Space and Dynamics
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in movement composition.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to develop control over [ACTIONS, SPACE OR DYNAMICS] within a dance sequence.
**TASK:** Create a practical progression that improves purposeful movement choices.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define observable movement qualities; isolate and combine elements gradually; use concise cues; include opportunities to compare contrasting choices; connect movement decisions to expressive intent.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning focus, teaching points, progressive tasks, comparison prompts, adaptations, challenges and observation criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not treat one expressive interpretation as objectively correct when the success criterion is purposeful communication.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [THEME], [MOVEMENT ELEMENT] or [SPACE].

## DA-04 - Build a Group Dance Sequence
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher experienced in collaborative dance composition.
**CONTEXT:** Groups of [GROUP SIZE] pupils will create a dance sequence responding to [STIMULUS].
**TASK:** Design a structured composition process that ensures meaningful participation.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include idea generation, movement selection, transitions, relationships, rehearsal and refinement; prevent one pupil from controlling all decisions; include accessible contribution routes; maintain individual accountability.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide composition stages, group roles or routines, planning template, rehearsal cycle, teacher observation points and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Group work must develop collaborative composition rather than simply dividing a fixed routine into parts.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GROUP SIZE], [STIMULUS] or [LESSON LENGTH].

## DA-05 - Use a Stimulus to Generate Movement
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary dance teacher who uses accessible stimuli to develop movement ideas.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will respond physically to [IMAGE, STORY, MUSIC, OBJECT, WORD OR THEME].
**TASK:** Create a teaching sequence that moves from interpretation to composed movement.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Ask pupils to identify qualities or ideas within the stimulus; generate multiple movement responses; select and refine ideas; explain how movement choices connect to the stimulus; avoid requiring a single literal interpretation.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide stimulus introduction, noticing prompts, movement tasks, selection process, refinement prompts, adaptation and assessment criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The stimulus must support the learning objective rather than functioning as decorative inspiration.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [STIMULUS], [SPACE] or [TIME].

## DA-06 - Rehearse and Refine a Performance
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in constructive rehearsal and refinement.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have created a [DANCE OR MOVEMENT SEQUENCE] and need to improve it before sharing.
**TASK:** Design a rehearsal process that uses evidence and feedback.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify one or two improvement priorities at a time; include observation or recording where available; distinguish descriptive feedback from judgement; provide a repeatable test-refine-rehearse cycle; preserve ownership of pupils' creative decisions.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide rehearsal routine, observation prompts, feedback framework, refinement cycle, adaptation and final self-assessment.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not replace pupil choreography with teacher choreography as the default response to an identified weakness.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [PERFORMANCE LENGTH], [GROUP SIZE] or [AVAILABLE RESOURCES].

## DA-07 - Give and Use Peer Feedback in Dance
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who teaches pupils to give constructive movement feedback.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will observe each other's [DANCE OR MOVEMENT SEQUENCES].
**TASK:** Create a structured peer-feedback activity.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Focus feedback on agreed observable or purposeful criteria; teach pupils how to describe evidence; require one actionable suggestion; provide respectful language; allow performers to decide which feedback to adopt.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide success criteria, observation prompts, feedback sentence stems, recording method, response routine and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Feedback must avoid personal comments about bodies, appearance or inherent ability.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SUCCESS CRITERIA] or [GROUP SIZE].

## DA-08 - Assess Dance and Movement Composition
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and authentic assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to assess pupils' understanding of [DANCE OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Create an assessment approach that evaluates both movement and purposeful decision-making.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the construct being assessed; use observable criteria; distinguish technical control from expressive or compositional choices; allow appropriate variation in valid performances; include pupil reflection.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide assessment task, evidence requirements, observation rubric, pupil self-assessment and feedback prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not reward similarity to a teacher model when originality or interpretation is part of the objective.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [OBJECTIVE], [SOLO/GROUP] or [ASSESSMENT TIME].

## DA-09 - Adapt Dance for Inclusion
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in inclusive movement education.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil or group needs support to access [DANCE ACTIVITY] while preserving the core learning objective [CORE OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Create adaptations that remove access barriers without unnecessarily reducing creative or physical thinking.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify likely barriers; consider space, timing, communication, sensory demands, movement range, equipment and response format; preserve choice; provide teacher prompts; identify where an adaptation changes the assessment construct.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide barrier analysis, adaptations, unchanged objective, participation routes, teacher prompts, safety considerations and assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not assume a particular movement range, body type or mode of participation is required unless it is genuinely central to the objective.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [CORE OBJECTIVE] or [ACCESS BARRIER].

## DA-10 - Create a Dance Performance Challenge
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs meaningful creative performance challenges.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] will create and share a short performance based on [THEME OR BRIEF].
**TASK:** Create a challenge with clear constraints and meaningful creative freedom.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the purpose and audience; include a small number of non-negotiable requirements; preserve multiple valid solutions; include planning, rehearsal, sharing and reflection; establish respectful audience behaviour and safe use of space.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge brief, constraints, process stages, success criteria, adaptation, extension, audience expectations and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Success should depend on purposeful movement choices and evidence of learning, not costume, confidence alone or prior dance experience.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [THEME], [GROUP SIZE], [SPACE] or [PERFORMANCE LENGTH].
