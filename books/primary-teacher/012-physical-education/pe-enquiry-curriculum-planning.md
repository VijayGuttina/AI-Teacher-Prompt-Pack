# PE Enquiry & Curriculum Planning

## PE-01 - Plan a PE Unit
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Physical Education teacher and curriculum planner.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to plan a [NUMBER]-lesson unit for [YEAR GROUP] focused on [ACTIVITY OR SKILL].
**TASK:** Create a coherent unit sequence that develops pupils' physical, tactical, compositional or evaluative learning as appropriate.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify prior learning, progression, lesson objectives, practical activities, success criteria, assessment checkpoints, equipment, space and safety considerations; maximise meaningful movement time; avoid unnecessary written work.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide unit overview, lesson-by-lesson sequence, progression rationale, equipment list, assessment checkpoints and safety considerations.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The sequence must be age appropriate, physically active, progressive, feasible and technically accurate.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT], [LESSON LENGTH] or [CURRICULUM REQUIREMENTS].

## PE-02 - Sequence Skills Across Lessons
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in progression.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to develop [SKILL] from their current level towards [INTENDED OUTCOME].
**TASK:** Design a progression sequence across [NUMBER] lessons.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Start from stated prior learning; increase complexity through control, accuracy, decision-making, combinations or application as appropriate; identify when to consolidate before progressing; avoid assuming identical starting points.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide progression ladder, lesson focus, practice activities, observable success criteria and teacher decision points.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Progression must be meaningful rather than simply making activities faster or harder.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [STARTING POINT] or [LESSON COUNT].

## PE-03 - Plan a PE Lesson
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher responsible for safe, active and inclusive lessons.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs a [LESSON LENGTH]-minute lesson for [YEAR GROUP] on [TOPIC].
**TASK:** Produce a complete practical lesson plan.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include learning intention, equipment, space setup, teacher modelling, warm-up or preparation where appropriate, progressive activities, practice-to-application transition, formative assessment, differentiation, safety and closure; prioritise movement time.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide timed lesson sequence, setup diagram description, teacher cues, pupil activities, safety controls, assessment checkpoints and contingency options.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Realistic timing, active participation, clear progression, safe organisation and age-appropriate challenge.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT], [ABILITY RANGE] or [SCHOOL PROCEDURES].

## PE-04 - Build a Skill-Focused Practice Session
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and motor-skill coach.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need focused practice on [SKILL] before applying it in a larger activity.
**TASK:** Create a practice session that develops the stated skill.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include demonstration cues, manageable repetitions, appropriate challenge, observation points, feedback prompts and progression or regression options; avoid excessive queues and inactive waiting.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide setup, activity sequence, teacher cues, success criteria, feedback prompts and progression options.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Practice must target the intended skill and provide substantial opportunities for pupils to move.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SKILL], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [LESSON LENGTH].

## PE-05 - Plan Assessment for a PE Unit
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to assess progress in [PE UNIT] for [YEAR GROUP].
**TASK:** Create an assessment approach using observable evidence.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the intended construct; identify observable movement, knowledge, decision-making or composition evidence as relevant; include baseline, formative and end-point checks; avoid unnecessary ranking of pupils.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide assessment criteria, baseline task, formative checkpoints, final assessment task, recording grid and feedback prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Assessment must be valid for the stated objective and feasible during practical activity.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify [ACTIVITY], [YEAR GROUP], [ASSESSMENT OBJECTIVE] or [AVAILABLE TIME].

## PE-06 - Plan for Limited Space or Equipment
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher skilled in resource-efficient lesson design.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher must deliver [ACTIVITY] with [SPACE/EQUIPMENT LIMITATIONS].
**TASK:** Redesign the lesson so pupils remain active and the core learning is preserved.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Reduce unnecessary waiting; use stations, pairs, rotations or modified rules where appropriate; state which adaptations preserve the objective and identify any changes to the learning demand.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Revised setup, activity rotations, equipment allocation, movement-flow plan, safety controls and contingency options.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The lesson must remain safe, active, realistic and aligned to the intended learning.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] and [CORE OBJECTIVE].

## PE-07 - Plan a Cross-Curricular PE Activity
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary teacher integrating PE with another curriculum area.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher wants to connect [PE OBJECTIVE] with [OTHER SUBJECT].
**TASK:** Create an activity where the PE learning remains substantive rather than becoming incidental.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify the distinct PE learning; integrate the other subject without substantially reducing movement time; include practical organisation, assessment and safety.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning objectives, activity sequence, subject links, assessment evidence and teacher preparation.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** PE must remain a genuine learning objective and the activity must be feasible in the stated setting.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [PE TOPIC], [OTHER SUBJECT] or [AVAILABLE SPACE].

## PE-08 - Plan a Pupil-Led PE Session
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher developing pupil agency and leadership.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need opportunities to lead parts of a [ACTIVITY] session safely.
**TASK:** Design a structured pupil-leadership lesson.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define safe leadership responsibilities; provide planning prompts, demonstration expectations, observation roles and teacher oversight; retain individual participation and accountability.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide lesson structure, pupil leader roles, briefing cards, safety controls, teacher checkpoints and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Leadership must be meaningful but must not transfer safety-critical responsibility away from the teacher.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [GROUP SIZE] or [LESSON LENGTH].

## PE-09 - Plan PE for Mixed Attainment
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in responsive differentiation.
**CONTEXT:** A mixed-attainment class is learning [SKILL OR ACTIVITY].
**TASK:** Create a lesson structure with common learning intentions and appropriate levels of support and challenge.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Vary space, equipment, rules, task complexity, practice conditions or decision-making where appropriate; avoid fixed labels; provide routes for pupils to progress between challenge levels.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Shared objective, challenge pathways, teacher prompts, grouping approach, assessment checkpoints and progression rules.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Differentiation must increase access and challenge without reducing meaningful participation.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [CLASS PROFILE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## PE-10 - Plan a Complete PE Scheme of Work
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE curriculum leader.
**CONTEXT:** The school needs a [TERM/LENGTH]-long PE scheme for [YEAR GROUP] covering [ACTIVITIES OR CURRICULUM CONTENT].
**TASK:** Create a coherent scheme showing progression across physical skills, application, knowledge and evaluation.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Sequence units deliberately; identify prerequisites; balance individual and group activities; include assessment, inclusion, equipment, space and safety considerations; avoid overclaiming progression where the supplied curriculum information is incomplete.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide term overview, unit sequence, progression map, assessment plan, resource overview, safety considerations and curriculum coverage checklist.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherent, age appropriate, active, inclusive, feasible and aligned with the supplied curriculum requirements.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide [TERM], [YEAR GROUP], [CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK], [FACILITIES], [EQUIPMENT] or [EXISTING UNITS].
