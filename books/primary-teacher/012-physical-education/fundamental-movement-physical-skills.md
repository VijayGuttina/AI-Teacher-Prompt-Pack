# Fundamental Movement & Physical Skills

## FM-01 - Teach a Fundamental Movement Skill
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Physical Education teacher specialising in the progressive teaching of fundamental movement skills.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are learning [MOVEMENT SKILL] within [ACTIVITY CONTEXT]. Their prior experience is [PRIOR KNOWLEDGE OR EXPERIENCE].
**TASK:** Create a practical lesson sequence that develops the movement skill safely and progressively.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the observable learning outcome; identify 3 to 5 age-appropriate teaching points; begin with an accessible entry activity; progress through demonstration, guided practice and increasingly independent application; provide adaptation and challenge; include appropriate safety controls and teacher observation points.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning objective, success criteria, key teaching points, warm-up or entry task, teaching sequence, adaptations, challenges, assessment checkpoints, safety considerations and cooldown or reflection.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Keep cues concise and observable. Do not overload pupils with technical detail or assume equipment or facilities not specified.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [MOVEMENT SKILL], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [LESSON LENGTH].

## FM-02 - Diagnose a Movement Difficulty
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher skilled in observing movement and identifying teachable next steps.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil or group is struggling with [MOVEMENT SKILL]. The teacher has observed [OBSERVABLE BEHAVIOURS].
**TASK:** Analyse the evidence and identify likely barriers and the most useful next teaching step.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observation from inference; avoid diagnosing medical or developmental conditions; identify possible causes only where supported by the evidence; suggest a simple check to distinguish between plausible explanations; recommend one priority intervention and one reassessment activity.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide observation summary, possible barriers, confidence level, diagnostic check, priority teaching response, practice task and reassessment indicator.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not infer hidden causes as facts. Keep the response focused on observable movement and instruction.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SKILL], [OBSERVATIONS] or [AVAILABLE EQUIPMENT].

## FM-03 - Create a Skill Progression
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE curriculum designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs a progression for [MOVEMENT SKILL] from [STARTING POINT] to [INTENDED OUTCOME] for [YEAR GROUP OR RANGE].
**TASK:** Build a clear sequence of progressive practice stages.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Change one or two demands at a time; move from simple and stable conditions toward greater complexity where appropriate; include entry criteria, observable success indicators and common errors; avoid treating all pupils as progressing at the same rate.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide 4 to 6 stages with purpose, activity, teaching focus, success indicator, adaptation and progression trigger.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Each stage must prepare pupils for the next. Do not increase speed, opposition, space and technical complexity simultaneously without justification.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [SKILL], [YEAR GROUP], [NUMBER OF LESSONS] or [EQUIPMENT].

## FM-04 - Design a Movement Circuit
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs active, safe and purposeful circuits.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] need practice across [SKILLS] using [AVAILABLE SPACE AND EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Create a movement circuit that maximises active learning time.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include 4 to 8 stations; state the skill focus and purpose of each station; plan safe movement and transitions; provide clear task cards; include easier and harder versions; avoid queues and unnecessary elimination.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide circuit overview, station table, equipment list, setup instructions, timings, rotation method, differentiation and teacher observation focus.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The circuit must be physically feasible for the stated class size and space and must not rely on unspecified equipment.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [SKILLS] or [EQUIPMENT].

## FM-05 - Develop Balance and Stability
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in fundamental movement and body control.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are developing [STATIC OR DYNAMIC] balance within [ACTIVITY].
**TASK:** Create a progressive practice sequence.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define observable balance behaviours; manipulate stable and unstable conditions carefully; include base of support, body control and visual focus where relevant; provide safe challenges; allow pupils to self-assess using simple criteria.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning focus, teaching points, progressive activities, adaptations, challenges, peer or self-assessment prompts and safety controls.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not present a single body position as the only correct solution when multiple stable strategies are appropriate.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ACTIVITY], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## FM-06 - Develop Locomotion and Direction Change
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in movement efficiency and spatial awareness.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to improve [RUNNING, HOPPING, SKIPPING, JUMPING OR DIRECTION CHANGE] in [ACTIVITY CONTEXT].
**TASK:** Design an active learning sequence.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Focus on observable movement qualities; include acceleration or deceleration where relevant; develop awareness of space and other pupils; progress complexity gradually; provide concise feedback cues and opportunities to apply the skill in a meaningful task.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide objective, teaching cues, progressive practices, game or application activity, adaptations, challenges and assessment indicators.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Maintain adequate spacing and safe stopping procedures. Avoid excessive technical terminology for the age group.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SKILL], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE] or [EQUIPMENT].

## FM-07 - Develop Object Control
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher specialising in throwing, catching, striking, kicking and receiving.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils in [YEAR GROUP] are developing [OBJECT CONTROL SKILL] using [EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Create a progressive skill-development session.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Match equipment to pupil size and ability; begin with high-success practice; vary distance, target, speed, trajectory, movement or opposition progressively; include partner and independent practice; provide safety rules for moving objects and shared space.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide learning objective, equipment, teaching points, practice progression, adaptations, challenges, application game and assessment checkpoints.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not increase multiple task demands at once without a clear reason. Ensure the activity avoids unsafe throwing or striking directions.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [SKILL], [YEAR GROUP], [EQUIPMENT], [SPACE] or [GROUP SIZE].

## FM-08 - Warm-Up With a Purpose
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who uses preparation activities to support the lesson learning.
**CONTEXT:** The main lesson focus is [MOVEMENT OR ACTIVITY] for [YEAR GROUP].
**TASK:** Design a purposeful warm-up that prepares pupils for the specific demands of the lesson.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include progressive movement, spatial awareness and rehearsal of relevant movement patterns; keep instructions simple; ensure all pupils remain involved; provide a transition into the main learning; avoid unsupported health or injury-prevention claims.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide purpose, setup, instructions, progression, adaptation, safety checks and explicit link to the main activity.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The warm-up must prepare pupils for the lesson rather than functioning as unrelated exercise.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [MAIN ACTIVITY], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [TIME AVAILABLE].

## FM-09 - Movement Observation Checklist
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher and formative assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to observe [MOVEMENT SKILL] during normal participation without stopping the lesson repeatedly.
**TASK:** Create a concise observation checklist.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Assess no more than 5 essential observable behaviours; use neutral language; distinguish secure, developing and not yet observed where appropriate; include space for a next-step note; avoid scoring irrelevant characteristics such as body type or speed unless they are part of the objective.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide checklist, observation guidance, common patterns to notice and follow-up teaching actions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Every criterion must relate directly to the stated learning objective and be realistically observable in a live lesson.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [SKILL], [YEAR GROUP], [ASSESSMENT PURPOSE] or [NUMBER OF PUPILS].

## FM-10 - Fundamental Movement Skill Challenge
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary PE teacher who designs inclusive, motivating physical challenges.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils will apply [MOVEMENT SKILLS] in a short challenge using [SPACE AND EQUIPMENT].
**TASK:** Create an inclusive challenge with clear success conditions and multiple entry points.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the challenge purpose; include a measurable or observable success condition; allow repeated attempts and improvement; avoid public ranking as the sole measure of success; provide adaptation and extension; include reflection on strategy, effort and skill development.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide challenge brief, setup, rules, success criteria, adaptation options, extension, safety controls and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Success must not depend solely on pre-existing physical ability. The challenge should reward learning, improvement or effective decision-making.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [SKILLS], [CLASS SIZE], [SPACE], [EQUIPMENT] or [TIME AVAILABLE].
