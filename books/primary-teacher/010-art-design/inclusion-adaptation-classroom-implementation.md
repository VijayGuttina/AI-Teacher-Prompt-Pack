# Inclusion, Adaptation & Classroom Implementation Workflows

**IDs:** IA-01 to IA-10

## IA-01 — Adapt an Art Task for SEND
**ROLE:** Inclusive primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner profile supplied. **TASK:** Identify access barriers and design targeted adaptations. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve artistic intent and meaningful choice where possible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, scaffold, independence route, safety check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Fine-motor/sensory/cognitive needs.

## IA-02 — Adapt Art Language for EAL
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task, vocabulary and language need supplied. **TASK:** Make instructions and technical vocabulary accessible without reducing conceptual demand. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use visuals, examples and sentence frames where useful. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Simplified input, vocabulary support, prompts, independent route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Meaning preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Language proficiency.

## IA-03 — Plan Accessible Practical Setup
**ROLE:** Art classroom teacher. **CONTEXT:** Activity, room and learner needs supplied. **TASK:** Plan layout, resources, tools and movement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe access and realistic classroom routines. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Setup plan, resource stations, movement rules, contingency. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Class size.

## IA-04 — Preserve Artistic Choice During Scaffolding
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and identified barrier supplied. **TASK:** Design support that reduces access difficulty without prescribing the finished artwork. **REQUIREMENTS:** State what support fades and what decisions remain with the pupil. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Scaffold, choices, fade-out, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## IA-05 — Adapt a Demonstration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Technique and learner needs supplied. **TASK:** Redesign teacher modelling for visual, verbal and practical access. **REQUIREMENTS:** Keep key technical decisions visible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration steps, visual cues, checks for understanding, alternative access. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technique accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## IA-06 — Manage Materials and Classroom Routines
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Activity, materials and class size supplied. **TASK:** Create efficient routines for distribution, use, storage and cleanup. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safety-critical controls. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before/during/after routine. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Limited resources.

## IA-07 — Build Independence in Art Making
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Learner currently reliant on adult support. **TASK:** Design a gradual release plan. **REQUIREMENTS:** Replace unnecessary prompting with tools, checklists or decision cues where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Current support, next step, fade plan, independence check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual.

## IA-08 — Plan Challenge Without Simply Adding Difficulty
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Secure learner/task supplied. **TASK:** Increase artistic depth through choices, refinement, experimentation or justification. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not equate challenge with extra workload alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Core task, depth challenge, evidence of higher-level thinking. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Artistic construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## IA-09 — Troubleshoot an Art Lesson
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Planned lesson and observed problem supplied. **TASK:** Identify practical, pedagogical or resource causes and propose proportionate fixes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observed problems from hypotheses. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, likely causes, quick fix, longer-term adjustment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Teacher reflection.

## IA-10 — Review Inclusion Across an Art Unit
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Unit sequence and learner needs supplied. **TASK:** Audit access, participation, agency, assessment and progression. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish genuine barriers from assumptions about learners. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strengths, barriers, adaptations, risks, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct and participation preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Whole unit/class.
