# Textiles, Collage & Mixed Media Workflows

**IDs:** TM-01 to TM-10

## TM-01 — Explore Collage Composition
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme, materials and year group supplied. **TASK:** Design an exploration of layering, overlap, scale and composition. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve artistic choice. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Exploration, prompts, making task, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear visual purpose. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## TM-02 — Teach Textile Techniques
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Textile material and technique supplied. **TASK:** Plan practical instruction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include appropriate tool/material safety. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, guided practice, independent application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technique accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Stitching/weaving.

## TM-03 — Investigate Pattern and Repetition
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Medium and objective supplied. **TASK:** Explore repeated visual or textile motifs. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include variation and deliberate choices, not mechanical copying only. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Investigation and making sequence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Pattern vocabulary. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Print/textile.

## TM-04 — Combine Materials Purposefully
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials and intended effect supplied. **TASK:** Plan a mixed-media investigation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Explain why combinations may affect texture, structure or visual effect. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiment table and application task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Material plausibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Recycled/found materials.

## TM-05 — Develop Layering and Surface
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Surface objective supplied. **TASK:** Explore layering, concealment, reveal and surface contrast. **REQUIREMENTS:** Encourage testing and reflection. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Practical sequence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Artistic decision-making. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Paper/fabric.

## TM-06 — Diagnose Mixed-Media Difficulties
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil work/process supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely material, technique or composition barriers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate visible evidence from inference about ability or intention. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, discriminating check, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## TM-07 — Give Feedback on Mixed-Media Work
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Work, objective and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Give specific process and artistic feedback. **REQUIREMENTS:** Respect legitimate stylistic choices. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, next decision, question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## TM-08 — Adapt Textile and Collage Activities
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original activity and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve physical, sensory or cognitive access. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve meaningful choice and making demand. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, fade-out, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved and safe. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## TM-09 — Plan a Collaborative Mixed-Media Piece
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Class size, materials and outcome supplied. **TASK:** Structure collaborative making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define individual responsibility, shared decisions and reflection. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Roles, process, checkpoints, evaluation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible classroom management. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Whole class/group.

## TM-10 — Retrieval and Revisit Textile Skills
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prior technique and new making challenge supplied. **TASK:** Connect recalled technique knowledge to a fresh material decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include practical application and reflection. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, demonstration, application, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 15/30 minutes.
