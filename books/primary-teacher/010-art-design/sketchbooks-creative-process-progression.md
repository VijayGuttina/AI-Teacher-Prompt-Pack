# Sketchbooks, Creative Process & Progression Workflows

**IDs:** SK-01 to SK-10

## SK-01 — Structure a Sketchbook Investigation
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Unit objective and medium supplied. **TASK:** Plan a sequence of experiments, observations, ideas and reflections. **REQUIREMENTS:** Treat the sketchbook as evidence of process, not only presentation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Page sequence, prompts, teacher modelling, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Purposeful progression. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SK-02 — Teach Idea Generation
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme and intended outcome supplied. **TASK:** Create divergent idea-generation activities. **REQUIREMENTS:** Allow multiple valid directions before selection. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Stimulus, idea prompts, selection criteria, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Genuine choice. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## SK-03 — Plan Experimentation and Iteration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Technique/material supplied. **TASK:** Structure test → observe → modify → retest cycles. **REQUIREMENTS:** Make revision purposeful rather than cosmetic. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiment grid and reflection prompts. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear decision points. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium.

## SK-04 — Model Artist Thinking Without Dictating Outcomes
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Practical task supplied. **TASK:** Model decision-making and reflection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Demonstrate thinking while leaving room for pupil choices. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Think-aloud script, teacher model, pupil prompts. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Live modelling/video.

## SK-05 — Review Creative Process Evidence
**ROLE:** Art assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Sketchbook evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify development in knowledge, skill, experimentation and decision-making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not infer effort or motivation solely from appearance. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, progression indicators, gaps, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/class.

## SK-06 — Map Artistic Progression Across Years
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Year-group samples or curriculum plans supplied. **TASK:** Identify progression in concepts, techniques, vocabulary and independence. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not treat increasingly complex materials as automatic progression. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Progression matrix and gaps. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct clarity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SK-07 — Diagnose Stalled Creative Process
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil process/sketchbook evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify where the process is breaking down. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observed evidence from assumptions about confidence, motivation or ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, possible barrier, discriminating check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## SK-08 — Build a Portfolio Reflection
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Selected work and objectives supplied. **TASK:** Guide pupils to identify development and future choices. **REQUIREMENTS:** Focus on evidence and decisions rather than generic self-praise. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Selection rationale, comparison, reflection, next goal. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specificity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** End-of-unit/year.

## SK-09 — Adapt Sketchbook Practice for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Sketchbook task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Remove access barriers without reducing reflection or artistic thinking. **REQUIREMENTS:** Offer alternative recording modes where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, independence route, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Creative construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## SK-10 — Retrieve and Reapply Creative Processes
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previous process knowledge and new project supplied. **TASK:** Design retrieval that informs a new artistic decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect vocabulary/process knowledge directly to experimentation or making. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer and progression. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/15 minutes.
