# Drawing, Mark-Making & Observation Workflows

**IDs:** DM-01 to DM-10

## DM-01 — Plan an Observational Drawing Task
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Subject, year group and materials supplied. **TASK:** Design an observational task. **REQUIREMENTS:** Focus attention on looking, proportion, shape, tone or texture as appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Setup, teacher prompts, pupil process, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Observation drives making. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DM-02 — Teach Mark-Making
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials and visual purpose supplied. **TASK:** Create a practical exploration of line and mark. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include experimentation rather than one prescribed outcome. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, exploration prompts, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technique and choice. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pencil/charcoal/ink.

## DM-03 — Develop Line and Shape
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Learning objective supplied. **TASK:** Create a sequence for exploring line and shape. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish contour, expressive and structural uses where relevant. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Teaching sequence and pupil task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Age-appropriate accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Abstract/observational.

## DM-04 — Teach Tone and Value
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and medium supplied. **TASK:** Teach pupils to observe and represent tonal variation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Explain light/dark relationships without implying a single correct artistic style. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Model, practice, challenge, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Visual accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Graphite/charcoal.

## DM-05 — Explore Texture
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Subject and medium supplied. **TASK:** Design a texture investigation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include visual and tactile language where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Exploration stations, prompts, vocabulary, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Meaningful experimentation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Natural/man-made objects.

## DM-06 — Diagnose Drawing Misconceptions
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil work and explanation supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely barriers in observation, representation or technique. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate visible evidence from inference about intention or ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, possible diagnosis, discriminating check, next teaching step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## DM-07 — Create a Drawing Scaffold
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Provide temporary support for observation and representation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not replace all artistic decision-making. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Scaffold, prompts, fade-out route, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Independence preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## DM-08 — Give Feedback on Pupil Drawing
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Work, objective and success criteria supplied. **TASK:** Give specific feedback on process and next artistic decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid generic praise and avoid treating stylistic difference as error. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence noticed, strength, next step, question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing/teacher notes.

## DM-09 — Compare Drawing Approaches
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Two or more approaches supplied. **TASK:** Compare purpose, marks, observation and visual effect. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid declaring one approach objectively superior without criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison table and discussion prompts. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Respect for artistic intent. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Artist references.

## DM-10 — Retrieval and Revisit Drawing Skills
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught technique and current task supplied. **TASK:** Create a short retrieval-to-application cycle. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect recall directly to a fresh drawing decision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, demonstration, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/20 minutes.
