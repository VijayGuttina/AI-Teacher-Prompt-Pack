# Artists, Craft Makers & Context Workflows

**IDs:** AC-01 to AC-10

## AC-01 — Introduce an Artist or Craft Maker
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Named practitioner supplied. **TASK:** Create an age-appropriate introduction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish verified biographical/contextual information from interpretation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, key works, questions, practical connection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No invented facts. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AC-02 — Analyse a Work of Art
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork or description supplied. **TASK:** Guide pupils from observation to interpretation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate what can be observed from what is inferred. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Observation, formal analysis, interpretation, questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## AC-03 — Compare Artists or Makers
**ROLE:** Art curriculum teacher. **CONTEXT:** Practitioners and works supplied. **TASK:** Compare techniques, purposes and contexts. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid superficial comparison or unsupported biography. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison matrix and discussion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Two/three practitioners.

## AC-04 — Connect Artist Study to Making
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artist/work and practical objective supplied. **TASK:** Translate observed ideas or techniques into an original pupil exploration. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not make imitation the sole objective. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, investigation, original task, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Authentic connection. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium.

## AC-05 — Explore Artistic Intent
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork and supplied context. **TASK:** Develop questions about possible artistic purpose. **REQUIREMENTS:** Label inference as inference and avoid claiming intent without evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, possible interpretations, questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Epistemic discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## AC-06 — Evaluate a Source About an Artist
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Source text supplied. **TASK:** Identify factual claims, interpretation and uncertainty. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not independently invent verification. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Claim audit and teaching guidance. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Source discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Teacher notes.

## AC-07 — Build Artist-Inspired Vocabulary
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Practitioner, artwork and year group supplied. **TASK:** Select useful visual and contextual vocabulary. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define terms accurately and link them to observable features. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Vocabulary table with examples. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical precision. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AC-08 — Diagnose Misconceptions About an Artist or Style
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil statement supplied. **TASK:** Identify factual or conceptual misunderstanding. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish factual error from a legitimate interpretation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, discriminating question, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## AC-09 — Adapt Artist Study for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artist study and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access to contextual and visual analysis. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve substantive knowledge and opportunities for interpretation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted input, scaffold, questions, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## AC-10 — Plan Retrieval and Revisit Artist Knowledge
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught practitioner/context and new making task supplied. **TASK:** Design purposeful retrieval. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect remembered knowledge to observation or making rather than isolated trivia. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, discussion, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Curriculum relevance. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 5/10 minutes.
