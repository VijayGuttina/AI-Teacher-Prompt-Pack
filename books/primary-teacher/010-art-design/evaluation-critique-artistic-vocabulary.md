# Evaluation, Critique & Artistic Vocabulary Workflows

**IDs:** EV-01 to EV-10

## EV-01 — Teach Art Vocabulary in Context
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork, technique and year group supplied. **TASK:** Select and teach precise vocabulary through observation and making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define terms accurately and connect them to visible or practical examples. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Vocabulary, examples, teacher prompts, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## EV-02 — Structure a Pupil Critique
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Work and learning objective supplied. **TASK:** Structure constructive peer or self-critique. **REQUIREMENTS:** Focus on evidence, artistic decisions and next steps rather than personal judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Notice, explain, question, next decision. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Age-appropriate and respectful. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pair/group.

## EV-03 — Give Process-Focused Art Feedback
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil work and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Provide actionable feedback. **REQUIREMENTS:** Refer to observable evidence and the intended artistic construct. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strength, evidence, next step, pupil question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specificity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## EV-04 — Compare Artistic Decisions
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Two works or approaches supplied. **TASK:** Compare decisions about line, colour, form, composition, material or process as relevant. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not equate difference with error. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence-based comparison. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## EV-05 — Build a Self-Evaluation Tool
**ROLE:** Art assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Unit objective supplied. **TASK:** Create a pupil self-evaluation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include knowledge, technique, experimentation and artistic decision-making where relevant. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Checklist, rating prompts, reflection questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Avoid rewarding conformity alone. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Printable/digital.

## EV-06 — Diagnose Vocabulary Misuse
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil statement supplied. **TASK:** Identify incorrect or imprecise terminology. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish vocabulary error from a legitimate interpretation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Statement, issue, precise meaning, discriminating question, reteach. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/class.

## EV-07 — Create a Hinge Question for Art Concepts
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Concept and misconceptions supplied. **TASK:** Create a diagnostic multiple-choice question. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distractors should represent plausible conceptual positions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, options, misconception map, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic power. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Mini-whiteboard.

## EV-08 — Moderate Art Work Using Shared Criteria
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Criteria and sample works supplied. **TASK:** Support consistent professional discussion. **REQUIREMENTS:** Judge against stated constructs, not taste or stylistic preference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence table, judgement rationale, uncertainty. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criterion fidelity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year-group moderation.

## EV-09 — Adapt Critique for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Critique task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Increase access to discussing and evaluating art. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve analytical demand and artistic agency. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted prompts, communication scaffold, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## EV-10 — Retrieval and Revisit Artistic Vocabulary
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught vocabulary and current artwork/task supplied. **TASK:** Build a short retrieval activity that feeds directly into analysis or making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use terms meaningfully rather than as isolated definitions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 5/10 minutes.
