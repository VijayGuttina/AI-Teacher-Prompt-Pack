# Sculpture, Form & 3D Construction Workflows

**IDs:** SF-01 to SF-10

## SF-01 — Explore Form and Volume
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials, year group and objective supplied. **TASK:** Design an investigation of three-dimensional form. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include manipulation, observation and reflection. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Exploration sequence and prompts. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate 3D vocabulary. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Clay/card/wire.

## SF-02 — Teach Joining and Construction
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials and intended structure supplied. **TASK:** Teach appropriate joining and construction techniques. **REQUIREMENTS:** Match technique to material and purpose; include safe handling. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, practice, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Recycled materials.

## SF-03 — Investigate Balance and Stability
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** 3D task supplied. **TASK:** Create a practical investigation of balance and stability. **REQUIREMENTS:** Encourage testing and revision rather than guaranteeing a result. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Challenge, test criteria, iteration log. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Physical plausibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Sculpture/mobile.

## SF-04 — Develop Surface and Texture
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Material supplied. **TASK:** Explore how surface treatment changes visual or tactile effect. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observed effect from subjective judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Exploration stations and reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Material accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Clay/card/found objects.

## SF-05 — Plan a 3D Design-to-Make Sequence
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Intended outcome and time supplied. **TASK:** Sequence idea development, prototyping, making and review. **REQUIREMENTS:** Allow purposeful revision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Lesson sequence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility and progression. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Number of lessons.

## SF-06 — Diagnose Construction Problems
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil model, process description or photograph description supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely structural or process barriers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observable construction issues from assumptions about skill or intention. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## SF-07 — Compare 3D Materials
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials and purpose supplied. **TASK:** Compare properties relevant to the intended construction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not invent material properties; distinguish typical from guaranteed behaviour. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison matrix and justified choice. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Available classroom stock.

## SF-08 — Give Feedback on Sculpture
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Work and artistic objective supplied. **TASK:** Give feedback on construction, form and artistic decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid prescribing one aesthetic outcome. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, next step, question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Objective alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## SF-09 — Adapt a 3D Making Task
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve physical or cognitive access while retaining meaningful 3D decision-making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify temporary scaffolds and independence route. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, fade-out, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved and safe. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## SF-10 — Retrieval and Revisit 3D Skills
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught construction skill and new challenge supplied. **TASK:** Reconnect prior knowledge to a new making decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include prediction, practical testing and reflection. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, demonstration, making challenge, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 15/30 minutes.
