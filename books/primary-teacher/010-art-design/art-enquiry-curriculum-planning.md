# Art Enquiry & Curriculum Planning Workflows

**IDs:** AE-01 to AE-10

## AE-01 — Plan an Art Enquiry
**ROLE:** Primary art and design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme, year group and curriculum intent supplied. **TASK:** Create an enquiry that leads to making, reflection and purposeful artistic decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define knowledge, skills and creative choices separately. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Enquiry question, sequence, resources, success criteria. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear artistic purpose. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AE-02 — Map Knowledge and Skills
**ROLE:** Art curriculum planner. **CONTEXT:** Unit and year group supplied. **TASK:** Separate substantive knowledge, disciplinary knowledge and practical skill. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid reducing progression to a materials checklist. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Progression map. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherent development. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Half-term/unit.

## AE-03 — Design a Sequence of Lessons
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** End outcome and available time supplied. **TASK:** Sequence exploration, modelling, practice, making and reflection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve time for experimentation and revision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Lesson sequence table. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Logical progression. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Number of lessons.

## AE-04 — Define Success Criteria for Creative Work
**ROLE:** Art assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Artistic objective supplied. **TASK:** Create observable, age-appropriate criteria. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not reward conformity as the only measure of quality. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Knowledge, skill and decision criteria. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-friendly language.

## AE-05 — Plan an Artist-Informed Unit
**ROLE:** Art curriculum teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artist/craft maker and unit theme supplied. **TASK:** Use contextual study to inform practical exploration without requiring imitation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish factual context from interpretation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, techniques, enquiry, making sequence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Authentic artistic connection. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Local artist.

## AE-06 — Plan Resource Use
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Intended activity and resources supplied. **TASK:** Plan efficient preparation and classroom organisation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe handling and realistic constraints. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Resource list, setup, distribution, cleanup. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Budget/class size.

## AE-07 — Diagnose a Weak Art Unit
**ROLE:** Art curriculum reviewer. **CONTEXT:** Existing unit plan supplied. **TASK:** Identify gaps in knowledge, skill, experimentation, making or reflection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Base diagnosis on supplied evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strengths, gaps, priority fixes. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-led. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Light-touch/full redesign.

## AE-08 — Adapt an Art Unit
**ROLE:** Inclusive primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original unit and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access while preserving meaningful artistic choice and practical learning. **REQUIREMENTS:** State what changes and what remains constant. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, independence route, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Artistic construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## AE-09 — Build a Retrieval-to-Making Bridge
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prior learning and new practical task supplied. **TASK:** Connect remembered knowledge to a new artistic decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid retrieval disconnected from making. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval prompt, discussion, application task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Purposeful transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 5/10 minutes.

## AE-10 — Review Curriculum Progression
**ROLE:** Art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Units across multiple year groups supplied. **TASK:** Identify progression, repetition and missing development. **REQUIREMENTS:** Examine concepts, techniques and independence separately. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Progression matrix and recommendations. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No assumed progression without evidence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2/whole school.
