# Colour, Painting & Printmaking Workflows

**IDs:** CP-01 to CP-10

## CP-01 — Explore Colour Mixing
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group, medium and objective supplied. **TASK:** Design a purposeful colour-mixing investigation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Encourage prediction, experimentation and observation rather than a single prescribed result. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Setup, prompts, exploration, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate colour terminology. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Paint medium.

## CP-02 — Teach Colour Relationships
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Colour concept supplied. **TASK:** Teach relationships such as warm/cool, complementary or analogous colours where appropriate. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid presenting subjective colour effects as universal facts. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, examples, practical task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Conceptual accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CP-03 — Develop Brush and Paint Control
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Medium and intended effect supplied. **TASK:** Plan technique practice. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect brush, water, paint and surface choices to observed effects. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, practice grid, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Watercolour/acrylic/tempera.

## CP-04 — Explore Opacity, Transparency and Layering
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Medium supplied. **TASK:** Create an investigation of paint properties and layering. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish material properties from artistic choices. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiment sequence and reflection prompts. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Medium accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Age group.

## CP-05 — Plan a Printmaking Investigation
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Print method and resources supplied. **TASK:** Design an exploration of repeat, pressure, texture, positive/negative space or registration as appropriate. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe preparation and cleanup. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, exploration, making, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Method accuracy and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Relief/mono/foam print.

## CP-06 — Compare Printmaking Methods
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Methods supplied. **TASK:** Compare process, control, texture and possible visual effects. **REQUIREMENTS:** Base comparison on actual methods, not invented properties. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison matrix and recommendation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Primary-friendly language.

## CP-07 — Diagnose Painting Difficulties
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil work and stated task supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely technique or decision barriers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate visible evidence from assumptions about ability or intention. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, possible diagnosis, discriminating check, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## CP-08 — Give Feedback on Colour or Painting
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Work, objective and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Give specific feedback on process and artistic decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid treating personal colour preference as a factual error. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, next decision, question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## CP-09 — Adapt a Painting or Printmaking Task
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access while preserving experimentation and artistic choice. **REQUIREMENTS:** State which barriers are removed and which artistic demands remain. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted task, scaffold, fade-out route, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Artistic construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## CP-10 — Retrieval and Revisit Colour/Print Skills
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prior technique and new making task supplied. **TASK:** Connect remembered process knowledge to a new artistic decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Retrieval must lead directly into making. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, demonstration, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer and practical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/20 minutes.
