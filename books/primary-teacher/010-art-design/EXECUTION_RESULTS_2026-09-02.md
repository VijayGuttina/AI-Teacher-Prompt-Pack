## Framework risk checks observed during execution

- Artistic agency was preserved where outcomes were intentionally open.
- Missing visual evidence was treated as a limitation rather than an invitation to invent details.
- Artist, artwork and contextual claims were not presented with unsupported certainty.
- Observation and interpretation were explicitly distinguished in analysis tasks.
- Diagnostic outputs avoided unsupported claims about motivation, effort or ability.
- Assessment outputs remained aligned to stated constructs rather than neatness or personal taste.
- Adaptations preserved the intended artistic or design decision where possible.
- Practical resource, timing and safety considerations were treated as implementation constraints.
