# Chapter 3 – General Teaching

## Chapter Overview

The General Teaching chapter is the foundation of the **Primary Teacher Prompt Pack**. Unlike the subject-specific chapters that follow, the prompts in this chapter are designed to support every aspect of effective classroom practice regardless of curriculum area.

Whether you are planning lessons, differentiating learning, improving assessment, managing workload or creating engaging classroom resources, these prompts provide practical, classroom-ready support that can be adapted to any primary subject.

Each prompt has been developed using a consistent prompt engineering framework to generate detailed, high-quality outputs from modern AI assistants. They are intended to reduce planning time, improve consistency and allow teachers to spend more time focusing on teaching and learning.

The prompts are aligned with evidence-informed teaching approaches and are suitable for teachers working across Key Stage 1 and Key Stage 2.

---

## Who This Chapter Is For

This chapter is suitable for:

- Primary classroom teachers
- Early Career Teachers (ECTs)
- Teaching Assistants
- Higher Level Teaching Assistants (HLTAs)
- Supply teachers
- Tutors
- Subject leaders
- Senior leaders
- Home educators
- Student teachers

Whether you are creating tomorrow's lesson, preparing a sequence of learning or looking for ways to reduce workload, these prompts provide a practical starting point.

---

## How to Use These Prompts

Every prompt follows exactly the same structure.

1. Read the **Purpose** to understand what the prompt is designed to achieve.
2. Review the **Editable Variables** and replace them with information relevant to your class.
3. Copy everything contained within the **COPY THE PROMPT BELOW** section.
4. Paste the prompt into your preferred AI model.
5. Review the generated output and make any professional adjustments necessary before using it with pupils.
6. Save successful outputs so they can be reused and refined in future.

For the best results:

- Provide as much classroom context as possible.
- Include the year group and curriculum being followed.
- Specify any SEND, EAL or additional learning needs.
- Be clear about lesson duration and intended outcomes.
- Never include personally identifiable pupil information.
