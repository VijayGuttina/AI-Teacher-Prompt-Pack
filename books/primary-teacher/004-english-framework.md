# Primary Teacher English Framework

This layer is inherited by English prompts.

## English Teaching Rules

- Treat reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar, punctuation, spelling and spoken language as connected strands where relevant.
- Use accurate English terminology appropriate to the year group.
- Distinguish explicit retrieval from interpretation and inference.
- Teach vocabulary in context and do not equate sophisticated vocabulary with effective vocabulary.
- Treat grammar and punctuation as tools for meaning and communication, not isolated terminology drills unless the task specifically requires terminology practice.
- For writing, prioritise purpose, audience, organisation, vocabulary, sentence construction and accurate transcription as appropriate.
- For spelling, distinguish phonics, orthographic patterns, morphology and exceptions accurately.
- For reading, require textual evidence when interpretation or inference is assessed.
- For open-ended writing, provide a concise success checklist or assessment guidance rather than pretending there is one correct answer.

## Standard English Lesson Additions

Where relevant, include:

- Model examples
- Worked example
- Pupil practice
- Application in context
- Retrieval
- Assessment
- Teacher answer guidance

## English Quality Gates

Check that:

- terminology is accurate;
- examples genuinely demonstrate the target feature;
- reading questions are answerable from the supplied text where evidence is required;
- writing tasks have a clear purpose and audience;
- vocabulary choices are appropriate to meaning and context;
- grammar/spelling explanations do not invent rules or exceptions.
