# Inclusion, Progression & Classroom Implementation Workflows

**IDs:** IP-01 to IP-10

## IP-01 — Adapt a D&T Task for SEND
**ROLE:** Inclusive primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Identify access barriers and design targeted adaptations. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve design thinking and meaningful technical learning where possible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, scaffold, independence route, safety check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Fine-motor/sensory/cognitive needs.

## IP-02 — Adapt D&T Language for EAL
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task, vocabulary and language need supplied. **TASK:** Make instructions and technical vocabulary accessible without reducing conceptual demand. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use visuals, examples and sentence frames where useful. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Accessible input, vocabulary support, prompts, independent route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Meaning preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Language proficiency.

## IP-03 — Plan Accessible Practical Setup
**ROLE:** D&T classroom teacher. **CONTEXT:** Activity, room and learner needs supplied. **TASK:** Plan layout, resources, tool access and movement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe access and realistic classroom routines. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Setup plan, resource stations, movement rules, contingency. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Class size.

## IP-04 — Preserve Design Agency During Scaffolding
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design/making task and identified barrier supplied. **TASK:** Design support that reduces access difficulty without prescribing the final product. **REQUIREMENTS:** State what support fades and which design decisions remain with pupils. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Scaffold, choices, fade-out, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## IP-05 — Build Technical Independence
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Learner currently reliant on adult prompting. **TASK:** Design a gradual-release plan using tools, checklists or decision cues. **REQUIREMENTS:** Fade unnecessary support while maintaining safety. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Current support, next step, fade plan, independence check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based and safe. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual.

## IP-06 — Map D&T Progression Across Years
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Year-group plans and/or pupil work supplied. **TASK:** Identify progression in design thinking, technical knowledge, making skill, evaluation and independence. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not treat more complex products alone as progression. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Progression matrix, strengths, gaps, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct clarity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## IP-07 — Plan Challenge Through Design Depth
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Secure learner/task supplied. **TASK:** Increase challenge through constraints, alternatives, testing, justification or refinement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not equate challenge with extra components or workload alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Core task, depth challenge, evidence of higher-level thinking. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Design construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## IP-08 — Manage Materials, Tools and Routines
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Activity, equipment and class size supplied. **TASK:** Create efficient routines for distribution, tool use, storage and cleanup. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safety-critical controls and contingency for limited resources. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before/during/after routine. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Resource constraints.

## IP-09 — Troubleshoot a D&T Lesson
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Planned lesson and observed implementation problem supplied. **TASK:** Identify practical, pedagogical or resource causes and propose proportionate fixes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observations from hypotheses. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, likely causes, quick fix, longer-term adjustment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Teacher reflection.

## IP-10 — Audit Inclusion and Progression Across a Unit
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Unit sequence, assessment evidence and learner needs supplied. **TASK:** Audit access, agency, technical demand, assessment and progression. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish genuine barriers from assumptions about learners. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strengths, barriers, adaptations, risks, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct, participation and progression preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Whole unit/class.
