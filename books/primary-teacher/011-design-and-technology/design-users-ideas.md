# Design, Users & Idea Development Workflows

**IDs:** DU-01 to DU-10

## DU-01 — Investigate a Design Brief
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief, year group and project supplied. **TASK:** Turn the brief into clear user, purpose, function and constraint questions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish stated requirements from assumptions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief analysis, questions, criteria. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Requirements traceability. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DU-02 — Identify User Needs
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product and intended user supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils identify relevant needs and priorities. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid stereotyping users or inventing preferences. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** User needs, evidence/questions, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## DU-03 — Evaluate Existing Products
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product examples supplied. **TASK:** Analyse function, materials, construction, aesthetics and user suitability. **REQUIREMENTS:** Judge against explicit criteria rather than taste alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Analysis table and discussion questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criterion alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## DU-04 — Generate Multiple Design Ideas
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Generate genuinely different solution concepts. **REQUIREMENTS:** Maintain feasibility while avoiding superficial variations. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Idea set, rationale, trade-offs. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diversity and feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Drawing/model format.

## DU-05 — Annotate a Design
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design sketch supplied or described. **TASK:** Create age-appropriate annotation prompts. **REQUIREMENTS:** Link annotations to materials, dimensions, function, joining and user needs as relevant. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Annotated-design checklist. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical relevance. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DU-06 — Compare Design Solutions
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Candidate designs and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Compare options and justify a selection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Make trade-offs explicit. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria matrix, decision and rationale. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based judgement. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## DU-07 — Develop a Prototype
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Selected design and available materials supplied. **TASK:** Plan a low-cost prototype or mock-up. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify what the prototype is intended to test. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Prototype plan, test questions, observations. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Purposeful prototyping. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Card/paper/recycled materials.

## DU-08 — Diagnose Weak Design Thinking
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil design evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify whether the barrier concerns user needs, criteria, function, feasibility or iteration. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate evidence from assumptions about ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypothesis, discriminating question/task, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## DU-09 — Give Feedback on a Design
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Give actionable feedback before making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not redesign the product for the pupil. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strength, evidence, design question, next decision. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency and criterion alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## DU-10 — Adapt a Design Brief
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original brief and learner/class need supplied. **TASK:** improve accessibility without removing substantive design thinking. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify unchanged design demand and adapted access route. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted brief, constraints, scaffold, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
