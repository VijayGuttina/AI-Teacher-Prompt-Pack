# Cooking, Nutrition & Food Skills Workflows

**IDs:** CN-01 to CN-10

## CN-01 — Plan a Cooking Demonstration
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Recipe, year group, equipment and lesson time supplied. **TASK:** Sequence a safe, clear cooking demonstration. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include hygiene, allergen awareness where relevant and key technical checkpoints. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Preparation, demonstration, pupil practice, cleanup. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical and safety accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** No-cook/simple cook.

## CN-02 — Teach a Food Preparation Skill
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Skill, food and equipment supplied. **TASK:** Teach the skill through modelling and guided practice. **REQUIREMENTS:** Match the method to the specified equipment and age group. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, guided practice, independent check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical and safety accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Chopping/measuring/mixing.

## CN-03 — Explore Healthy and Varied Diets
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and curriculum objective supplied. **TASK:** Create an age-appropriate learning activity about a healthy, varied diet. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid simplistic moral labels for individual foods and distinguish curriculum guidance from medical advice. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Input, activity, discussion, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Balanced and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CN-04 — Compare Ingredients by Function
**ROLE:** D&T food teacher. **CONTEXT:** Recipe and ingredients supplied. **TASK:** Explore ingredient roles such as structure, texture, flavour or binding where relevant. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid unsupported universal claims. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Investigation table, practical test, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Food-science plausibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Recipe.

## CN-05 — Adapt a Recipe Within Constraints
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original recipe, design brief, equipment and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Develop justified ingredient or process changes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Consider function, safety, dietary/allergen constraints supplied by the teacher and likely effect on outcome. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Original, proposed change, rationale, predicted effect, test. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible and clearly caveated. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Budget/equipment.

## CN-06 — Diagnose Food Preparation Difficulties
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil process/product evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely technical or process barriers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observable evidence from assumptions about effort or ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, possible cause, discriminating check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## CN-07 — Teach Food Hygiene and Safety
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Cooking activity, equipment and school procedures supplied. **TASK:** Create age-appropriate safety and hygiene instruction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Prioritise the school's current risk assessment and local procedures; do not substitute generic AI advice for them. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before/during/after checklist and teacher reminders. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safety-conscious and operational. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Activity.

## CN-08 — Evaluate a Food Product
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, brief and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate appearance, taste, texture, function and suitability as relevant. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish sensory preference from objective criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence table, judgement, improvement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## CN-09 — Adapt Cooking for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Recipe/process and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve physical, sensory, cognitive or language access. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve the intended food-preparation learning where possible and maintain safety. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, scaffold, alternative method, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct and safety preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## CN-10 — Retrieve and Reapply Food Skills
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught food skill and new recipe supplied. **TASK:** Create retrieval that leads into practical application. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect vocabulary, hygiene and technique to the new task. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, demonstration, cooking task, evaluation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer and feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/20 minutes.
