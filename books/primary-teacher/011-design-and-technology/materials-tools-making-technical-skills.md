# Materials, Tools, Making & Technical Skills Workflows

**IDs:** MT-01 to MT-10

## MT-01 — Select Materials for a Design
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design brief, constraints and available materials supplied. **TASK:** Compare materials against relevant properties and purpose. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observed classroom properties from general claims. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, comparison, justified choice. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical plausibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## MT-02 — Teach a Making Technique
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, material and technique supplied. **TASK:** Create a clear teacher demonstration and guided practice sequence. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include tool safety and quality checkpoints. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, practice, independent making. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technique and safety accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Tool/material.

## MT-03 — Match Tools to Tasks
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Making task and available tools supplied. **TASK:** Identify appropriate tools and explain their purposes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not recommend unsuitable or unnecessarily hazardous tools. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Tool-purpose table, safe-use notes, alternatives. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Age group.

## MT-04 — Teach Measuring and Marking Out
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Material and product supplied. **TASK:** Teach accurate measuring, marking and checking. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include units, reference points and tolerance where age-appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, guided practice, check routine. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Measurement accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Card/textile/wood.

## MT-05 — Diagnose Making Difficulties
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil product/process evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely technical barriers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observed evidence from assumptions about effort or ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, possible cause, discriminating check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## MT-06 — Improve Product Quality
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prototype and quality criteria supplied. **TASK:** Identify proportionate improvements to accuracy, function, finish or construction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Prioritise changes linked to the design brief. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, priority, improvement, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Prototype/final product.

## MT-07 — Plan Efficient Making Routines
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Class size, resources and lesson time supplied. **TASK:** Plan material distribution, tool access, making sequence and cleanup. **REQUIREMENTS:** Build in safety-critical controls. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before/during/after routine. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Limited resources.

## MT-08 — Adapt Making for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task, tools and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Reduce access barriers while retaining the intended design/making construct where possible. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish accommodation from lowering the technical demand. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, alternative method, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safety and construct validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## MT-09 — Teach Quality Control
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product criteria supplied. **TASK:** Create in-process quality checks. **REQUIREMENTS:** Checks must be observable and linked to function or design requirements. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Checkpoints, criteria, corrective actions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Measurable and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## MT-10 — Retrieve and Reapply Technical Skills
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught technique and new product supplied. **TASK:** Design a short retrieval-to-making activity. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect recalled knowledge to a new technical decision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, demonstration, application, quality check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer and feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/20 minutes.
