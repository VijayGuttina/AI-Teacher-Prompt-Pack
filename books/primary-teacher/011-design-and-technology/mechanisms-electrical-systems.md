# Mechanisms & Electrical Systems Workflows

**IDs:** ME-01 to ME-10

## ME-01 — Teach a Mechanism
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Mechanism and intended product supplied. **TASK:** Explain how the mechanism works and sequence a practical investigation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use accurate age-appropriate terminology and observable cause/effect. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, demonstration, investigation, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Levers/linkages/gears/pulleys.

## ME-02 — Compare Mechanism Options
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product function and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Compare mechanisms against function, reliability, space and making constraints. **REQUIREMENTS:** Justify selection using stated criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison matrix and recommendation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## ME-03 — Diagnose a Mechanism Failure
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product behaviour and construction evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify plausible causes of failure. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observed behaviour from hypotheses and propose a discriminating test. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, test, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## ME-04 — Design a Mechanism Investigation
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Mechanism and learning objective supplied. **TASK:** Create a controlled practical comparison. **REQUIREMENTS:** Change one relevant variable where feasible and define what pupils should observe. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, setup, variables, observation, conclusion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Fair-test logic. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## ME-05 — Teach Simple Electrical Systems
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Circuit components and product supplied. **TASK:** Explain and investigate a simple electrical system. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use correct terminology and age-appropriate safety controls. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Component roles, circuit task, fault check, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Electrical accuracy and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## ME-06 — Diagnose a Circuit Fault
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Circuit components and observed behaviour supplied. **TASK:** Create a systematic fault-finding sequence. **REQUIREMENTS:** Check likely causes methodically rather than guessing. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Symptom, checks, possible cause, corrective action. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safe and logically sequenced. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Battery/switch/lamp/motor.

## ME-07 — Design a Functional Electrical Product
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** User, function and available components supplied. **TASK:** Develop a simple product concept using an electrical system. **REQUIREMENTS:** Link circuit choices to the intended function and constraints. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, circuit concept, product sketch, testing criteria. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Functional coherence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## ME-08 — Evaluate Mechanism or Circuit Performance
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prototype and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate function using evidence from testing. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish measured/observed performance from opinion. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, evidence, judgement, improvement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing.

## ME-09 — Adapt Mechanism/Electrical Tasks for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Practical task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access without removing the intended systems-thinking or design construct. **REQUIREMENTS:** Maintain safety and meaningful decision-making. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, scaffold, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct and safety preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## ME-10 — Retrieve and Reapply Systems Knowledge
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught mechanism/electrical knowledge and new design task supplied. **TASK:** Create retrieval that feeds directly into a new design decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Require explanation or application, not isolated recall alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, application, design decision, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/15 minutes.
