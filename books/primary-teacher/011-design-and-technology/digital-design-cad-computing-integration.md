# Digital Design, CAD & Computing Integration Workflows

**IDs:** DC-01 to DC-10

## DC-01 — Plan a Digital Design Activity
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design brief, year group and available software supplied. **TASK:** Create a purposeful digital design activity. **REQUIREMENTS:** Digital tools must serve the design objective rather than replace design thinking. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective, task, tool use, design evidence, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Curriculum alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DC-02 — Use CAD to Develop a Design
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, constraints and CAD capability supplied. **TASK:** Sequence an age-appropriate CAD design process. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include dimensions, constraints and iterative refinement where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, modelling steps, checkpoints, test/export. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 2D/3D CAD.

## DC-03 — Teach Digital Modelling Vocabulary
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Software/task supplied. **TASK:** Create accessible teaching for relevant digital modelling terminology. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define terms through observable operations or design decisions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Vocabulary, examples, non-examples, application check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Terminology accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group/software.

## DC-04 — Diagnose a CAD or Digital Design Problem
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil model, error and software/task description supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely conceptual or procedural barriers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish design reasoning problems from interface-operation problems. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, discriminating check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## DC-05 — Integrate Sensors or Control Systems
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product goal, available components and curriculum objective supplied. **TASK:** Design a simple control-system activity where appropriate. **REQUIREMENTS:** Explain input, process and output at an age-appropriate level. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** System concept, components, sequence, test criteria. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Systems coherence and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## DC-06 — Evaluate Digital and Physical Prototypes
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Digital model, physical prototype and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Compare how well each represents or meets the design requirements. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish visual appearance from functional evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, evidence, judgement, next iteration. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based evaluation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## DC-07 — Use Digital Tools to Communicate a Design
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design and audience supplied. **TASK:** Select an appropriate digital representation such as annotated drawing, presentation or model. **REQUIREMENTS:** Communication must make design intent and technical decisions clear. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Representation, required annotations, audience check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clarity and accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil presentation.

## DC-08 — Adapt Digital D&T Tasks for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Digital task, software and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Remove access barriers while preserving design and computing intent where possible. **REQUIREMENTS:** Consider interface, language, motor and cognitive demands without assuming a learner's needs. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, scaffold, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct and safeguarding preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## DC-09 — Compare Manual and Digital Design Methods
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design task and available methods supplied. **TASK:** Compare manual and digital approaches for purpose, precision, iteration and communication. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid treating digital as inherently superior. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, comparison, justified method choice. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Context-sensitive judgement. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## DC-10 — Retrieve and Reapply Digital Design Knowledge
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught digital design skill and new product brief supplied. **TASK:** Create retrieval leading to a new design decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Require application, not software-menu recall alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, application, design decision, quality check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/15 minutes.
