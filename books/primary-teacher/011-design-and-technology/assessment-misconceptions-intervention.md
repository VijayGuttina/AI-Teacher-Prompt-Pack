# Assessment, Misconceptions & Intervention Workflows

**IDs:** AMI-01 to AMI-10

## AMI-01 — Design a D&T Exit Check
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Objective and prior learning supplied. **TASK:** Create a short check of relevant design, technical or systems knowledge. **REQUIREMENTS:** Assess the intended construct, not handwriting, neatness or product appearance alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Questions, expected evidence, interpretation, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AMI-02 — Create a D&T Hinge Question
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Concept and likely misconceptions supplied. **TASK:** Create a diagnostic hinge question. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distractors represent plausible conceptual errors. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, options, misconception map, teacher response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic value. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Mini-whiteboard.

## AMI-03 — Analyse Pupil Design/Technical Evidence
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil design, product or explanation supplied. **TASK:** Identify what the evidence demonstrates and what remains uncertain. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not infer motivation, effort or ability solely from product quality. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, interpretation, uncertainty, next check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## AMI-04 — Diagnose a Technical Misconception
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil explanation and practical evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify a likely misunderstanding of material, tool, mechanism, structure or process. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish conceptual misunderstanding from execution difficulty. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, discriminating task, reteach. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Topic.

## AMI-05 — Plan Targeted D&T Intervention
**ROLE:** Primary D&T intervention teacher. **CONTEXT:** Specific evidence and objective supplied. **TASK:** Design a short intervention. **REQUIREMENTS:** Target the identified barrier without unnecessarily lowering the design or technical objective. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, teaching move, guided practice, independent check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/20 minutes.

## AMI-06 — Group Pupils by D&T Learning Need
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Evidence from a class supplied. **TASK:** Create flexible groups based on current learning need. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use evidence, avoid fixed labels and specify regrouping conditions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Groups, rationale, task, exit check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-led and temporary. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Whole class.

## AMI-07 — Build a Retrieval-Re-teach Cycle
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prior learning and assessment evidence supplied. **TASK:** Build retrieval, focused reteaching, application and recheck. **REQUIREMENTS:** Recheck the same construct. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Cycle with prompts and evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Assessment-intervention alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 15/30 minutes.

## AMI-08 — Write a D&T Assessment Summary
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Pupil/class evidence supplied. **TASK:** Summarise strengths, gaps and priorities. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observation from inference and avoid overclaiming. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strengths, gaps, priority, recommended action. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence proportionality. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil/parent/leadership.

## AMI-09 — Adapt D&T Assessment for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Assessment task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Remove access barriers while assessing the same intended construct where possible. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish access accommodation from changing the construct and maintain practical safety. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, unchanged demand, alternative response mode, interpretation guidance. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Fairness and construct validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## AMI-10 — Evaluate Whether a D&T Intervention Worked
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Baseline evidence, intervention and follow-up evidence supplied. **TASK:** Judge evidence of change. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid causal certainty when evidence is limited. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Baseline, intervention, follow-up, conclusion, remaining uncertainty. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based conclusion. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.
