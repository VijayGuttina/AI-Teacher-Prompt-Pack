# Structures, Textiles & Product Construction Workflows

**IDs:** ST-01 to ST-10

## ST-01 — Explore Structural Stability
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Structure/product challenge supplied. **TASK:** Investigate how form, materials and joining affect stability. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use observable tests and age-appropriate technical vocabulary. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Prediction, build/test, observation, conclusion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical plausibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Towers/bridges/packaging.

## ST-02 — Compare Joining Methods
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials and product supplied. **TASK:** Compare suitable joining methods. **REQUIREMENTS:** Consider strength, flexibility, appearance, tools and intended function. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison, practical test, justified choice. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Card/textile/wood.

## ST-03 — Diagnose Structural Weakness
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prototype and test evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely structural causes and design a discriminating test. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate evidence from hypotheses. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Failure evidence, possible causes, test, redesign. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## ST-04 — Design and Test Packaging
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, contents and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Develop and test packaging for protection and function. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include material efficiency and relevant user needs. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, ideas, prototype, test, improvement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## ST-05 — Teach Basic Textile Construction
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Textile product, fabric and tools supplied. **TASK:** Sequence a practical construction skill. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe tool use and quality checkpoints. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, guided practice, independent making. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Joining/decorating/finishing.

## ST-06 — Compare Textile Materials
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product brief and fabrics supplied. **TASK:** Investigate properties relevant to the intended use. **REQUIREMENTS:** Base conclusions on practical evidence where possible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Test criteria, results, comparison, selection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Absorbency/strength/flexibility.

## ST-07 — Diagnose Textile Construction Problems
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Textile product/process evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely causes of inaccurate or weak construction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish technical knowledge gaps from execution difficulties. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, discriminating check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## ST-08 — Plan Product Construction Sequence
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Design and materials supplied. **TASK:** Create an efficient build sequence with checkpoints. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify dependencies and points where later work could make earlier correction difficult. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Ordered steps, tools/materials, checkpoints, contingency. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## ST-09 — Evaluate Construction Quality
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Finished/prototype product and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate construction against function, accuracy and finish. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid treating neatness alone as quality. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, judgement, priority improvement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criterion-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil/teacher.

## ST-10 — Adapt Construction for Access
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Construction task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Provide safe access adaptations while preserving the intended design and making construct where possible. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify which technical decisions remain with the pupil. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, scaffold, alternative method, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct and safety preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
