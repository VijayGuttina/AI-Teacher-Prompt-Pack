# Testing, Evaluation & Iteration Workflows

**IDs:** TE-01 to TE-10

## TE-01 — Create a Product Test Plan
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product brief and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Define practical tests for function, performance and suitability. **REQUIREMENTS:** Tests must produce observable evidence linked to criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criterion, test, evidence, judgement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Valid and feasible. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## TE-02 — Evaluate a Prototype Against the Brief
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief, prototype and test evidence supplied. **TASK:** Judge how well the prototype meets the brief. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate evidence from opinion and identify unmet requirements. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Requirement matrix, evidence, judgement, priority. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Brief alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil/teacher.

## TE-03 — Plan an Iteration Cycle
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prototype, test result and constraint supplied. **TASK:** Design a focused improve → retest cycle. **REQUIREMENTS:** Change should be linked to evidence and the original design requirement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Problem, proposed change, prediction, retest, conclusion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Causal reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** One/two cycles.

## TE-04 — Diagnose Why a Product Failed a Test
**ROLE:** Diagnostic D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Test outcome and product evidence supplied. **TASK:** Generate plausible causes and a discriminating investigation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not assert a cause without evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, test, likely finding, action. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## TE-05 — Compare Design Solutions Using Criteria
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Multiple prototypes and shared criteria supplied. **TASK:** Compare solutions and justify a selection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use stated functional and user criteria rather than aesthetic preference alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison matrix and recommendation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transparent judgement. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## TE-06 — Teach Fair Product Testing
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product and test question supplied. **TASK:** Explain how to make a useful comparison. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify what changes, what is kept consistent and what is measured/observed where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, variables, method, evidence, conclusion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Appropriate test logic. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## TE-07 — Use User Feedback Properly
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, target user and feedback supplied. **TASK:** Analyse feedback and identify actionable design implications. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish individual preference from evidence about the stated user requirement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Feedback themes, evidence, implications, priority changes. **QUALITY CHECKS:** User-need alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Peer/client testing.

## TE-08 — Evaluate Material or Construction Changes
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original and revised prototypes supplied. **TASK:** Determine whether a material or construction change improved performance. **REQUIREMENTS:** Compare against the same relevant criterion where possible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before/after evidence, judgement, limitations. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Fair comparison. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product type.

## TE-09 — Adapt Testing for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Test activity and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Make the testing process accessible while preserving the intended evaluation construct. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish access support from changing the criterion being assessed. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, evidence route, interpretation guidance. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct validity and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## TE-10 — Retrieve and Reapply Evaluation Skills
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previous testing/evaluation knowledge and new product supplied. **TASK:** Build a short retrieval-to-application activity. **REQUIREMENTS:** Require pupils to apply criteria and evidence to a new design decision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, test, evidence, decision, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Transfer. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/15 minutes.
