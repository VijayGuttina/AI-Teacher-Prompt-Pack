# Curriculum, Sustainability & Cross-Curricular D&T Workflows

**IDs:** CS-01 to CS-10

## CS-01 — Plan a D&T Unit
**ROLE:** Primary D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Year group, curriculum objectives, available resources and timetable supplied. **TASK:** Sequence a coherent design-make-evaluate unit. **REQUIREMENTS:** Progress from context and design through making, testing and evaluation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Unit map, lesson sequence, assessment points, resources. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherence and feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CS-02 — Map D&T Curriculum Progression
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Existing curriculum map and year-group evidence supplied. **TASK:** Audit progression in design knowledge, technical knowledge, making skills and evaluation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify genuine gaps, duplication and unclear progression. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Progression matrix, findings, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct clarity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Whole school.

## CS-03 — Design a Sustainable Product Challenge
**ROLE:** Primary D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** User, product and resource constraints supplied. **TASK:** Create a design brief incorporating relevant sustainability considerations. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid unsupported environmental claims and consider whole-life impacts proportionately. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, constraints, sustainability criteria, evidence questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Realistic and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CS-04 — Evaluate Sustainability Claims
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product/material claims supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils distinguish evidence from marketing or unsupported claims. **REQUIREMENTS:** Flag where reliable external evidence would be needed. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Claim, evidence required, confidence, design implication. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No invented evidence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product/material.

## CS-05 — Integrate D&T With Science
**ROLE:** Primary curriculum teacher. **CONTEXT:** D&T brief and science objective supplied. **TASK:** Identify a genuine connection where science knowledge informs a design decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not turn D&T into a science worksheet; preserve the design/making construct. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Shared concept, D&T task, science application, assessment evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Disciplinary integrity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Materials/forces/electricity.

## CS-06 — Integrate D&T With Mathematics
**ROLE:** Primary curriculum teacher. **CONTEXT:** D&T product and mathematics objective supplied. **TASK:** Build purposeful mathematical application into design or making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Mathematics must serve an authentic design decision rather than decorative calculation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Mathematical demand, design context, task, evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Both subject constructs preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Measurement/shape/data.

## CS-07 — Integrate D&T With Computing
**ROLE:** Primary curriculum teacher. **CONTEXT:** D&T project and computing objective supplied. **TASK:** Identify an authentic computing contribution such as control, modelling or digital communication. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid superficial software use. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Computing role, D&T role, activity, assessment evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Disciplinary integrity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## CS-08 — Plan a Resource-Constrained D&T Project
**ROLE:** D&T teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief, budget, equipment and material constraints supplied. **TASK:** Design a viable project under the stated constraints. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve meaningful design decisions and identify substitutions explicitly. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Constraint analysis, resource plan, alternatives, contingency. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility and safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Low-resource classroom.

## CS-09 — Audit a D&T Scheme for Curriculum Balance
**ROLE:** D&T subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Scheme of work supplied. **TASK:** Audit balance across designing, technical knowledge, making, testing, evaluation, materials and contexts. **REQUIREMENTS:** Base findings only on supplied evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Coverage matrix, strengths, gaps, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Term/year.

## CS-10 — Create a Cross-Curricular D&T Project
**ROLE:** Primary curriculum designer. **CONTEXT:** Year group, D&T objectives and one or more partner-subject objectives supplied. **TASK:** Design a coherent project with authentic disciplinary contributions. **REQUIREMENTS:** D&T must retain a genuine design-make-evaluate cycle and partner subjects must retain their own disciplinary integrity. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Driving brief, subject contributions, sequence, assessment, resources. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherence, feasibility and construct validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Science/maths/computing/geography.
