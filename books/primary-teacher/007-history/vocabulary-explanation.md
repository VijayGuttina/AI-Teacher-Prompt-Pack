# Historical Vocabulary and Explanation Workflows

## VE-01 — Historical vocabulary builder
**Purpose:** Build precise topic vocabulary.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; terms.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist.
### TASK
Define and contextualise essential vocabulary.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Term; pupil-friendly definition; historical context; example; non-example.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Definitions must be historically accurate and not erase important distinctions.

## VE-02 — Vocabulary in context
**Purpose:** Help pupils use historical terms accurately.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; terms; topic.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create sentence and application tasks using vocabulary meaningfully.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model sentences; completion task; application questions; answers.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not reward mechanically inserted terminology when the historical meaning is wrong.

## VE-03 — Historical explanation scaffold
**Purpose:** Scaffold an explanation of why or how something changed.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Build a claim-evidence-explanation scaffold.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; scaffold; model; success criteria; self-check.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Every explanation must be anchored to historical evidence.

## VE-04 — Causal language builder
**Purpose:** Develop precise language for causation.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; event; factors.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History pedagogy specialist.
### TASK
Teach distinctions among cause, trigger, contributing factor and consequence where appropriate.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Term; example; contrast; practice; answers.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not imply that causal language proves causal relationships.

## VE-05 — Historical paragraph model
**Purpose:** Model concise historical writing.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; evidence; desired length.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Write a model paragraph that uses evidence and explains its relevance.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model paragraph; annotations; success criteria; adaptation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not include invented evidence or unsupported factual detail.

## VE-06 — Improve historical explanation
**Purpose:** Improve a supplied pupil explanation.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; question; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Identify the smallest changes needed to improve historical accuracy, evidence use and explanation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Strengths; precise issues; improved version; teaching prompts; next attempt.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Preserve the pupil's intended meaning where possible and do not invent evidence.

## VE-07 — Explanation misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose whether weak explanation reflects knowledge or disciplinary reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; question; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Separate factual gaps, vocabulary problems and reasoning problems.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observable issue; evidence; diagnostic check; interpretation; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer general literacy or ability from one historical response.

## VE-08 — Historical discussion stems
**Purpose:** Support evidence-based classroom discussion.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; evidence; discussion goal.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create discussion stems for agreeing, challenging, qualifying and citing evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Stem bank; examples; teacher prompts; success criteria.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Stems must promote historical reasoning rather than generic opinion sharing.

## VE-09 — Adapt historical writing
**Purpose:** Make historical writing accessible while preserving disciplinary demand.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt sentence structure, vocabulary support, planning or recording while retaining evidence-based explanation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved evidence; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not reduce the historical thinking objective to copying or recall.

## VE-10 — Historical explanation success criteria
**Purpose:** Create usable criteria for historical explanations.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; evidence; curriculum objective.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Create concise, observable success criteria.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; pupil-friendly version; examples; self-assessment questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Criteria must assess historical accuracy and reasoning, not merely presentation.
