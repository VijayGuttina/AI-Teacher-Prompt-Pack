# Primary History Framework

## Purpose

A compact workflow library for primary teachers using AI to plan, teach, assess and adapt History in the context of the National Curriculum for England.

## Design principles

1. **Historical thinking first.** Workflows must develop chronology, causation, change and continuity, significance, similarity and difference, interpretation, and use of evidence.
2. **Evidence before assertion.** AI-generated historical claims must be treated as material to verify, not as an authority.
3. **Sources are not facts.** Workflows distinguish a source, what it says/shows, provenance, reliability/usefulness and the historical claim being investigated.
4. **Age-appropriate without distortion.** Simplification is permitted for accessibility, but material historical inaccuracies are not.
5. **Enquiry over activity generation.** A practical classroom activity must serve a historical question or disciplinary objective.
6. **No invented evidence.** If a primary source is requested but not supplied, the workflow must not fabricate quotations, archive references or provenance.
7. **Assessment should reveal historical thinking.** Answers should distinguish recall from explanation, inference, source evaluation and argument.
8. **Adaptation preserves disciplinary demand.** Accessibility support must not remove the historical reasoning being assessed.

## Workflow architecture

Each workflow uses a compact structure:

- Purpose
- Editable Variables
- ROLE
- CONTEXT where required
- TASK
- REQUIREMENTS where required
- OUTPUT FORMAT
- QUALITY CHECKS

## Quality gate

Every workflow must be checked for historical accuracy, curriculum suitability, disciplinary integrity, age appropriateness, source integrity, absence of fabricated evidence, and practical teacher usability.

## Verification principle

Authorship is not execution verification. A workflow remains `Not Yet Tested` until representative execution has been performed and recorded with the exact AI tool/model, date, input, result and reviewer notes.

## Review cycle

Default review: quarterly, or sooner after a material model change, curriculum change, identified historical error, or material workflow revision.
