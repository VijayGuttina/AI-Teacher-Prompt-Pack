# British History Workflows

## BH-01 — British history overview
**Purpose:** Build a concise, accurate overview of a specified British historical period.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; period; focus.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher and historian.
### TASK
Create a contextual overview supporting the stated curriculum enquiry.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; chronology; key developments; evidence; questions; answers.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Check dates, terminology and geographical scope.

## BH-02 — Monarchy and power
**Purpose:** Explore how power was exercised and contested.
**Editable Variables:** Period; monarch/rulers; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Examine power through evidence, institutions and people rather than biography alone.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; evidence; power structures; questions; judgement.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid implying that monarchs alone determined historical change.

## BH-03 — Conflict and conquest
**Purpose:** Examine a British conflict or conquest.
**Editable Variables:** Event; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Explain causes, events and consequences using appropriate evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Chronology; causes; evidence; consequences; questions; answers.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid presenting contested historical claims as settled fact.

## BH-04 — British society
**Purpose:** Explore social life and inequality in a period.
**Editable Variables:** Period; social groups; theme; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist.
### TASK
Compare experiences across relevant groups using evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Groups; evidence; similarities; differences; limitations; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid treating one group as representative of the whole society.

## BH-05 — Industrial change
**Purpose:** Explore industrialisation and its consequences.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; place; period; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Connect technological and economic change to evidence about people's lives.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Development; evidence; changes; continuities; consequences; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid single-cause explanations of complex social change.

## BH-06 — Migration and movement
**Purpose:** Investigate movement of people in British history.
**Editable Variables:** Period; group; evidence; place.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Use evidence to explore why people moved and what changed as a result.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; push/pull factors; evidence; experiences; limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid reducing migration to simplistic motives.

## BH-07 — Rights and change
**Purpose:** Explore a historical development in rights or representation.
**Editable Variables:** Period; issue; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Trace change over time using evidence and explicit criteria.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Before; change; evidence; significance; limitations; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not project modern legal or political concepts backwards without explanation.

## BH-08 — British local/national comparison
**Purpose:** Compare developments in two British locations or communities.
**Editable Variables:** Locations; period; theme; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Compare using consistent historical criteria.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; similarities; differences; explanation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Keep geographical and chronological boundaries explicit.

## BH-09 — British history misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose errors in knowledge or interpretation.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; pupil response.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History diagnostic specialist.
### TASK
Identify observable error and create a discriminating check.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Error; possible cause; diagnostic question; interpretation; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Separate factual recall errors from disciplinary reasoning errors.

## BH-10 — Adapt British-history task
**Purpose:** Adapt a British History task while preserving historical demand.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt language, source presentation or recording without removing the historical reasoning target.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved evidence; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
The adapted task must still require historical reasoning.
