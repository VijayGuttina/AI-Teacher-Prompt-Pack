# Chronology, Change and Continuity Workflows

## CC-01 — Timeline Builder

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 650–950 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Chronology, Timeline

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PERIOD]` | `[EVENTS]` | `[DATES]` | `[OBJECTIVE]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher and historian with expertise in chronological reasoning.

### CONTEXT
Create a timeline for `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils studying `[PERIOD]`, using `[EVENTS]` and `[DATES]` to achieve `[OBJECTIVE]`.

### TASK
Build an accurate chronological representation that helps pupils understand sequence, duration, overlap and historical context.

### REQUIREMENTS
Use only supplied, established or clearly marked approximate dates. Distinguish exact dates, date ranges and broad periods. Make the representation's scale explicit and avoid spacing events proportionally unless the model is intended to represent duration accurately.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Chronological scope; timeline; event descriptions; duration notes; chronology questions; answers; misconception check; limitations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Verify every date, ordering and period label. Check that events are placed according to the same chronological convention and that approximate dates are not presented as false precision.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for a visual wall timeline, card sort, digital timeline or comparative timeline.

## CC-02 — Sequence and Justify

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Chronology, Sequencing, Reasoning

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[EVENTS]` | `[TOPIC]` | `[EVIDENCE]` | `[DIFFICULTY]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher specialising in chronological reasoning.

### CONTEXT
Design a sequencing task for `[YEAR_GROUP]` on `[TOPIC]` using `[EVENTS]`, supported where necessary by `[EVIDENCE]`, at `[DIFFICULTY]` level.

### TASK
Require pupils to establish a historically accurate sequence and justify their decisions using dates, period knowledge or evidence.

### REQUIREMENTS
Include plausible ordering challenges without making the task dependent on trivial date memorisation. Where events overlap, explicitly address this rather than forcing a false single sequence. Include at least one justification task that reveals whether pupils understand why the ordering is correct.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Learning objective; event cards; evidence clues; correct sequence; justification prompts; common errors; answers; teacher interpretation.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every sequence must be historically accurate. Ensure clues are sufficient to solve the task without unsupported guessing.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for mixed-period sorting, within-period sequencing, cause-to-consequence sequencing or local history.

## CC-03 — Change and Continuity Matrix

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Change, Continuity, Evidence

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PERIODS]` | `[THEME]` | `[EVIDENCE]` | `[COMPARISON_CRITERIA]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist with expertise in change and continuity.

### CONTEXT
Compare `[THEME]` across `[PERIODS]` with `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils using `[EVIDENCE]` and `[COMPARISON_CRITERIA]`.

### TASK
Create an evidence-led matrix that distinguishes change, continuity, variation and uncertainty across time.

### REQUIREMENTS
Use consistent criteria across periods. Do not label something as change merely because two examples look different without evidence about the underlying pattern. Require pupils to identify both what changed and what remained similar, while recognising variation between people and places.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Scope; comparison criteria; evidence matrix; change; continuity; variation; uncertainty; explanation questions; model answers; judgement.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check that each claim is supported by evidence from the relevant period and that continuity is not confused with absence of change.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for homes, work, technology, transport, childhood, settlement or political power.

## CC-04 — Turning Point Analysis

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, History, Significance, Turning Points, Change

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[EVENT]` | `[BEFORE_EVIDENCE]` | `[AFTER_EVIDENCE]` | `[SIGNIFICANCE_CRITERIA]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in historical significance and evidence-led judgement.

### CONTEXT
Investigate whether `[EVENT]` was a turning point for `[YEAR_GROUP]`, using `[BEFORE_EVIDENCE]`, `[AFTER_EVIDENCE]` and `[SIGNIFICANCE_CRITERIA]`.

### TASK
Design an enquiry requiring pupils to define turning-point criteria, compare evidence before and after the event and consider alternative interpretations.

### REQUIREMENTS
Distinguish immediate change from long-term significance. Do not assume that a dramatic event was necessarily the most significant. Include evidence that may challenge the turning-point interpretation and require a reasoned judgement.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Enquiry question; criteria; context; before evidence; after evidence; arguments for; arguments against; judgement scaffold; model response; limitations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure significance criteria can actually be applied to the supplied evidence. Avoid presenting later outcomes as inevitable consequences of the event.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for political reforms, battles, inventions, social movements or local historical events.

## CC-05 — Period Comparison

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 650–950 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Comparison, Change and Continuity

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PERIOD_A]` | `[PERIOD_B]` | `[THEME]` | `[EVIDENCE]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist with expertise in comparative historical enquiry.

### CONTEXT
Compare `[PERIOD_A]` and `[PERIOD_B]` through `[THEME]` for `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils using `[EVIDENCE]`.

### TASK
Create a structured comparison using equivalent criteria and requiring evidence-based conclusions about similarity, difference, change and continuity.

### REQUIREMENTS
Compare like with like and define the scope of each period. Avoid flattening internal differences within either period. Distinguish direct comparison from causal explanation: a difference does not automatically establish why it occurred.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Scope; comparison criteria; evidence table; similarities; differences; possible explanations; limitations; conclusion questions; answers.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check that evidence from both periods is comparable and that conclusions do not overgeneralise from isolated examples.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for Roman and Anglo-Saxon Britain, Victorian and modern schooling, medieval and Tudor life or local historical periods.

## CC-06 — Chronology Misconception Diagnosis

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Chronology, Diagnosis, Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PUPIL_RESPONSES]` | `[TOPIC]` | `[TARGET_OBJECTIVE]` | `[RECENT_TEACHING]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary History diagnostic specialist.

### CONTEXT
Analyse `[PUPIL_RESPONSES]` from `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils studying `[TOPIC]` against `[TARGET_OBJECTIVE]`, considering `[RECENT_TEACHING]`.

### TASK
Identify observable chronology errors, generate competing explanations and design discriminating checks before recommending intervention.

### REQUIREMENTS
Consider accidental sequencing, vocabulary confusion, weak period knowledge, misunderstanding duration, scale confusion and overgeneralised chronological rules as separate possibilities. Do not infer conceptual weakness from one isolated error.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed evidence; possible explanations; evidence for and against; diagnostic task; expected response patterns; interpretation; targeted response; follow-up check.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Each diagnostic task must distinguish between plausible explanations rather than merely repeating the original item.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual conferencing, small-group intervention or whole-class hinge questions.

**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Chronology, Timescales, Representation

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PERIOD]` | `[DATES]` | `[REPRESENTATION]` | `[AVAILABLE_SPACE_OR_TIME]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher with expertise in chronological scale and historical representation.

### CONTEXT
Help `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils understand the duration and relative chronology of `[PERIOD]` using `[DATES]` and `[REPRESENTATION]` within `[AVAILABLE_SPACE_OR_TIME]`.

### TASK
Design a model or activity that makes relative duration, overlap and distance in time meaningful without creating false proportional claims.

### REQUIREMENTS

**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Generational Change, Oral History, Evidence

### CONTEXT
Explore `[TOPIC]` over `[TIMEFRAME]` with `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils using `[EVIDENCE]` and `[COMPARISON_GROUPS]`.

### TASK
Use personal accounts alongside other evidence to investigate how experiences changed within or across generations.

## CC-10 — Adapt Chronology Task

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, History, Chronology, Adaptive Teaching, Inclusion

### Editable Variables
`[ORIGINAL_TASK]` | `[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[HISTORICAL_OBJECTIVE]` | `[ACCESS_BARRIER]` | `[SUPPORT_AVAILABLE]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching and chronological reasoning.

### CONTEXT
The original task is `[ORIGINAL_TASK]` for `[YEAR_GROUP]`. The historical objective is `[HISTORICAL_OBJECTIVE]`; the access barrier is `[ACCESS_BARRIER]`; available support is `[SUPPORT_AVAILABLE]`.

### TASK
Adapt visual, language, interaction or recording demands while preserving the pupil's responsibility for sequencing, reasoning about duration or evaluating change and continuity.

### REQUIREMENTS
Analyse reading, vocabulary, visual access, working memory, motor demands and prior knowledge separately. Do not give the chronological order, key comparison or final judgement merely to reduce task difficulty. Include scaffold fading and a route towards greater independence.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Original objective; barrier analysis; adapted task; access support; preserved chronological demand; recording alternatives; scaffold fading; independence route; review point.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm that the same substantive chronological decision remains necessary and that the adaptation changes access rather than silently changing the learning objective.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for SEND, EAL, dyslexia, visual access, motor access, working-memory needs or communication barriers.
