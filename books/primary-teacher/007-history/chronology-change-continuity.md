# Chronology, Change and Continuity Workflows

## CC-01 — Timeline builder
**Purpose:** Build an accurate chronological timeline.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; period; events; dates.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a timeline using only supplied or verifiable dates and distinguish exact dates from approximate periods.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Timeline; event descriptions; chronology questions; answers.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Check dates, ordering and period labels.

## CC-02 — Sequence and justify
**Purpose:** Develop chronological reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; events; topic.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a sequencing task requiring pupils to justify the order.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Cards; correct sequence; justification prompts; answers.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Every sequence must be historically accurate.

## CC-03 — Change and continuity matrix
**Purpose:** Compare a society or theme across time.
**Editable Variables:** Periods; theme; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist.
### TASK
Create a matrix identifying evidence of change, continuity and uncertainty.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Periods; theme; evidence; change; continuity; explanation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not label something change merely because it appears different without contextual evidence.

## CC-04 — Turning point analysis
**Purpose:** Explore whether an event was a significant turning point.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; event; before/after evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create an evidence-led turning-point task.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; criteria; evidence; judgement; alternative interpretation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Make significance criteria explicit.

## CC-05 — Period comparison
**Purpose:** Compare two historical periods without flattening differences.
**Editable Variables:** Periods; theme; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Create a comparison using consistent criteria.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; similarities; differences; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Compare like with like and avoid unsupported generalisations.

## CC-06 — Chronology misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose errors in historical sequencing.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; pupil responses; topic.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History diagnostic specialist.
### TASK
Identify observable chronology errors and create discriminating checks.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Error; possible cause; diagnostic task; interpretation; response.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer conceptual weakness from one accidental sequencing error.

## CC-07 — Scale and duration lesson
**Purpose:** Help pupils understand historical timescales.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; period; dates; representation.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a representation that makes duration and relative chronology meaningful.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model; activity; questions; answers; limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Keep proportionality accurate when the model implies scale.

## CC-08 — Generational change
**Purpose:** Explore change within a human lifetime or across generations.
**Editable Variables:** Topic; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Use evidence to distinguish personal memory from broader historical change.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; comparison; questions; conclusions; limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not treat individual memories as representative of an entire population.

## CC-09 — Change/continuity writing scaffold
**Purpose:** Support evidence-based historical explanation.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; period; theme; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a scaffold requiring evidence for both change and continuity.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Sentence/function prompts; model paragraph; checklist; success criteria.
### QUALITY CHECKS
The model must contain historically defensible claims.

## CC-10 — Adapt chronology task
**Purpose:** Adapt a chronology activity while preserving chronological reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt visual, language or recording demands without removing sequencing, duration or change/continuity reasoning.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm the same chronological decision remains necessary.
