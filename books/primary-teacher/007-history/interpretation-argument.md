# Interpretation and Historical Argument Workflows

## IA-01 — Historical interpretation comparison
**Purpose:** Compare interpretations of a historical topic.
**Editable Variables:** Interpretations; topic; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Identify differences in emphasis, evidence and explanation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Interpretation A; Interpretation B; differences; reasons; evidence; judgement.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not invent historians' views or quotations.

## IA-02 — Why interpretations differ
**Purpose:** Explore why historical accounts may differ.
**Editable Variables:** Topic; interpretations; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Create a task examining evidence, perspective, purpose, available sources and historical context.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Factors; evidence; questions; model explanation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid reducing all disagreement to personal bias.

## IA-03 — Significance judgement
**Purpose:** Judge the significance of a person, event or development.
**Editable Variables:** Subject; criteria; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Apply explicit significance criteria and require evidence-based judgement.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; ranking; judgement; alternative view.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Make the criteria transparent and avoid unsupported claims.

## IA-04 — Causation explanation
**Purpose:** Explain causes of a historical event.
**Editable Variables:** Event; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist.
### TASK
Distinguish causes, triggers, contributing factors and consequences where appropriate.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Factors; evidence; relationships; model explanation; limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not imply that historical events have only one cause.

## IA-05 — Consequence analysis
**Purpose:** Analyse short- and longer-term consequences.
**Editable Variables:** Event; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Trace defensible consequences and distinguish direct evidence from later inference.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Consequence; evidence; timescale; explanation; limitation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid deterministic chains unsupported by evidence.

## IA-06 — Historical argument planner
**Purpose:** Plan an evidence-based answer to an enquiry.
**Editable Variables:** Question; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a claim-evidence-explanation structure with qualification where appropriate.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Thesis; evidence; explanation; counterpoint; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Every major claim must be supportable from the supplied evidence.

## IA-07 — Alternative explanation task
**Purpose:** Prevent premature closure in historical reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Enquiry; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Generate plausible alternative explanations based on the supplied evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Explanation A; Explanation B; supporting evidence; limitations; judgement.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not manufacture alternatives that contradict known evidence without flagging the issue.

## IA-08 — Historical debate
**Purpose:** Structure a classroom debate around evidence rather than opinion alone.
**Editable Variables:** Topic; question; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create positions, evidence requirements and rebuttal prompts.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Motion; evidence pack; roles; questions; success criteria; debrief.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Arguments must be anchored to historical evidence.

## IA-09 — Argument misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose weaknesses in historical explanations.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; question; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Identify errors such as unsupported assertion, chronology confusion, source misuse or weak causal reasoning.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed issue; evidence; diagnostic question; response; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer writing ability from historical reasoning alone.

## IA-10 — Adapt historical argument
**Purpose:** Adapt an argument task without reducing reasoning demand.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt scaffolds, language or recording while retaining the need to make and support a historical judgement.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved reasoning; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
The pupil must still select and use evidence to support a judgement.
