# Local History and Historical Place Workflows

## LH-01 — Local history enquiry
**Purpose:** Create an evidence-led enquiry about a local place or community.
**Editable Variables:** Place; period; year group; question.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Build an enquiry using local evidence and clear historical questions.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; context; evidence; activities; conclusion; limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not invent local facts, buildings, people or dates.

## LH-02 — Local source pack
**Purpose:** Curate sources for a local-history investigation.
**Editable Variables:** Place; period; available sources.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist.
### TASK
Organise supplied or verifiable local sources by evidential purpose.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Source; provenance; what it may establish; limitation; question.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Never fabricate archive references or quotations.

## LH-03 — Historical building investigation
**Purpose:** Investigate a local building or site.
**Editable Variables:** Site; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a source-led investigation of change, continuity and significance.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observation; historical evidence; questions; change; significance; caveats.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish architectural observation from historical inference.

## LH-04 — Local map comparison
**Purpose:** Compare historical and modern maps.
**Editable Variables:** Place; maps; period; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History and geography specialist.
### TASK
Create a comparison activity identifying defensible changes and continuities.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Map features; changes; continuities; questions; explanations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer a cause from map change alone.

## LH-05 — Oral history preparation
**Purpose:** Prepare pupils for an oral-history interview.
**Editable Variables:** Topic; interviewee; period; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create ethical, neutral and useful interview questions.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Purpose; questions; follow-ups; consent/safeguarding reminders; source limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Make clear that memory is valuable evidence but not automatically complete or representative.

## LH-06 — Local chronology
**Purpose:** Build a local timeline from supplied evidence.
**Editable Variables:** Place; dates; events; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a chronology and questions about local change.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Timeline; event descriptions; questions; answers; uncertainties.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Check dates and distinguish exact dates from approximate periods.

## LH-07 — Place significance
**Purpose:** Judge the historical significance of a local site or event.
**Editable Variables:** Place/event; criteria; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Apply explicit significance criteria to supplied evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; judgement; alternative view; limitations.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid declaring significance simply because something survives today.

## LH-08 — Local history fieldwork
**Purpose:** Plan a safe historical field visit.
**Editable Variables:** Location; year group; evidence targets; duration.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Design observation, source and recording tasks for a local visit.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objectives; route/task sequence; evidence sheet; questions; safety/logistics prompts.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not substitute unverified local claims for evidence; follow school safeguarding and visit procedures.

## LH-09 — Local history misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose errors arising from assumptions about a familiar place.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; local evidence; enquiry question.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History diagnostic specialist.
### TASK
Separate present-day observation from historical evidence and inference.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Error; evidence; diagnostic question; interpretation; response.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not assume familiarity equals historical knowledge.

## LH-10 — Adapt local history enquiry
**Purpose:** Make local-history work accessible while retaining evidence use.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; location/evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt route, source presentation, vocabulary or recording while preserving historical enquiry.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved evidence; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Pupils must still interpret historical evidence.
