# Significant People, Events and Comparisons Workflows

## SP-01 — Significance enquiry
**Purpose:** Judge why a person, event or development matters historically.
**Editable Variables:** Subject; criteria; year group; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create an evidence-led significance enquiry using explicit criteria.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; questions; judgement; alternative view.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not equate fame with historical significance.

## SP-02 — Significant person profile
**Purpose:** Build a historically balanced profile.
**Editable Variables:** Person; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Explain the person's actions, context, significance and limitations of the evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; actions; evidence; significance; limitations; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid hero worship and anachronistic judgement.

## SP-03 — Event analysis
**Purpose:** Analyse a significant historical event.
**Editable Variables:** Event; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Structure causes, key developments, consequences and significance.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; causes; chronology; consequences; significance; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid implying one cause or one consequence where evidence indicates complexity.

## SP-04 — Person comparison
**Purpose:** Compare two historical figures using consistent criteria.
**Editable Variables:** People; period; criteria; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Create an evidence-based comparison.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; similarities; differences; judgement.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure both figures are compared within their historical contexts.

## SP-05 — Event comparison
**Purpose:** Compare two events or developments.
**Editable Variables:** Events; periods; theme; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Compare causes, experiences, consequences or significance as appropriate.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; similarities; differences; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not force symmetry where the historical contexts differ materially.

## SP-06 — Ranking significance
**Purpose:** Make significance criteria visible through ranking.
**Editable Variables:** People/events; criteria; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a ranking task requiring evidence-based justification.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; items; evidence prompts; model judgement; alternative ranking.
### QUALITY CHECKS
There must be defensible criteria, not a predetermined ranking presented as fact.

## SP-07 — Legacy investigation
**Purpose:** Examine whether a person's or event's effects persisted.
**Editable Variables:** Subject; later period; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Distinguish immediate consequence, longer-term legacy and retrospective reputation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; timescale; legacy claim; limitations; judgement.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not assume present-day remembrance proves historical impact.

## SP-08 — Historical empathy with evidence
**Purpose:** Support contextual understanding without inventing thoughts or feelings.
**Editable Variables:** Person/group; period; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a contextual perspective task grounded in what evidence can support.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; evidence; possible perspectives; uncertainty; pupil task.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present imagined emotions or dialogue as historical fact.

## SP-09 — Comparison misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose weak or unfair historical comparisons.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; comparison; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Identify errors in criteria, context, chronology or evidence use.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed issue; evidence; diagnostic question; interpretation; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Base diagnosis on the actual response supplied.

## SP-10 — Adapt comparison task
**Purpose:** Adapt comparison while preserving historical judgement.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt presentation, language or recording without removing comparative reasoning.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved criteria; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Pupils must still compare using explicit historical evidence.
