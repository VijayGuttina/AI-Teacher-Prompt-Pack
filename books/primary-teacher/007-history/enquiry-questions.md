# Historical Enquiry and Questions Workflows

## HE-01 — Big enquiry question
**Purpose:** Frame a meaningful historical enquiry.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; period; disciplinary focus.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a substantive enquiry question that can be answered through evidence and historical reasoning.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Enquiry question; rationale; sub-questions; evidence needed; likely misconceptions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid questions answerable only by recall or with unsupported moral judgement.

## HE-02 — Enquiry sequence
**Purpose:** Build a sequence from question to evidence-based conclusion.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; evidence; lesson count.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject specialist.
### TASK
Design a coherent enquiry sequence in which each lesson contributes evidence toward the final answer.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; lesson sequence; evidence; disciplinary skill; interim conclusion; final task.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Every lesson must have a clear evidential or disciplinary purpose.

## HE-03 — Prior knowledge diagnostic
**Purpose:** Identify relevant prior historical knowledge before an enquiry.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; prerequisites.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Create a short diagnostic that distinguishes secure knowledge from likely misconceptions.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Questions; answers; diagnostic interpretation; teaching response.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid using the diagnostic as a proxy for general ability.

## HE-04 — Historical mystery
**Purpose:** Create an evidence-led historical mystery.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; supplied evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a mystery where pupils use supplied evidence to develop and revise explanations.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Mystery; evidence cards; questions; reasoning route; model conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not invent quotations, artefacts or provenance.

## HE-05 — Historical question ladder
**Purpose:** Move pupils from observation to historical explanation.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; source set; topic.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History pedagogy specialist.
### TASK
Create questions progressing from retrieval and observation to inference and explanation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question ladder; intended thinking; answers/guidance.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Label inference as inference rather than fact.

## HE-06 — Enquiry evidence planner
**Purpose:** Identify the evidence required to answer a historical question.
**Editable Variables:** Question; period; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary historian and teacher.
### TASK
Specify the types of evidence needed and what each could legitimately establish.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Claim; evidence needed; possible source; limitation; follow-up question.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not claim that one source can establish more than its evidence supports.

## HE-07 — Enquiry lesson plan
**Purpose:** Turn an enquiry question into a classroom lesson.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; evidence; duration.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a lesson with explicit historical thinking, evidence use and assessment.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; prior knowledge; input; enquiry; evidence task; assessment; misconception; exit check.
### QUALITY CHECKS
The activity must serve the stated historical objective.

## HE-08 — Pupil-generated historical questions
**Purpose:** Help pupils generate useful questions about a topic.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; stimulus; topic.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Provide prompts that help pupils generate answerable historical questions.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Stimulus; question stems; examples; sorting criteria; challenge.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish historical questions from simple factual lookups.

## HE-09 — Enquiry conclusion scaffold
**Purpose:** Support evidence-based conclusions.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; enquiry question; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create a scaffold that requires pupils to answer the question using evidence and qualification.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Claim; evidence; explanation; qualification; model answer.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not supply unsupported conclusions as if established by evidence.

## HE-10 — Adapt historical enquiry
**Purpose:** Improve access without reducing historical reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt language, representation, recording or sequencing while preserving the historical question and evidence requirement.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adaptation; support; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Pupils must still make the intended historical inference or judgement.
