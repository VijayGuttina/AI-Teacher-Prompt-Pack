# Assessment, Misconception and Intervention Workflows

## ADI-01 — History exit ticket
**Purpose:** Assess a specific historical objective quickly.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; topic; recent learning.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Create a short check sampling relevant historical knowledge and disciplinary thinking.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Questions; answers; what each assesses; interpretation; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Align every item to the stated objective.

## ADI-02 — History hinge question
**Purpose:** Diagnose a misconception during live teaching.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; misconception.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Create a hinge question with plausible, diagnostically distinct distractors.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; options; correct answer; distractor interpretations; teacher response.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Distractors must represent meaningful historical errors, not silly answers.

## ADI-03 — Historical knowledge assessment
**Purpose:** Build a balanced assessment for a unit or objective set.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; objectives; topic; item count.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History assessment designer.
### TASK
Create items covering appropriate recall, chronology, evidence use and explanation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Blueprint; questions; answers; marking guidance; interpretation; next actions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Check factual accuracy and alignment to each objective.

## ADI-04 — Analyse pupil History work
**Purpose:** Analyse supplied responses for strengths, errors and next teaching needs.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil work; question; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History diagnostic specialist.
### CONTEXT
Treat explanations for errors as hypotheses unless established by evidence.
### TASK
Identify observable errors and the smallest useful diagnostic next step.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Strengths; observable errors; pattern; hypotheses; diagnostic check; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer fixed ability, motivation or general literacy from insufficient evidence.

## ADI-05 — Source-use misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose misuse of historical evidence.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; source; enquiry question.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History assessment specialist.
### TASK
Determine whether the issue concerns observation, inference, provenance, relevance or overclaiming.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed issue; evidence; diagnostic task; interpretation; response.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Base the diagnosis on the supplied response and source.

## ADI-06 — Targeted History intervention
**Purpose:** Design a short intervention for an evidenced historical gap.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; gap; evidence; prerequisites; sessions.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History intervention teacher.
### TASK
Design a focused sequence using retrieval, explicit modelling, guided practice and independent application.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Need; prerequisites; sessions; modelling; practice; checks; exit criterion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not build an intervention around an unverified diagnosis.

## ADI-07 — History retrieval planner
**Purpose:** Reactivate knowledge needed for current learning.
**Editable Variables:** Current topic; prerequisite knowledge; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create purposeful retrieval that supports the current historical enquiry.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Questions; answers; prerequisite link; common error; re-teach note.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid generic historical trivia unrelated to the learning sequence.

## ADI-08 — Adapt History assessment
**Purpose:** Improve access to assessment without changing the historical construct.
**Editable Variables:** Original assessment; year group; access barrier; objective.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt language, layout, recording or scaffolding while preserving the historical knowledge/reasoning being assessed.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adaptation; support; preserved construct; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not provide the historical answer through the adaptation.

## ADI-09 — Evidence summary for teaching
**Purpose:** Convert assessment evidence into proportionate teaching decisions.
**Editable Variables:** Assessment data; pupil work; objectives; next lesson constraints.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History subject leader.
### TASK
Summarise what the evidence establishes, what it does not establish and what should happen next.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Secure learning; developing learning; errors; evidence limits; priority actions; diagnostic follow-up.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Every recommendation must be traceable to supplied evidence.

## ADI-10 — Unit review and reteach planner
**Purpose:** Use end-of-unit evidence to plan retrieval, reteaching and future sequencing.
**Editable Variables:** Unit objectives; assessment results; misconceptions; next unit; available time.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History subject leader.
### TASK
Identify secure knowledge, unresolved gaps and high-value reteaching priorities without overreacting to isolated errors.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence summary; secure learning; gaps; priority misconceptions; reteach plan; retrieval schedule; sequencing notes.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish cohort patterns from individual anomalies and keep claims proportionate to the evidence.
