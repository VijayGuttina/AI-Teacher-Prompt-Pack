# Ancient History Workflows

## AH-01 — Ancient civilisation overview
**Purpose:** Build an accurate overview of a named ancient civilisation.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; civilisation; period; focus.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher and historian.
### TASK
Create a concise overview focused on the stated enquiry rather than an encyclopaedic list.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; chronology; key features; evidence; questions; answers; caveats.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Check dates, terminology and avoid treating a civilisation as culturally uniform.

## AH-02 — Ancient society comparison
**Purpose:** Compare aspects of two ancient societies.
**Editable Variables:** Societies; theme; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Compare using common criteria and evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; similarities; differences; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Compare equivalent aspects and avoid anachronistic categories.

## AH-03 — Ancient source enquiry
**Purpose:** Use supplied ancient evidence to answer a question.
**Editable Variables:** Source; topic; question; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Analyse the source for content, provenance and usefulness.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observation; inference; provenance; usefulness; limitation; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Never fabricate translations, inscriptions or provenance.

## AH-04 — Ancient daily life
**Purpose:** Investigate everyday life using evidence.
**Editable Variables:** Civilisation; social group; topic; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary ancient-history specialist.
### TASK
Create an evidence-led reconstruction while identifying uncertainty.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; reconstruction; questions; what is uncertain; vocabulary.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present evidence from one social group as representative of everyone.

## AH-05 — Archaeology enquiry
**Purpose:** Use archaeological evidence appropriately.
**Editable Variables:** Site; artefacts; year group; enquiry question.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher.
### TASK
Create an inference activity using supplied archaeological evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Artefacts; observations; inferences; alternative explanations; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish what an artefact directly shows from what historians infer.

## AH-06 — Ancient religion and belief
**Purpose:** Explore ancient beliefs without stereotyping.
**Editable Variables:** Civilisation; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Explain evidence about belief and practice with appropriate uncertainty.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; practices; interpretations; vocabulary; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid presenting later interpretations as direct ancient testimony.

## AH-07 — Ancient technology
**Purpose:** Explore a technological development and its historical significance.
**Editable Variables:** Technology; civilisation; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History teacher.
### TASK
Link technology to historical context and evidence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Development; evidence; uses; significance; limitations; questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid assuming that technological change automatically produced a single social outcome.

## AH-08 — Ancient legacy
**Purpose:** Examine a defensible claim about ancient influence.
**Editable Variables:** Ancient society; later period; claim; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History specialist.
### TASK
Test the claim using evidence rather than asserting direct influence.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Claim; evidence; links; limitations; judgement.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish continuity, reuse, influence and coincidence.

## AH-09 — Ancient history misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose common misconceptions about an ancient society.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; pupil responses.
### ROLE
Act as a primary History diagnostic specialist.
### TASK
Identify observable errors and design discriminating checks.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Error; possible explanation; evidence; diagnostic task; response.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer misunderstanding from vocabulary alone.

## AH-10 — Adapt ancient-history enquiry
**Purpose:** Improve access while preserving historical reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary History teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt source, language or recording demands without removing the evidence-based historical judgement.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved evidence; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
The pupil must still reason from historical evidence.
