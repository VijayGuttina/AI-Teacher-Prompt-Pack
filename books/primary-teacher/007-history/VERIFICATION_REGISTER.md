## History-specific QA gates

1. Historical factual accuracy.
2. Chronological accuracy and periodisation.
3. Evidence is distinguished from inference.
4. Source provenance is not invented.
5. Quotations are never fabricated or attributed without supplied/verified evidence.
6. Historical interpretation is distinguished from established fact.
7. Causation, consequence and significance are not reduced to unsupported single-factor claims.
8. Age-appropriate simplification does not materially distort the history.
9. Assessment claims remain proportionate to supplied evidence.
10. Adaptive teaching preserves the intended historical construct.
