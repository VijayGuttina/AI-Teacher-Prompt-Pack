# Identity, Diversity & Belonging

This family provides ten copy/paste-ready workflows for primary PSHE teaching about identity, diversity, belonging, difference and inclusion. The prompts are designed to support respectful, accurate education without stereotyping, tokenism, forced disclosure or assumptions about pupils and families.

## Family-wide controls

Every workflow must:
- use developmentally appropriate language and examples;
- distinguish identity from appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, culture, language, disability, family structure and other characteristics rather than treating them as interchangeable;
- represent diversity substantively rather than through token examples;
- avoid stereotypes, caricatures, deficit assumptions and essentialising statements;
- never imply that one pupil, family, community or source represents an entire group;
- never require pupils to disclose their identity, family circumstances, beliefs, culture, disability, home life or personal experiences;
- avoid asking pupils to act as representatives or spokespersons for a group;
- use fictional or carefully selected examples rather than pressuring pupils to provide autobiographical material;
- use inclusive family and community representation without implying that every family has the same structure or experience;
- distinguish equality, equity, fairness, inclusion, belonging, identity, diversity and respect where relevant;
- avoid reducing diversity to festivals, food, clothing, famous people or cultural artefacts alone;
- avoid framing difference as a problem that pupils must solve;
- handle prejudice, discrimination and exclusion without victim-blaming;
- distinguish disagreement, exclusion, unkindness, prejudice, discrimination and safeguarding concerns;
- use school policy where supplied and never invent local safeguarding, behaviour or inclusion procedures;
- use current reliable evidence when demographic, legal or policy claims are required;
- assess knowledge, reasoning, vocabulary and application rather than personal identity, confidence, disclosure or conformity with teacher-preferred views;
- preserve the intended learning construct when adapting for SEND, EAL or other access needs.

Year group: [YEAR GROUP]

Known school expectations: [SUPPLIED EXPECTATIONS]

### TASK

### REQUIREMENTS
