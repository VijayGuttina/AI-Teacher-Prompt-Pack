# Resilience, Emotions & Decision-Making

This family provides ten copy/paste-ready ChatGPT workflows for teaching primary pupils about emotions, resilience, coping, decision-making, problem-solving and reflective self-management. The workflows are designed for PSHE education. They do not turn classroom activities into counselling, therapy, diagnosis or mental health assessment.

## Family-wide controls

Every workflow must:
- use developmentally appropriate language, examples and expectations;
- distinguish PSHE education from counselling, psychotherapy, diagnosis or treatment;
- avoid asking pupils to disclose trauma, abuse, self-harm, mental health history, family circumstances or other private experiences;
- never diagnose a pupil or infer a mental health condition from classroom responses;
- avoid presenting resilience as simply trying harder or tolerating harmful circumstances;
- distinguish ordinary setbacks, difficult emotions, persistent distress and safeguarding concerns without making clinical judgements;
- teach proportionate coping, problem-solving and help-seeking strategies;
- make clear that seeking adult help is a strength, not a failure;
- avoid victim-blaming and avoid implying that children control circumstances caused by other people;
- use fictional or low-risk scenarios where possible;
- avoid forced emotional disclosure, public sharing of personal experiences or activities that require pupils to reveal sensitive information;
- use school procedures where supplied and never invent safeguarding, pastoral or referral arrangements;
- use current reliable guidance if a workflow refers to mental health, health services or current policy;
- assess observable knowledge, reasoning and application rather than emotional state, personality, confidence or willingness to disclose;
- preserve the intended construct when adapting for SEND, EAL or other access needs;
- acknowledge that coping strategies vary between people and situations and avoid promising that one technique will work for everyone.
