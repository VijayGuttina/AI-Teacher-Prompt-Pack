# Relationships, Respect & Personal Boundaries

This family provides copy-paste-ready prompts for teaching primary pupils about respectful relationships, communication, personal boundaries, consent-related concepts where developmentally appropriate, friendship, disagreement, agency and help-seeking. The prompts use fictional situations wherever personal disclosure could create pressure. They keep classroom education distinct from safeguarding investigation, counselling and therapeutic intervention.

## RB-01 Plan a Respectful Relationships Lesson

**AI model compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot
