# Human Geography Workflows

**Workflow family:** Human Geography  
**IDs:** HG-01 to HG-10

## HG-01 — Explain a Human Geography Concept
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Topic, year group and objective supplied. **TASK:** Explain the concept accurately. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use precise vocabulary, accessible language and evidence; do not invent local facts. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, vocabulary, check question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy, clarity, curriculum fit. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add place example.

## HG-02 — Compare Places
**ROLE:** Act as a geography specialist. **CONTEXT:** Two supplied places. **TASK:** Compare human characteristics. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use comparable dimensions and supplied evidence; do not fabricate statistics. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Table plus model paragraph. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Like-for-like comparison. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add data.

## HG-03 — Explain Land Use
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Place and land-use evidence supplied. **TASK:** Explain patterns and purposes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observation from inference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence table and explanation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence fidelity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add map.

## HG-04 — Explore Population Patterns
**ROLE:** Act as a geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Supplied population data. **TASK:** Identify patterns and ask geographical questions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use only supplied data and units; avoid unsupported causal claims. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Pattern statements, questions, possible explanations. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Data accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## HG-05 — Explain Settlement Patterns
**ROLE:** Act as a geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Settlement map/description supplied. **TASK:** Explain distribution and location factors. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish evidence from hypothesis. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Factors table and paragraph. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Spatial reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Rural/urban.

## HG-06 — Explore Resource Use
**ROLE:** Act as a geography specialist. **CONTEXT:** Resource and place supplied. **TASK:** Explore how people use resources. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish physical availability from human decisions; avoid invented facts. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Resource-use chain. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Balance and accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Sustainability link.

## HG-07 — Analyse Human Impact
**ROLE:** Act as a geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Human activity and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Analyse impacts. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include benefits, costs and uncertainty where relevant. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Impact matrix and conclusion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-led reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add stakeholder perspectives.

## HG-08 — Design a Human Geography Enquiry
**ROLE:** Act as a primary enquiry designer. **CONTEXT:** Topic, place and year group supplied. **TASK:** Create an answerable enquiry. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define evidence needed and avoid assumptions about the place. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, sub-questions, evidence plan, outcome. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility and geographical demand. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Fieldwork.

## HG-09 — Adapt Human Geography Reasoning
**ROLE:** Act as an inclusive geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access while preserving geographical reasoning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve concept, evidence use and explanation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation and scaffold. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## HG-10 — Diagnose Human Geography Understanding
**ROLE:** Act as a diagnostic teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil responses supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely conceptual gaps. **REQUIREMENTS:** Base diagnosis on evidence and include a discriminating check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, hinge question, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group.
