# Locational and Place Knowledge Workflows

## LP-01 — Place profile
**Purpose:** Build an accurate geographical profile of a named place.
**Editable Variables:** Place; scale; year group; focus.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Geography teacher.
### TASK
Create a concise place profile focused on location, physical and human characteristics and the stated enquiry.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Location; physical features; human features; key vocabulary; questions; caveats.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not invent local facts and distinguish current information from historical information.

## LP-02 — Locate and describe
**Purpose:** Develop precise locational knowledge.
**Editable Variables:** Places; map; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary Geography specialist.
### TASK
Create questions requiring pupils to locate and describe places using appropriate geographical language.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Locations; questions; answers; vocabulary; map reference prompts.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Check place names, relative locations and scale.

## LP-03 — Compare two places
**Purpose:** Compare places at an explicit scale.
**Editable Variables:** Places; scale; criteria; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Geography teacher.
### TASK
Create a structured comparison using common geographical criteria.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; evidence; similarities; differences; explanation; conclusion.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not stereotype places or compare unmatched characteristics.

## LP-04 — Geographical region profile
**Purpose:** Explain characteristics of a region.
**Editable Variables:** Region; scale; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary Geography subject specialist.
### TASK
Describe the region using physical and human characteristics and appropriate spatial language.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Location; characteristics; evidence; vocabulary; enquiry questions.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Define the geographical boundaries used.

## LP-05 — Locational knowledge retrieval
**Purpose:** Reactivate essential place knowledge.
**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; locations.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Geography teacher.
### TASK
Create purposeful retrieval questions linked to current learning.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Questions; answers; common errors; follow-up.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid disconnected place-name trivia.

## LP-06 — Scale progression
**Purpose:** Move understanding from local to global scale.
**Editable Variables:** Topic; places; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary Geography specialist.
### TASK
Create a sequence showing how the same geographical issue appears at different scales.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Scale; example; relationship; questions; vocabulary.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Keep scale distinctions explicit.

## LP-07 — Place misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose inaccurate or stereotyped geographical understanding.
**Editable Variables:** Pupil response; place; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary Geography assessment specialist.
### TASK
Identify the observable misconception and create a discriminating diagnostic check.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Error; evidence; diagnostic question; interpretation; next step.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer general ability from one geographical response.

## LP-08 — Place enquiry question
**Purpose:** Create an enquiry about why a place has particular characteristics.
**Editable Variables:** Place; evidence; year group.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Geography teacher.
### TASK
Create a question requiring pupils to connect location, physical and human factors.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Enquiry question; sub-questions; evidence needed; final task.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid questions answerable only by copying a place description.

## LP-09 — Place facts to geographical explanation
**Purpose:** Move pupils from recall to explanation.
**Editable Variables:** Place; facts; year group.
### ROLE
Act as a primary Geography teacher.
### TASK
Turn supplied factual knowledge into questions requiring geographical explanation.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Fact; why/how question; evidence; model explanation.
### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not invent causal relationships not supported by evidence.

## LP-10 — Adapt place-learning task
**Purpose:** Improve access without removing geographical reasoning.
**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; evidence.
### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Geography teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.
### TASK
Adapt language, map presentation or recording while preserving the geographical objective.
### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; support; preserved demand; independence route.
### QUALITY CHECKS
The pupil must still use geographical evidence or concepts.
