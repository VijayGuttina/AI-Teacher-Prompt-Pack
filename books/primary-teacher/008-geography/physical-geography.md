# Physical Geography Workflows

**Workflow family:** Physical Geography  
**IDs:** PG-01 to PG-10

## PG-01 — Explain a Physical Process
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Topic, year group, prior knowledge and curriculum objective are supplied by the teacher.  
**TASK:** Explain the named physical process accurately at the requested level.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Use correct geographical vocabulary; sequence cause and effect; distinguish simplified model from literal reality; do not invent local facts.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Teacher-ready explanation, key vocabulary, one check-for-understanding question.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy; age appropriateness; causal sequence; vocabulary.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add location, diagram description or misconception.

## PG-02 — Build a Process Sequence
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Process and year group supplied.  
**TASK:** Convert the process into a numbered sequence.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** No missing causal links; use arrows/sequence language; flag where the model is simplified.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** 5–8 step sequence plus vocabulary.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Scientific/geographical accuracy and logical order.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add a sorting activity.

## PG-03 — Compare Physical Landscapes
**ROLE:** Act as a geography curriculum specialist.  
**CONTEXT:** Two supplied landscapes or named curriculum examples.  
**TASK:** Produce an evidence-led comparison.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Compare formation, features and processes; avoid invented statistics.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison table and short model paragraph.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Like-for-like comparison; accurate terminology.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify KS1/KS2 and scaffold level.

## PG-04 — Interpret a Physical Feature
**ROLE:** Act as a geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Image, diagram or description supplied.  
**TASK:** Help pupils identify and explain the physical feature.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Base observations only on supplied evidence; separate observation from inference.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Observation → inference → explanation table.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** No unsupported claims.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add vocabulary bank.

## PG-05 — Generate a Physical Geography Enquiry
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography enquiry designer.  
**CONTEXT:** Topic, place and year group supplied.  
**TASK:** Create a question-driven enquiry.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Question must be answerable from obtainable evidence; include data/source requirements.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Enquiry question, sub-questions, evidence, pupil outcome.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Geographical demand; feasibility; evidence availability.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Fieldwork or desk-based.

## PG-06 — Explain Cause and Effect
**ROLE:** Act as a geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Physical event/process supplied.  
**TASK:** Build a cause-effect chain.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish immediate and longer-term effects; avoid implying certainty where evidence is limited.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Chain plus model explanation.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Causal validity and qualification.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add misconception.

## PG-07 — Diagnose a Physical Geography Misconception
**ROLE:** Act as a diagnostic geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Pupil response supplied.  
**TASK:** Identify the likely misconception and design a diagnostic check.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Do not infer more than the evidence supports; distinguish error from incomplete understanding.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, likely misconception, hinge question, response pathway.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic validity.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group.

## PG-08 — Create a Diagram Brief
**ROLE:** Act as a geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Physical process supplied.  
**TASK:** Create a precise brief for a pupil diagram.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include labels, sequence and spatial relationships; do not rely on an unprovided map.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Diagram specification and marking points.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Spatial and process accuracy.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add blank-template instructions.

## PG-09 — Adapt a Physical Geography Task
**ROLE:** Act as an inclusive primary geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied.  
**TASK:** Adapt access without reducing the geographical concept.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve substantive and disciplinary demand; specify scaffold and independence route.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Original demand, adaptation, scaffold, stretch.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL/reading support.

## PG-10 — Retrieval and Re-teach Physical Geography
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography teacher.  
**CONTEXT:** Recently taught concept and pupil errors supplied.  
**TASK:** Create a retrieval-to-reteach cycle.  
**REQUIREMENTS:** Target diagnosed gaps; use cumulative retrieval; include exit check.  
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, reteach, guided practice, independent check.  
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Alignment to identified gaps.  
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify duration.
