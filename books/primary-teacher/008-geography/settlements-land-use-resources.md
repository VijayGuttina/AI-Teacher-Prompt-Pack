# Settlements, Land Use & Resources Workflows

**IDs:** SR-01 to SR-10

## SR-01 — Identify Settlement Types
**ROLE:** Primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Place/map supplied. **TASK:** Identify settlement characteristics. **REQUIREMENTS:** Base claims on evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Features table and explanation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Correct terminology. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Rural/urban.

## SR-02 — Explain Settlement Location
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Map/evidence supplied. **TASK:** Explain why a settlement may be located where it is. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observed factors from hypotheses. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Factor-evidence table. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Spatial reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** River/coast/transport.

## SR-03 — Analyse Land Use
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Actual map/image supplied. **TASK:** Analyse land-use pattern. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observation and inference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Pattern, evidence, explanation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Map fidelity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Local study.

## SR-04 — Compare Land Uses
**ROLE:** Geography specialist. **CONTEXT:** Two supplied places. **TASK:** Compare land use. **REQUIREMENTS:** Like-for-like categories; no invented statistics. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Table plus conclusion. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Comparability. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Urban/rural.

## SR-05 — Explore Resource Distribution
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Supplied map/data. **TASK:** Identify distribution and ask questions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve scale and units. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Findings and questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Data fidelity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Energy/water/food.

## SR-06 — Explain Resource Use
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Resource and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Explain how people use it. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish physical constraints and human choices. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation chain. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Sustainability.

## SR-07 — Evaluate a Land-Use Decision
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Decision scenario supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate options geographically. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use evidence and stakeholder perspectives without presenting opinion as fact. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Options matrix and reasoned judgement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence use. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Planning scenario.

## SR-08 — Design a Settlement Enquiry
**ROLE:** Enquiry designer. **CONTEXT:** Place and year group supplied. **TASK:** Create a feasible enquiry. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define evidence needed; do not invent site information. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, method, evidence, outcome. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Fieldwork.

## SR-09 — Diagnose Spatial Misconceptions
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil answer/map supplied. **TASK:** Diagnose misunderstanding. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify whether issue is location, scale, vocabulary or inference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Diagnosis, check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group.

## SR-10 — Adapt Settlement/Resource Tasks
**ROLE:** Inclusive geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access while retaining spatial reasoning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve map/data interpretation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted task, scaffold, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
