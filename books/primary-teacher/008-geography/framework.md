# Primary Geography Framework

## Purpose

A compact workflow library for primary teachers using AI to plan, teach, assess and adapt Geography in the context of the National Curriculum for England.

## Design principles

1. **Geographical thinking first.** Workflows develop place knowledge, locational knowledge, human and physical geography, geographical skills and fieldwork.
2. **Maps and data are evidence.** AI must distinguish observed information from interpretation and inference.
3. **No invented places or data.** When maps, datasets, photographs or local information are not supplied or verified, the workflow must not fabricate them.
4. **Scale matters.** Workflows distinguish local, regional, national and global scales.
5. **Fieldwork is purposeful.** Activities must connect data collection to a geographical question.
6. **Environmental issues require balance.** Avoid presenting contested claims as settled fact and distinguish evidence from value judgement.
7. **Adaptation preserves geographical demand.** Accessibility support must not remove the geographical reasoning being assessed.
8. **Assessment should reveal geographical understanding.** Separate recall, map/data skills, explanation and evaluation.

## Workflow architecture

Each workflow uses a compact structure:

- Purpose
- Editable Variables
- ROLE
- CONTEXT where required
- TASK
- REQUIREMENTS where required
- OUTPUT FORMAT
- QUALITY CHECKS

## Quality gate

Every workflow must be checked for geographical accuracy, scale, curriculum suitability, data integrity, map/data interpretation, age appropriateness and practical teacher usability.

## Verification principle

Authorship is not execution verification. A workflow remains `Not Yet Tested` until representative execution has been performed and recorded with the exact AI tool/model, date, input, result and reviewer notes.

## Review cycle

Default review: quarterly, or sooner after a material model change, curriculum change, factual/geographical correction, data issue or workflow revision.
