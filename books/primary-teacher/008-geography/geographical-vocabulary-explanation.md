# Geographical Vocabulary & Explanation Workflows

**IDs:** GV-01 to GV-10

## GV-01 — Build a Vocabulary Bank
**ROLE:** Primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Topic/year group supplied. **TASK:** Build a focused vocabulary bank. **REQUIREMENTS:** Prioritise disciplinary terms; give pupil-friendly definitions without changing meaning. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Term, definition, example, non-example. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** EAL.

## GV-02 — Explain a Geographical Term
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Term and year group supplied. **TASK:** Explain precisely. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid circular definitions and misleading synonyms. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Definition, example, misconception check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Terminological accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## GV-03 — Sentence Scaffolds
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Concept and writing task supplied. **TASK:** Create graduated sentence scaffolds. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve geographical reasoning. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Beginning, developing, secure scaffold. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Writing outcome.

## GV-04 — Model Geographical Explanation
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Question/evidence supplied. **TASK:** Produce a model explanation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use evidence, causal/connective language and appropriate qualification. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Model plus annotation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group.

## GV-05 — Improve Geographical Precision
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil response supplied. **TASK:** Improve terminology and reasoning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not replace pupil thinking with unexplained expert language. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Original issue, improved version, explanation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy and accessibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Vocabulary target.

## GV-06 — Distinguish Similar Terms
**ROLE:** Geography specialist. **CONTEXT:** Confusable terms supplied. **TASK:** Explain distinctions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use contrasting examples. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison table and quick check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No false distinctions. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Topic.

## GV-07 — Build a Retrieval Quiz
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Vocabulary/topic supplied. **TASK:** Create retrieval questions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Test meaning and application, not only recall. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Questions and answers. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy and coverage. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Difficulty levels.

## GV-08 — Diagnose Vocabulary Barriers
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil response supplied. **TASK:** Determine whether difficulty is vocabulary or conceptual. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use a discriminating check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** EAL/SEND.

## GV-09 — Adapt Explanation for Accessibility
**ROLE:** Inclusive geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Explanation and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve accessibility without reducing geographical precision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Retain disciplinary vocabulary with supported definitions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted explanation and scaffold. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy and access. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Reading level.

## GV-10 — Assess Geographical Explanation
**ROLE:** Geography assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Question, criteria and pupil response supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate geographical explanation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate factual accuracy, evidence use, terminology and reasoning. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strengths, gaps, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criterion alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Rubric.
