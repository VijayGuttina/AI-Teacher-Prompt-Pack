# Climate, Weather & Environmental Change Workflows

**IDs:** CW-01 to CW-10

## CW-01 — Distinguish Weather and Climate
**ROLE:** Primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and objective supplied. **TASK:** Explain the distinction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use accurate timescale language and examples; avoid misleading absolutes. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, examples, check question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Conceptual accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CW-02 — Interpret Weather Data
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Actual weather table supplied. **TASK:** Identify patterns. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve units and dates; separate observation from explanation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Pattern statements and evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Data fidelity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Graph support.

## CW-03 — Explain a Water-Cycle Link
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Water-cycle component supplied. **TASK:** Explain a linked process. **REQUIREMENTS:** Sequence processes accurately; use vocabulary. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Sequence and explanation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Process accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Diagram.

## CW-04 — Explain Climate Patterns
**ROLE:** Geography specialist. **CONTEXT:** Place/data supplied. **TASK:** Explain a climate pattern. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use supplied evidence and qualify explanations where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence and explanation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unsupported causal claims. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Latitude/altitude.

## CW-05 — Explore Environmental Change
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Evidence set supplied. **TASK:** Identify observed change and possible drivers. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate measured evidence, inference and uncertainty. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence matrix. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Claim discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Local/global.

## CW-06 — Explain Human Responses
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Environmental issue supplied. **TASK:** Compare responses. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish factual effectiveness claims from value judgements. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Response comparison. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Balance and evidence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Stakeholders.

## CW-07 — Evaluate an Environmental Claim
**ROLE:** Critical geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Claim and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Test whether evidence supports the claim. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify missing evidence and uncertainty. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Claim, evidence, judgement, caveat. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Logical validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Media literacy.

## CW-08 — Create a Climate/Weather Enquiry
**ROLE:** Enquiry designer. **CONTEXT:** Place, data and year group supplied. **TASK:** Design an answerable enquiry. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define evidence and avoid invented local information. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, evidence plan, outcome. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Fieldwork.

## CW-09 — Diagnose Climate/Weather Misconceptions
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil response supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely misconception. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use evidence and a discriminating check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Diagnosis, hinge question, reteach. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic strength. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group.

## CW-10 — Adapt Climate/Environmental Learning
**ROLE:** Inclusive geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Adapt access while preserving geographical reasoning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Retain evidence interpretation and conceptual demand. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted task and scaffold. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
