# Geography Worked Examples

These examples demonstrate workflow use. They are examples, not execution-verification evidence.

## 1. Map reasoning
**Workflow:** MS-08  
**Input:** A supplied school-area map showing a river, road and settlement.  
**Expected approach:** Identify only visible features, then infer possible relationships and label those inferences as hypotheses. Do not invent distances or local facts.

## 2. Physical process
**Workflow:** PG-02  
**Input:** Explain the stages of the water cycle to Year 4.  
**Expected approach:** Give a clear sequence using evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection, while keeping the explanation age-appropriate.

## 3. Fieldwork conclusion
**Workflow:** GF-06  
**Input:** Use the supplied traffic-count table to answer the enquiry question.  
**Expected approach:** Cite actual values, state the supported conclusion and identify limitations rather than inventing additional observations.

## 4. Sustainability decision
**Workflow:** IS-04  
**Input:** Compare two supplied school-energy options using the supplied evidence.  
**Expected approach:** Compare environmental, social and economic considerations and make a qualified evidence-based judgement.

## 5. Vocabulary diagnosis
**Workflow:** GV-08  
**Input:** A pupil uses “weather” and “climate” interchangeably.  
**Expected approach:** Test whether the issue is terminology alone or a deeper timescale misconception, then select the smallest effective intervention.

## 6. Assessment diagnosis
**Workflow:** GA-04  
**Input:** Three pupil responses to a map-and-explanation question.  
**Expected approach:** Identify evidenced strengths and gaps, distinguish conceptual errors from vocabulary/access issues, and propose targeted next teaching steps.
