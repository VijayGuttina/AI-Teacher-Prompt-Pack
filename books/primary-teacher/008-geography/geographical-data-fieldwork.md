# Geographical Data & Fieldwork Workflows

**IDs:** GF-01 to GF-10

## GF-01 — Plan Fieldwork
**ROLE:** Act as a primary geography fieldwork designer. **CONTEXT:** Question, location, year group and practical constraints supplied. **TASK:** Plan feasible fieldwork. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define observations, sampling, equipment and recording; never invent site conditions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Fieldwork plan. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safety, feasibility, validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School grounds/local area.

## GF-02 — Design a Data Collection Sheet
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Enquiry supplied. **TASK:** Design a recording sheet. **REQUIREMENTS:** Clear variables/categories, units and instructions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Printable table plus guidance. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Data usability. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add tally columns.

## GF-03 — Sampling Strategy
**ROLE:** Geography fieldwork specialist. **CONTEXT:** Enquiry and site supplied. **TASK:** Recommend an age-appropriate sampling approach. **REQUIREMENTS:** Explain rationale and limitations; do not claim representativeness without justification. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Method, rationale, limitation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Sampling validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Systematic/random/convenience.

## GF-04 — Analyse Pupil Data
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Actual dataset supplied. **TASK:** Identify patterns. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve values and units; distinguish pattern from explanation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Findings, evidence, questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No fabricated data. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add graph.

## GF-05 — Choose a Graph
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Dataset and enquiry supplied. **TASK:** Select and specify an appropriate graph. **REQUIREMENTS:** Match graph to variable types and retain labels/units. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Recommendation and plotting instructions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Appropriate representation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Spreadsheet instructions.

## GF-06 — Interpret Fieldwork Results
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Results supplied. **TASK:** Build an evidence-based conclusion. **REQUIREMENTS:** Cite data, acknowledge limitations and avoid causal overclaiming. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence → conclusion → limitation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Valid inference. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Sentence scaffold.

## GF-07 — Evaluate a Method
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Fieldwork method supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate reliability, validity and feasibility. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate actual limitations from generic ones. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strengths, limitations, improvements. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specificity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add pupil reflection.

## GF-08 — Fieldwork Risk and Practicality Check
**ROLE:** Primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Proposed activity supplied. **TASK:** Identify practical/safety considerations. **REQUIREMENTS:** Keep advice proportionate; do not invent hazards. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Risk considerations and mitigations. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Age-appropriate practicality. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School policy.

## GF-09 — Convert Data into a Geographical Argument
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Dataset and question supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils construct an argument. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use precise evidence and acknowledge counter-evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Claim, evidence, reasoning, limitation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## GF-10 — Diagnose Data-Interpretation Errors
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil graph/conclusion supplied. **TASK:** Diagnose the error and plan reteaching. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify whether error concerns reading, calculation, representation or inference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Diagnosis, check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add original dataset.
