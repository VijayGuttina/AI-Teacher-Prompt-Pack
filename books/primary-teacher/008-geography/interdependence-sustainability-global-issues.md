# Interdependence, Sustainability & Global Issues Workflows

**IDs:** IS-01 to IS-10

## IS-01 — Explain Interdependence
**ROLE:** Primary geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Topic and year group supplied. **TASK:** Explain connections between places/people. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use evidence and avoid stereotypes. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Connection chain and vocabulary. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accuracy and balance. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Food/trade/resources.

## IS-02 — Analyse a Global Supply Chain
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Supplied product journey. **TASK:** Map geographical connections. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use only verified/supplied stages. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Chain, locations, dependencies. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No invented stages. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Product supplied.

## IS-03 — Explore Sustainability
**ROLE:** Geography specialist. **CONTEXT:** Issue and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Explore environmental, social and economic dimensions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish evidence from value judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Three-dimension matrix. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Balance. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Local/global.

## IS-04 — Compare Sustainability Options
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Options and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Compare likely consequences. **REQUIREMENTS:** State assumptions and uncertainty. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Decision matrix and reasoned judgement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Stakeholders.

## IS-05 — Evaluate a Global Issue Claim
**ROLE:** Critical geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Claim and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate support. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify missing evidence and distinguish fact/opinion. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Claim-evidence judgement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Media literacy.

## IS-06 — Design a Sustainability Enquiry
**ROLE:** Enquiry designer. **CONTEXT:** Topic, place and evidence constraints supplied. **TASK:** Design an answerable enquiry. **REQUIREMENTS:** No invented local data. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, evidence, method, outcome. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School/community.

## IS-07 — Explore Stakeholder Perspectives
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Issue and named stakeholders supplied. **TASK:** Compare perspectives. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not invent quotations or attribute unsupported views. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Perspective matrix using paraphrase. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Attribution discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add evidence.

## IS-08 — Build an Evidence-Based Recommendation
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Decision question and evidence supplied. **TASK:** Produce a reasoned recommendation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Cite supplied evidence and acknowledge trade-offs. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Recommendation with evidence and caveat. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Logical support. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## IS-09 — Diagnose Sustainability Misconceptions
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil response supplied. **TASK:** Identify misconception and test it. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid assuming attitudes from one response. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, hinge question, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group.

## IS-10 — Adapt Global-Issue Learning
**ROLE:** Inclusive geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Adapt access while preserving evidence-based geographical reasoning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Retain complexity and avoid oversimplification. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
