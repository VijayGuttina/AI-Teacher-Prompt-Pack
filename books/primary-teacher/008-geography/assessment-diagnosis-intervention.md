# Geography Assessment, Diagnosis & Intervention Workflows

**IDs:** GA-01 to GA-10

## GA-01 — Exit Ticket
**ROLE:** Geography assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Objective and year group supplied. **TASK:** Create a short exit ticket. **REQUIREMENTS:** Assess the intended geographical construct. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** 3–5 questions, answers, diagnostic interpretation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Difficulty.

## GA-02 — Hinge Question
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Concept and misconception supplied. **TASK:** Create a discriminating hinge question. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distractors must represent plausible misconceptions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Question, options, diagnosis, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic power. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Topic.

## GA-03 — Objective-Aligned Assessment
**ROLE:** Geography assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Curriculum objective supplied. **TASK:** Design an assessment. **REQUIREMENTS:** Balance knowledge and geographical reasoning where required. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Assessment, mark guidance, success criteria. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## GA-04 — Analyse Pupil Work
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Actual pupil response/work supplied. **TASK:** Identify strengths and gaps. **REQUIREMENTS:** Evidence before inference; do not overdiagnose. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Diagnostic validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Multiple pupils.

## GA-05 — Flexible Grouping
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Assessment evidence supplied. **TASK:** Create temporary instructional groups. **REQUIREMENTS:** Group by specific learning need, not labels; include regrouping trigger. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Groups, need, teaching response, review point. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence basis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Class size.

## GA-06 — Targeted Intervention
**ROLE:** Geography intervention specialist. **CONTEXT:** Diagnosed gap supplied. **TASK:** Design a short intervention. **REQUIREMENTS:** Target the construct and include pre/post check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective, teaching sequence, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Alignment and feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Duration.

## GA-07 — Misconception Check
**ROLE:** Diagnostic geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Suspected misconception supplied. **TASK:** Create a diagnostic sequence. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish misconception from vocabulary/access issue. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Check, interpretation, reteach. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Discriminating evidence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group.

## GA-08 — Adaptive Geography Task
**ROLE:** Inclusive geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner profile supplied. **TASK:** Adapt access. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve geographical concept, evidence and reasoning. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, independence route, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## GA-09 — Retrieval and Re-teach Cycle
**ROLE:** Geography teacher. **CONTEXT:** Assessment evidence supplied. **TASK:** Build a retrieval/reteach cycle. **REQUIREMENTS:** Start from diagnosed gaps and finish with a fresh check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, reteach, practice, reassessment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** One lesson/week.

## GA-10 — Evidence-Based Assessment Summary
**ROLE:** Geography assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Pupil/class evidence supplied. **TASK:** Summarise attainment and priorities. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observed evidence from inference; avoid unsupported labels. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Strengths, gaps, priorities, planned response, review measure. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence traceability. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Teacher report.
