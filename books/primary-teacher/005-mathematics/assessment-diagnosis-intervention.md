# Assessment, Diagnosis and Intervention Workflows

## ADI-01 — Mathematics Exit Ticket

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Assessment, Exit Ticket, Formative Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[PREREQUISITES]` | `[NUMBER_OF_QUESTIONS]` | `[DIFFICULTY]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics assessment specialist.

### CONTEXT
Create an exit ticket for `[YEAR_GROUP]` on `[OBJECTIVE]`. Relevant prerequisites are `[PREREQUISITES]`; include `[NUMBER_OF_QUESTIONS]` questions at `[DIFFICULTY]` level.

### TASK
Create a concise assessment that samples the intended mathematical understanding through appropriately varied items and produces actionable evidence for the next lesson.

### REQUIREMENTS
Include retrieval where useful, at least one conceptual or reasoning item where appropriate, and enough variation to avoid mistaking familiarity with one format for secure understanding. Provide complete answers and interpretation guidance.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; questions; answer key; what each item assesses; response interpretation; likely next teaching action; optional extension.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Verify every calculation, unit, notation and answer. Ensure every item directly contributes evidence about the stated objective.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for a 5-, 10- or 15-minute exit ticket.

### Example Input
Year 4; objective: subtract four-digit numbers with regrouping; prerequisites: place value; 5 questions; mixed difficulty.

### Example Output
Combine straightforward calculations with one contrasting example and one explanation prompt so the teacher can distinguish procedural accuracy from understanding of regrouping.

## ADI-02 — Mathematics Hinge Question

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Hinge Questions, Diagnostic Assessment, Misconceptions

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[LIKELY_MISCONCEPTIONS]` | `[NUMBER_OF_OPTIONS]` | `[RESPONSE_METHOD]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics assessment specialist designing diagnostic hinge questions.

### CONTEXT
Create a hinge question for `[YEAR_GROUP]` on `[OBJECTIVE]`. Likely misconceptions are `[LIKELY_MISCONCEPTIONS]`; provide `[NUMBER_OF_OPTIONS]` response options using `[RESPONSE_METHOD]`.

### TASK
Design one high-information question that can rapidly distinguish secure understanding from specific plausible errors during teaching.

### REQUIREMENTS
Each distractor must correspond to a plausible mathematical reasoning path or misconception. Avoid options that can be eliminated through superficial test-taking cues. Explain what the teacher should do for each response pattern.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; options; correct answer; diagnostic rationale for each option; teacher response; follow-up question.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Test whether the options genuinely discriminate among the intended explanations and whether only one option is correct under the stated conditions.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for mini-whiteboards, oral response, multiple choice or paired discussion.

### Example Input
Year 5; objective: compare decimals; misconceptions: comparing digit strings rather than place value; 4 options; mini-whiteboards.

### Example Output
Use carefully chosen values where superficial digit-length reasoning conflicts with correct place-value comparison, then map each incorrect response to a specific diagnostic hypothesis.

## ADI-03 — Curriculum Objective Assessment

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Assessment Design, Curriculum Objectives, Marking

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[CURRICULUM_OBJECTIVE]` | `[COVERAGE_SCOPE]` | `[NUMBER_OF_ITEMS]` | `[DIFFICULTY_RANGE]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics assessment designer with expertise in curriculum alignment and fair assessment.

### CONTEXT
Build an assessment for `[YEAR_GROUP]` against `[CURRICULUM_OBJECTIVE]`, covering `[COVERAGE_SCOPE]` with `[NUMBER_OF_ITEMS]` items across `[DIFFICULTY_RANGE]`.

### TASK
Create a balanced assessment that samples the objective across representations, structures and levels of reasoning appropriate to the year group.

### REQUIREMENTS
Separate procedural fluency, conceptual understanding and reasoning evidence where appropriate. Avoid over-weighting one question type. Provide a transparent mark scheme and interpretation guidance without unsupported numerical thresholds.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Assessment blueprint; questions; mark scheme; answers; objective coverage; interpretation guidance; common error patterns; follow-up actions.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check mathematical accuracy, curriculum appropriateness, clarity, accessibility and fairness. Confirm that marks reward the intended mathematical evidence.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for formative assessment, end-of-unit assessment or moderation.

### Example Input
Year 6; objective: solve problems involving fractions; scope: comparison and four operations within curriculum expectations; 12 items; mixed difficulty.

### Example Output
Allocate items across comparison, representation, calculation and contextual reasoning, with enough evidence to distinguish a procedural gap from a conceptual one.

## ADI-04 — Analyse Pupil Work

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Assessment, Diagnosis, Pupil Work

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[PUPIL_WORK]` | `[RECENT_TEACHING]` | `[ASSESSMENT_CONTEXT]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics assessment and diagnostic specialist.

### CONTEXT
Analyse `[PUPIL_WORK]` from `[YEAR_GROUP]` against `[OBJECTIVE]`. Recent teaching is `[RECENT_TEACHING]`; assessment context is `[ASSESSMENT_CONTEXT]`.

### TASK
Identify observable strengths, errors and response patterns, then generate proportionate hypotheses and the smallest useful diagnostic or teaching next step.

### REQUIREMENTS
Separate observation from inference. Consider calculation, representation, language, operation selection, notation and prerequisite knowledge. Treat explanations of errors as hypotheses unless directly evidenced.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence observed; strengths; errors; patterns; plausible explanations; evidence supporting each; diagnostic check; next teaching step; follow-up assessment.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer fixed ability, effort or a stable misconception from insufficient evidence. Recommendations must be traceable to the supplied work.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual pupil conferencing, small-group analysis or whole-class review.

### Example Input
Year 4; objective: multiply two-digit numbers; pupil work: accurate one-digit facts but inconsistent partitioning; recent teaching: area model; assessment: independent work.

### Example Output
Distinguish fact recall from understanding of place-value partitioning, then use a contrasting diagnostic example before prescribing intervention.

## ADI-05 — Group Pupils for Targeted Mathematics Teaching

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Flexible Grouping, Assessment, Adaptive Teaching

### Editable Variables
`[PUPIL_RESULTS]` | `[OBJECTIVES]` | `[ERROR_PATTERNS]` | `[AVAILABLE_ADULT_SUPPORT]` | `[LESSON_DURATION]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher using assessment evidence to plan flexible instruction.

### CONTEXT
Use `[PUPIL_RESULTS]`, `[OBJECTIVES]` and `[ERROR_PATTERNS]` to form temporary groups within `[LESSON_DURATION]`, considering `[AVAILABLE_ADULT_SUPPORT]`.

### TASK
Create evidence-based, objective-specific teaching groups with a clear mathematical purpose and regrouping criterion.

### REQUIREMENTS
Do not label pupils by fixed ability. Group according to the current learning need evidenced by the data. Where evidence is ambiguous, flag it and recommend a quick diagnostic check rather than forcing a grouping decision.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Group; evidence; mathematical need; teaching focus; activity; teacher/adult role; success criterion; exit or regrouping criterion.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure every grouping decision is supported by supplied evidence and that groups remain flexible.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for whole-class lessons, intervention blocks or limited adult support.

### Example Input
Three pupils confuse numerator and denominator; four can calculate accurately but cannot explain; lesson duration: 25 minutes; one teaching adult.

### Example Output
Create two temporary groups with distinct teaching purposes rather than a broad “lower” group, and specify the evidence required for pupils to move between them.

## ADI-06 — Targeted Intervention Sequence

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Intervention, Diagnostic Teaching, Retrieval

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[IDENTIFIED_GAP]` | `[EVIDENCE]` | `[PREREQUISITES]` | `[NUMBER_OF_SESSIONS]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics intervention teacher.

### CONTEXT
Design an intervention for `[YEAR_GROUP]` addressing `[IDENTIFIED_GAP]`, based on `[EVIDENCE]`, with prerequisites `[PREREQUISITES]` over `[NUMBER_OF_SESSIONS]` sessions.

### TASK
Build a short, cumulative sequence that begins with prerequisite knowledge, teaches the identified concept explicitly, provides guided practice and tests transfer.

### REQUIREMENTS
Use diagnostic checks throughout. Include retrieval of prerequisite knowledge, representations where useful, gradually reduced scaffolding and a final independent transfer task. State what evidence would justify changing the plan.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Need and evidence; prerequisite check; session-by-session plan; representations; practice; diagnostic checks; success criterion; transfer assessment; review decision.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not design around an unverified diagnosis. Ensure each session directly addresses the identified mathematical need.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for 1:1, small-group or short daily intervention.

### Example Input
Year 3; gap: understand division as equal grouping; evidence: accurate sharing but difficulty interpreting division equations; prerequisites: equal groups; 4 sessions.

### Example Output
Move from concrete grouping to equations and word problems, checking at each stage whether the pupil can transfer the structure rather than simply repeat a demonstrated procedure.

## ADI-07 — Intervention Misconception Check

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Diagnostic Assessment, Misconceptions, Intervention

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[SUSPECTED_MISCONCEPTION]` | `[EVIDENCE]` | `[MATHEMATICAL_TOPIC]` | `[NUMBER_OF_CHECKS]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics diagnostic specialist.

### CONTEXT
Test the hypothesis `[SUSPECTED_MISCONCEPTION]` in `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils using `[NUMBER_OF_CHECKS]` diagnostic checks about `[MATHEMATICAL_TOPIC]`. Existing evidence is `[EVIDENCE]`.

### TASK
Design discriminating questions that distinguish the suspected misconception from plausible alternative explanations.

### REQUIREMENTS
Use contrasting examples and varied representations where useful. Explain what different responses would imply and what evidence would weaken the original hypothesis.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Hypothesis; diagnostic questions; expected responses; alternative explanations; interpretation guide; follow-up teaching.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Each check must add new evidence rather than merely repeat the original task.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual assessment, small-group intervention or teacher conferencing.

### Example Input
Year 5; suspected misconception: larger denominator means larger fraction; evidence: repeatedly chooses 1/8 over 1/6 as larger; topic: unit fractions; 4 checks.

### Example Output
Use fraction strips, number-line comparisons and symbolic contrasts to determine whether the pupil's issue is denominator magnitude, representation or language.

## ADI-08 — Adaptive Mathematics Task

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Adaptive Teaching, Inclusion, Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[ORIGINAL_TASK]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[ACCESS_BARRIER]` | `[EXISTING_SUPPORT]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### CONTEXT
Adapt `[ORIGINAL_TASK]` for `[YEAR_GROUP]` while preserving `[OBJECTIVE]`. The identified access barrier is `[ACCESS_BARRIER]`; existing support is `[EXISTING_SUPPORT]`.

### TASK
Modify access conditions without unnecessarily changing the mathematical construct being assessed or taught.

### REQUIREMENTS
Consider representation, language, layout, chunking, worked examples, manipulatives, response format and scaffold fading. Explicitly state what changes and what remains unchanged.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Original construct; barrier analysis; adapted task; support; preserved demand; independence route; evidence of learning; review point.

### Example Input
Year 5; objective: solve multi-step fraction problem; barrier: dense written instructions; support: visual vocabulary bank.

### Example Output
Simplify the presentation and separate essential information visually while retaining the same quantities, decisions and reasoning demands.

## ADI-09 — Retrieval and Re-Teach Planner

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Retrieval Practice, Re-Teaching, Retention

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[IDENTIFIED_GAP]` | `[PRIOR_LEARNING]` | `[LESSON_SEQUENCE]` | `[REVIEW_INTERVAL]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher designing cumulative retrieval and re-teaching.

### CONTEXT
Address `[IDENTIFIED_GAP]` for `[YEAR_GROUP]` using `[PRIOR_LEARNING]`, within `[LESSON_SEQUENCE]`, with later review at `[REVIEW_INTERVAL]`.

### TASK
Create a retrieval-to-reteach cycle that diagnoses prerequisite security, addresses the gap and checks whether learning is retained and transferred.

### REQUIREMENTS
Sequence retrieval from accessible prerequisite knowledge toward the target. Include explicit re-teaching, guided practice, independent application and delayed retrieval. Avoid using retrieval merely as repeated testing without instructional response.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Gap; prerequisite retrieval; interpretation; re-teaching point; guided practice; independent application; delayed retrieval; success criterion; decision rule.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure retrieval questions test relevant prerequisite knowledge and that later checks assess retention rather than exact memorisation of previous items.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for daily starters, weekly review or intervention cycles.

### Example Input
Year 4; gap: understanding equivalent fractions; prior learning: unit fractions; sequence: three lessons; review interval: one week.

### Example Output
Begin with partitioning and unit-fraction retrieval, explicitly reconnect equivalent representations, then revisit equivalence after a delay using different numerical examples.

## ADI-10 — Mathematics Assessment Summary for Teacher

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Assessment Analysis, Teaching Priorities, Data Interpretation

### Editable Variables
`[ASSESSMENT_DATA]` | `[OBJECTIVES]` | `[PUPIL_OR_GROUP_INFORMATION]` | `[RECENT_TEACHING]` | `[NEXT_LESSON_CONSTRAINTS]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics assessment lead.

### CONTEXT
Interpret `[ASSESSMENT_DATA]` against `[OBJECTIVES]`, considering `[PUPIL_OR_GROUP_INFORMATION]`, `[RECENT_TEACHING]` and `[NEXT_LESSON_CONSTRAINTS]`.

### TASK
Produce a concise teaching summary that distinguishes what the evidence demonstrates, what remains uncertain and what should happen next.

### REQUIREMENTS
Prioritise actionable mathematical information. Identify secure and developing understanding, recurring errors, evidence limitations and the smallest useful next teaching actions. Avoid unsupported ranking, ability labels or causal claims.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Secure understanding; developing understanding; common errors; evidence limitations; priority teaching actions; diagnostic follow-up; suggested review point.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every recommendation must be traceable to the supplied evidence. Flag insufficient data rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual pupils, teaching groups, year-group meetings or moderation discussions.

### Example Input
Assessment data show most Year 6 pupils can calculate percentages of 100 but struggle when the whole changes; recent teaching focused on benchmark percentages; next lesson has 45 minutes.

### Example Output
Prioritise proportional reasoning with non-100 wholes, while retaining benchmark retrieval and using a short hinge check to determine whether the issue is percentage meaning or calculation.
