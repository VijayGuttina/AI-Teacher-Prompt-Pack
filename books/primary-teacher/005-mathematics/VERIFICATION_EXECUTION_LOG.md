# Primary Mathematics Workflow Execution Log

**Execution date:** 25 August 2026

**AI tool/model:** ChatGPT / GPT-5.6 Luna

**Verification type:** In-model qualitative execution testing. This is not multi-platform/API verification.

**Reviewer:** In-model QA review

## Verification method

Each workflow was exercised against a representative primary-teacher input and its expected output behaviour was inspected for:

- mathematical correctness;
- curriculum appropriateness;
- alignment to the stated teacher job;
- completeness of the requested output structure;
- misconception/evidence discipline where relevant;
- preservation of mathematical demand during adaptation;
- ambiguity, terminology and formatting issues.

Generated mathematics and answer keys were independently checked during review. `PASS` means no blocking issue was identified in the representative execution. It does not mean every possible input has been tested.
