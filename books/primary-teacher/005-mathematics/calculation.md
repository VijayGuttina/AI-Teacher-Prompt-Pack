# Calculation and Fluency Workflows

## CF-01 — Calculation lesson sequence

**Purpose:** Plan explicit teaching and practice for a stated calculation objective.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; operation; number range; prior knowledge; duration.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher.

### TASK
Create a sequence from prerequisite retrieval through modelling, guided practice, reasoning and independent application.

### REQUIREMENTS
Make the chosen calculation strategy explicit and distinguish efficient methods from merely possible methods.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; prerequisites; model; guided practice; independent practice; reasoning; assessment; misconceptions.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Verify every calculation and answer.

---

## CF-02 — Calculation strategy comparison

**Purpose:** Compare methods for solving a calculation and identify when each is efficient.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; calculation type; examples; known methods.

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics specialist.

### TASK
Compare appropriate strategies, showing the mathematical reasoning behind each rather than simply listing procedures.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Method; worked example; reasoning; efficiency/use case; possible misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
All methods must be mathematically valid for the supplied examples.

---

## CF-03 — Fluency practice generator

**Purpose:** Create purposeful fluency practice without repetitive cosmetic variation.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; skill; number range; number of items; difficulty.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher.

### TASK
Generate a varied fluency set that develops accuracy, efficiency and flexibility.

### REQUIREMENTS
Include structural variation and avoid unnecessary complexity.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Practice set; answers; intended strategy; optional challenge.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check all calculations and ensure each item tests the stated skill.

---

## CF-04 — Arithmetic misconception diagnosis

**Purpose:** Diagnose patterns in calculation errors from pupil work.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; operation; pupil work; intended strategy.

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics assessment specialist.

### TASK
Analyse the supplied work, distinguish observations from hypotheses and design a diagnostic follow-up.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed pattern; possible explanation; evidence; diagnostic item; teaching response; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer a misconception without evidence.

---

## CF-05 — Mental calculation strategy builder

**Purpose:** Develop efficient mental strategies for a stated calculation type.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; operation; number range; known strategies.

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics fluency specialist.

### TASK
Create examples and teacher prompts that help pupils select and explain efficient mental strategies.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Strategy options; examples; teacher prompts; answers; common errors.

### QUALITY CHECKS

## CF-06 — Written method teaching sequence

**Purpose:** Teach a formal written calculation method while maintaining conceptual understanding.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; operation; number range; prerequisite methods.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher.

### TASK
Create an explicit teaching sequence linking the written method to the underlying place-value or multiplicative/additive structure.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Prerequisite; representation; teacher model; guided practice; independent practice; reasoning check; misconceptions.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check place-value alignment and every worked example.

---

## CF-07 — Calculation reasoning problems

**Purpose:** Apply calculation knowledge in reasoning contexts.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; operation; number range; context; number of problems.

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics reasoning specialist.

### TASK
Create problems that require pupils to choose, explain or check a calculation rather than mechanically execute a named operation.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Problems; expected reasoning; answers; common misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Contexts must be mathematically coherent and not introduce unnecessary reading demand.

---

## CF-08 — Calculation error analysis

**Purpose:** Turn incorrect calculations into useful teaching material.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; correct calculation; pupil error; known method.

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics teacher.

### TASK
Analyse the error and create a short teaching response that uses the incorrect example productively.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Error; likely issue; diagnostic question; correction; similar-but-different practice; answer.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not treat one isolated error as proof of a stable misconception.

---

## CF-09 — Adapt calculation practice

**Purpose:** Adapt a calculation task for access needs while retaining mathematical intent.

**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; objective; access barrier; support available.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt presentation, scaffolding or quantity without unnecessarily lowering the mathematical demand.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; adaptation; teacher guidance; independent version.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The adaptation must preserve the intended calculation or reasoning objective.

---

## CF-10 — Fluency assessment and next steps

**Purpose:** Create a short assessment that distinguishes accuracy, efficiency and strategy selection.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; skill; number range; number of items.

### ROLE
Act as a primary mathematics assessment specialist.

### TASK
Create a compact check with an interpretation guide and targeted next steps.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Assessment; answer key; interpretation; next teaching action; extension.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check every answer and ensure interpretation is supported by the evidence collected.
