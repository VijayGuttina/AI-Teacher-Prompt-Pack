## GM-01 — Geometry Concept Lesson

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 700–1,000 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Geometry, Shape, Position, Lesson Planning

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[SHAPES_OR_CONCEPTS]` | `[PREREQUISITES]` | `[DURATION]` | `[REPRESENTATIONS]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary mathematics teacher and geometry specialist.

### CONTEXT
Teach `[OBJECTIVE]` to `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils, building from `[PREREQUISITES]` within `[DURATION]`. Use `[REPRESENTATIONS]` where they expose the mathematical structure.

### TASK
Create a conceptually clear lesson progressing from prior knowledge and precise vocabulary through modelling, classification or construction, reasoning and independent assessment.

### REQUIREMENTS

### OUTPUT FORMAT

### QUALITY CHECKS
Verify every geometric statement, classification, measurement and diagram description.

### Example Output
Present triangles in varied orientations and sizes, classify them by defining properties, and ask pupils to explain why a visually unusual triangle still belongs to the same class.

### CONTEXT
Create work for `[YEAR_GROUP]` using `[SHAPES]`, `[SIDE_LENGTHS]`, `[UNITS]` and `[DIFFICULTY]`.

### ROLE

### CONTEXT

### TASK

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary teacher specialising in adaptive mathematics teaching.

### CONTEXT
The original task is `[ORIGINAL_TASK]` for `[YEAR_GROUP]`. The mathematical objective is `[MATHEMATICAL_OBJECTIVE]`; the access barrier is `[ACCESS_BARRIER]`; existing support is `[EXISTING_SUPPORT]`.

### TASK
Adapt visual clarity, language, representation, recording demands or scaffolding while preserving the mathematical objective and core reasoning demand.

### REQUIREMENTS

### OUTPUT FORMAT

### QUALITY CHECKS

### Example Input

### Example Output
