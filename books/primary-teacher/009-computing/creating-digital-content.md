# Creating Digital Content Workflows

**IDs:** DC-01 to DC-10

## DC-01 — Plan Digital Content
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Purpose, audience and format supplied. **TASK:** Plan a digital product. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define content, audience and success criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Content plan and checklist. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Purpose alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Presentation/video/document.

## DC-02 — Storyboard a Digital Product
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product and audience supplied. **TASK:** Create a storyboard. **REQUIREMENTS:** Sequence content and identify media needed without inventing assets. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Scene/page table. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Video/slideshow.

## DC-03 — Teach Responsible Media Selection
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Content task supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils choose appropriate digital media. **REQUIREMENTS:** Address relevance, accessibility and rights/attribution where applicable. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Decision checklist. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practicality. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Image/audio/video.

## DC-04 — Improve a Digital Presentation
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Draft supplied. **TASK:** Improve clarity, structure and accessibility. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve pupil intent and distinguish design advice from factual corrections. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Specific revisions and rationale. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Audience fit. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Slides.

## DC-05 — Teach Formatting and Layout
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Tool and task supplied. **TASK:** Create an age-appropriate formatting lesson. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not assume unavailable interface features. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Teacher sequence and pupil task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Tool fidelity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Word processor/presentation.

## DC-06 — Diagnose Digital-Content Misconceptions
**ROLE:** Diagnostic computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil product and explanation supplied. **TASK:** Diagnose the underlying difficulty. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish content, audience, structure and tool-use issues. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group.

## DC-07 — Evaluate a Digital Product
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product and success criteria supplied. **TASK:** Evaluate against criteria. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid subjective judgements presented as facts. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria table and next steps. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criterion alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil self-assessment.

## DC-08 — Adapt Digital-Creation Tasks
**ROLE:** Inclusive computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access while preserving digital-creation decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Offer scaffolds without removing purposeful choice. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adapted task, scaffold, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## DC-09 — Design a Digital Publishing Workflow
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product and tools supplied. **TASK:** Sequence creation, review and publishing steps. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include file management, audience and safety checks. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Workflow checklist. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School platform.

## DC-10 — Retrieval and Re-teach Digital Creation
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Skill gap supplied. **TASK:** Create a short retrieval-to-reteach cycle. **REQUIREMENTS:** Target the diagnosed skill and include an independent check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, modelling, practice, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify tool.
