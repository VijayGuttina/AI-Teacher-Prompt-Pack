# Online Safety & Responsible Use Workflows

**IDs:** OS-01 to OS-10

## OS-01 — Teach Personal Information Safety
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and scenario supplied. **TASK:** Teach pupils what information should be protected and why. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use age-appropriate examples and avoid requesting real personal information. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Lesson sequence and check questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safety and accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## OS-02 — Teach Strong Password Practice
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Age group supplied. **TASK:** Explain strong authentication habits. **REQUIREMENTS:** Never request actual passwords; distinguish password advice from account-specific instructions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation and practice activity. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Security accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School policy.

## OS-03 — Recognise Phishing and Scams
**ROLE:** Online-safety teacher. **CONTEXT:** Example message supplied. **TASK:** Identify warning signs. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not expose pupils to live malicious links; use fictional or safely reproduced examples. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Red-flag checklist and reasoning. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safety. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Email/message examples.

## OS-04 — Teach Safe Searching
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Topic and age group supplied. **TASK:** Teach safer search behaviour. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid unnecessary exposure to unsuitable search results and explain reporting/blocking routes appropriate to the supplied environment. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Routine and practice scenarios. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Age appropriateness. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School search environment.

## OS-05 — Evaluate Online Content
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Content examples supplied. **TASK:** Teach pupils to question reliability, purpose and provenance. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate factual verification from opinion. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evaluation checklist and examples. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Critical reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** News/social media.

## OS-06 — Teach Respectful Digital Communication
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Scenario supplied. **TASK:** Develop responsible communication habits. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid reproducing harmful personal content; focus on behaviour and reporting. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Scenario cards and response pathways. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safeguarding suitability. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Class agreement.

## OS-07 — Respond to an Online-Safety Scenario
**ROLE:** Computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Fictional scenario supplied. **TASK:** Generate an age-appropriate response pathway. **REQUIREMENTS:** Prioritise safety, trusted-adult support and appropriate reporting; do not give unsafe investigative instructions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Immediate actions, who to tell, follow-up learning. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safeguarding. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** School procedures.

## OS-08 — Diagnose Online-Safety Misconceptions
**ROLE:** Diagnostic computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil response supplied. **TASK:** Identify misunderstanding about risk or responsible behaviour. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not infer personal circumstances from a single answer. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, diagnosis, check, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safeguarding and validity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Year group.

## OS-09 — Adapt Online-Safety Teaching
**ROLE:** Inclusive computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original task and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access without reducing essential safety knowledge. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve decision-making and help-seeking behaviours. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation, scaffold, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct preservation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## OS-10 — Build a Retrieval and Re-teach Cycle
**ROLE:** Primary computing teacher. **CONTEXT:** Online-safety concept and pupil errors supplied. **TASK:** Create a retrieval-to-reteach cycle. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use safe fictional scenarios and include an exit check. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, reteach, scenario practice, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safety and alignment. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify duration.
