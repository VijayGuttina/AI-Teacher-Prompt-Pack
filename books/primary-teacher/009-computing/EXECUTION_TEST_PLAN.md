**Subject:** Primary Computing  
**Workflow count:** 100  
**Planned representative tests:** 20

## Purpose

Test representative Computing workflows before subject freeze. The suite is designed to expose systemic defects rather than claim that every workflow has been individually executed.

## Pass criteria

A representative execution passes when it is:

1. technically accurate;
2. aligned to the stated Computing objective;
3. appropriate for the stated primary age group;
4. explicit about assumptions where environment or software matters;
5. free of fabricated execution claims, data, sources or interface features;
6. safe with respect to privacy and online behaviour;
7. evidence-based when diagnosing pupils;
8. accessible without unnecessarily reducing computational demand;
9. usable by a teacher without substantial repair;
10. clear about uncertainty where technology or product details may change.

## Recording requirement

Each execution record should capture: test ID, workflow ID, model/model family, test date, exact representative input, result summary, pass/fail decision, reviewer notes, remediation and re-test status.

## Verification rule

Representative tests establish confidence in workflow families but do not automatically convert untested workflows into individually verified workflows.
