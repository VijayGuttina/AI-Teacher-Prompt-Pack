## Scope notes

The product is UK-primary focused and defaults to the National Curriculum for England. RE, PSHE/RSHE, MFL and EYFS are included because they are common primary-school teaching needs even though they do not map identically to the statutory National Curriculum subject list.

PSHE/RSHE content must be handled as a curriculum-sensitive module and should not assume a single national scheme. Where statutory or school-policy requirements vary, prompts should require the teacher to specify their framework or school policy.

RE should support different local agreed syllabuses and faith/community contexts rather than presenting one universal syllabus.

MFL should allow the teacher to specify the language and school scheme.

The taxonomy is deliberately broader than a simple subject list because teachers buy solutions to teaching jobs, not only subject labels.

## Generation principle

Do not create hundreds of prompts for every module by default. Establish the module framework first, identify the highest-value teaching workflows, then create a curated prompt set. Use variables and reusable frameworks to cover legitimate variations without duplicating near-identical prompts.
