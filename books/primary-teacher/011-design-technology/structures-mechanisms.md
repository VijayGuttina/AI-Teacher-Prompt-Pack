# Structures, Mechanisms & Mechanical Systems Workflows

**IDs:** SM-01 to SM-10

## SM-01 — Teach Structure Strength
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and structure task supplied. **TASK:** Explain how shape, joining and material choice can affect strength. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use age-appropriate technical language and practical examples. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, demonstration, investigation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technical claims are defensible. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SM-02 — Design a Frame Structure
**ROLE:** Design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product brief, materials and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Create a frame-structure design task. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include stability, joining and testing. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, criteria, design prompts, test plan. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construction is realistic. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Paper/card/timber.

## SM-03 — Investigate Levers
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and available materials supplied. **TASK:** Plan an investigation of levers and mechanical advantage at an appropriate level. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define pivot, load and effort accurately. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Practical investigation and recording table. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Terminology and method are accurate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SM-04 — Design a Lever Mechanism
**ROLE:** DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Intended moving product supplied. **TASK:** Guide pupils through designing and testing a lever mechanism. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify movement requirement and connection points. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Design steps, criteria, testing questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Mechanism is plausible. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Moving picture/toy.

## SM-05 — Teach Linkages
**ROLE:** Primary design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group and linkage materials supplied. **TASK:** Explain and demonstrate a simple linkage. **REQUIREMENTS:** Make component movement explicit and avoid unexplained technical jargon. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Teacher explanation, demonstration, pupil task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Movement description is accurate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Simple/complex linkage.

## SM-06 — Design a Linkage Product
**ROLE:** DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief and materials supplied. **TASK:** Create a linkage design-and-make activity. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include prototype, testing and refinement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, construction sequence, tests. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Joining and movement are feasible. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## SM-07 — Investigate Wheels and Axles
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Vehicle/product challenge supplied. **TASK:** Plan a practical investigation of wheels and axles. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify axle alignment, wheel movement and relevant constraints. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Investigation, variables, recording table. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical setup is realistic. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Cardboard construction.

## SM-08 — Design a Moving Vehicle
**ROLE:** Design teacher. **CONTEXT:** User, purpose, materials and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Guide pupils to design and test a wheeled product. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include rolling performance, stability and user criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Specification, ideas, prototype and test plan. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria are testable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SM-09 — Diagnose Mechanism Failure
**ROLE:** DT assessment specialist. **CONTEXT:** Pupil product behaviour and construction evidence supplied. **TASK:** Identify likely mechanical causes and propose discriminating tests. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observed behaviour from hypotheses. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, tests, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unsupported diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Lever/linkage/wheel.

## SM-10 — Evaluate Mechanical Performance
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, criteria and test results supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils evaluate performance and identify improvements. **REQUIREMENTS:** Compare results with criteria rather than appearance alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evaluation table and improvement priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Claims reference evidence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-friendly language.
