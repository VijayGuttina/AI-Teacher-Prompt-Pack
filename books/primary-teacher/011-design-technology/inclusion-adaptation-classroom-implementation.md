# Inclusion, Adaptation & Classroom Implementation

## IA-01 - Adapt a DT Task Without Changing the Core Objective
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Design & Technology teacher specialising in inclusive curriculum design.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil needs support to access [DT TASK] while the intended learning objective remains [CORE OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Adapt the task for [YEAR GROUP] without reducing the essential DT learning.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify the barrier; preserve the core knowledge or skill; adapt instructions, sequencing, resources, tools, recording, or support as appropriate; distinguish access support from changing the learning objective; maintain pupil agency and practical challenge.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide barrier analysis, adaptations, what remains unchanged, teacher prompts, pupil resources, and an independence check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Adaptations must remove an access barrier without unnecessarily lowering the intended DT learning demand.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [SPECIFIC NEED], [YEAR GROUP], [TASK], or [AVAILABLE RESOURCES].

## IA-02 - Scaffold Practical Instructions
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher who designs clear, accessible practical instructions.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are finding the making sequence for [PRODUCT] difficult to follow independently.
**TASK:** Create a scaffolded instruction sequence for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Break the process into manageable stages; use concise action language; include visual or verbal cues where useful; identify check points; avoid excessive prompting; include safety information at the relevant stage; provide a route toward independent working.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide teacher demonstration sequence, pupil step cards, check points, safety prompts, and gradual-release support plan.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Instructions must remain technically accurate and must not remove essential thinking or decision-making from pupils.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [PRODUCT], [YEAR GROUP], [TOOLS], [MATERIALS], or [SUPPORT REQUIREMENT].

## IA-03 - Tool and Equipment Adaptation
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher experienced in safe, inclusive practical work.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil needs an adaptation to access [TOOL OR EQUIPMENT] safely while completing [DT TASK].
**TASK:** Identify practical adaptations that preserve the intended skill or learning as far as reasonably possible.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify the specific barrier; consider alternative tools, grips, positioning, stabilisation, shared equipment, pre-preparation, or adult support where appropriate; assess safety; identify what learning may be affected by each adaptation.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide barrier, adaptation options, benefits, limitations, safety considerations, supervision requirements, and teacher decision guide.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Never recommend an adaptation that introduces an unsafe use of a tool or creates a misleading assessment of the intended skill.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [TOOL], [TASK], [YEAR GROUP], or [SPECIFIC ACCESS REQUIREMENT].

## IA-04 - Differentiate by Support, Not by Expectation
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in high-expectation inclusive teaching.
**CONTEXT:** A mixed-attainment class is completing [DT TASK] and requires differentiated support.
**TASK:** Create a differentiation plan that keeps the core objective common while varying support and independence.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the shared learning objective; provide scaffold, guided, independent, and extension support; identify prompts that can be removed over time; avoid creating unnecessary lower-level versions of the task; maintain meaningful pupil choice.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide shared objective, support levels, teacher prompts, resource adaptations, independence pathway, and extension options.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Differentiation must increase access and appropriate challenge without automatically equating support level with ability.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [TASK], [CLASS PROFILE], or [SPECIFIC BARRIERS].

## IA-05 - Accessible Recording and Evaluation
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher who designs accessible ways for pupils to communicate understanding.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to record design decisions and evaluation for [PRODUCT], but written recording may create an unnecessary barrier.
**TASK:** Create alternative recording routes while preserving the assessment objective.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Consider written, oral, annotated drawing, photograph with commentary, checklist, structured table, audio, or teacher-scribed responses where appropriate; identify what evidence each route captures; preserve technical vocabulary and reasoning requirements.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide 3 to 5 recording options, evidence captured, accessibility benefits, limitations, and assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Alternative formats must assess the same intended understanding where equivalence is claimed.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [ASSESSMENT OBJECTIVE], [PRODUCT], or [PUPIL NEED].

## IA-06 - Group Work and Pupil Roles
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in inclusive collaborative practical learning.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are working in groups on [DT PROJECT] and need roles that support genuine participation.
**TASK:** Design a collaborative working structure for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Create meaningful roles linked to design, materials, making, testing, recording, and evaluation as relevant; prevent one pupil from completing the practical work for others; rotate roles where appropriate; preserve individual accountability and decision-making.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide role cards, group workflow, rotation plan, individual accountability method, teacher observation points, and reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Every role must contribute to the design process and pupils must retain opportunities to demonstrate the intended learning themselves.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [GROUP SIZE], [PROJECT], or [CLASSROOM CONSTRAINTS].

## IA-07 - Classroom Resource and Station Setup
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher and practical classroom organiser.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to organise resources for [DT LESSON] so pupils can work safely and efficiently.
**TASK:** Create a station-based classroom setup for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Plan materials, tools, work areas, demonstrations, testing areas, waste, storage, and movement; reduce unnecessary waiting; make resource limits clear; include safety controls and accessibility considerations; define pupil responsibilities for collection and return.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide room zones, station contents, pupil flow, safety controls, resource quantities, teacher preparation checklist, and reset procedure.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The setup must be realistic for the room, class size, equipment, and lesson duration.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [ROOM], [CLASS SIZE], [PROJECT], [TOOLS], or [LESSON LENGTH].

## IA-08 - Supporting Independence During Making
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher who develops pupil independence through structured support.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils working on [PRODUCT] frequently ask the teacher for help with decisions or practical problems.
**TASK:** Create an independence routine that helps pupils solve manageable problems before requesting direct teacher intervention.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Establish a help-seeking sequence; provide self-check questions; include peer consultation rules; define when teacher intervention is required; use prompts that encourage diagnosis and reasoning; ensure safety issues bypass the normal help sequence.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide pupil help routine, self-check card, teacher prompt hierarchy, safety exceptions, and independence assessment criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The routine must increase productive independence without encouraging pupils to attempt unsafe procedures or unsuitable repairs.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [PROJECT], [COMMON PROBLEMS], or [CLASSROOM ROUTINES].

## IA-09 - Inclusive Practical Assessment
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in fair and inclusive assessment.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils with different access needs are being assessed on [DT OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Create an inclusive assessment approach that distinguishes the learning objective from barriers caused by the assessment format.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify the construct being assessed; identify irrelevant access barriers; provide equivalent response routes where appropriate; retain essential technical and practical criteria; define reasonable adaptations; state when an adaptation changes what can validly be concluded.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide construct definition, access barriers, permitted adaptations, assessment criteria, evidence routes, and validity cautions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not claim equivalent assessment where an adaptation materially changes the skill or knowledge being measured.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [OBJECTIVE], [YEAR GROUP], [ASSESSMENT METHOD], or [SPECIFIC ACCESS NEED].

## IA-10 - Full DT Lesson Implementation Plan
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Design & Technology teacher responsible for delivering a safe, inclusive, practical lesson.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to deliver [DT LESSON] to [YEAR GROUP] using [MATERIALS AND TOOLS].
**TASK:** Produce a complete classroom implementation plan from preparation through evaluation.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include learning objective and success criteria; prior knowledge; teacher modelling; resources; room setup; safety briefing; practical sequence; pupil decision points; differentiation and accessibility; formative assessment; troubleshooting; cleanup; evaluation; extension; and contingency planning for limited resources or time.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide a timed lesson plan, teacher preparation checklist, pupil instructions, safety controls, differentiation table, assessment checkpoints, contingency options, and end-of-lesson review.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Ensure the lesson is technically accurate, age-appropriate, feasible, inclusive, safe, and aligned with the DT design-make-evaluate process.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [LESSON LENGTH], [CLASS SIZE], [PROJECT], [TOOLS], [MATERIALS], or [SPECIFIC ACCESS REQUIREMENTS].
