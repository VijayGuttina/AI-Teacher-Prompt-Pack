# Design & Technology Enquiry & Curriculum Planning Workflows

**IDs:** DE-01 to DE-10

## DE-01 — Plan a Design & Technology Unit
**ROLE:** Primary Design & Technology teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group, theme, curriculum intent and available lessons supplied. **TASK:** Create a coherent unit linking research, designing, making, testing and evaluation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify knowledge, technical skills and design decisions separately; preserve meaningful practical work. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Unit overview, lesson sequence, resources, assessment opportunities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear design purpose; realistic timing; safe implementation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DE-02 — Write a Design Brief
**ROLE:** Primary design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product purpose, user and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Write an age-appropriate design brief. **REQUIREMENTS:** State user, need, purpose and relevant constraints without prescribing one solution. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief plus teacher notes. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Genuine design problem; pupil agency retained. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/pair/group brief.

## DE-03 — Define Technical Objectives
**ROLE:** Design & Technology curriculum planner. **CONTEXT:** Intended product and year group supplied. **TASK:** Define the technical knowledge and making skills pupils need. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate knowledge from process skill and design judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective table with evidence of success. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Objectives are teachable and assessable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Knowledge-heavy/practical-heavy.

## DE-04 — Sequence a Design Process
**ROLE:** Primary Design & Technology teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product and lesson count supplied. **TASK:** Sequence research, specification, ideas, planning, making, testing and improvement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Allocate realistic time to practical stages and evaluation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Lesson sequence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Logical dependency between stages. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 3/6/8 lessons.

## DE-05 — Plan Progression Across Year Groups
**ROLE:** Design & Technology subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Existing units across year groups supplied. **TASK:** Map progression in design thinking, technical knowledge and making skills. **REQUIREMENTS:** Identify repetition, gaps and increasing independence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Progression matrix. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Progression is evidenced rather than assumed. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DE-06 — Plan Resource Preparation
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Class size, product, materials, tools and room constraints supplied. **TASK:** Create a preparation and distribution plan. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include quantities, setup, storage, cleanup and safety. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Resource checklist and lesson setup plan. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Budget/class size.

## DE-07 — Audit a DT Unit
**ROLE:** Design & Technology curriculum reviewer. **CONTEXT:** Existing unit supplied. **TASK:** Identify strengths, gaps and priority improvements. **REQUIREMENTS:** Review design intent, technical knowledge, making, testing and evaluation separately. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Audit table with priority actions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-led recommendations. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Light-touch/full review.

## DE-08 — Adapt a DT Unit
**ROLE:** Inclusive Design & Technology teacher. **CONTEXT:** Original unit and learner need supplied. **TASK:** Improve access while preserving the intended design and technical construct. **REQUIREMENTS:** State what changes, what remains constant and how independence is supported. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Adaptation plan, scaffold, independence route, stretch. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unnecessary reduction in cognitive or design demand. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## DE-09 — Build Retrieval into Practical Work
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prior technical knowledge and new practical task supplied. **TASK:** Create a short retrieval-to-application sequence. **REQUIREMENTS:** Retrieval must prepare pupils for a genuine design or making decision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval questions, discussion, application task. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear transfer to practical work. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 5/10 minutes.

## DE-10 — Plan a Design Challenge
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group, problem, constraints and available resources supplied. **TASK:** Create an open-ended design challenge with meaningful constraints. **REQUIREMENTS:** Permit multiple viable solutions and require justification. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Challenge brief, criteria, process and reflection questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Appropriate challenge and genuine design decision-making. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/team challenge.
