# Materials, Textiles & Construction

## MT-01 - Select Materials for a Purpose
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and materials specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to choose materials for a product based on its intended purpose.
**TASK:** Help pupils compare the supplied materials and justify an appropriate choice.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Base choices on relevant properties and product requirements; avoid unsupported claims; distinguish observable properties from assumptions; include user and sustainability considerations where relevant.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Material/property table, design requirements, justified recommendation, pupil questions, misconception check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, age appropriate, practical, and linked directly to product purpose.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, product, available materials, and taught properties.

## MT-02 - Investigate Material Properties
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher planning a practical materials investigation.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to investigate properties of materials before making design decisions.
**TASK:** Create a simple investigation that produces useful evidence about the supplied materials.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the property being investigated; use a fair and manageable method; distinguish observation from conclusion; include appropriate safety guidance.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Investigation question, prediction, method, results table, conclusion prompts, design application.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable, age appropriate, safe, and technically accurate.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group, materials, property, equipment, and lesson duration.

## MT-03 - Choose Textiles and Construction Methods
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher specialising in textiles and construction.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are designing a textile or fabric-based product.
**TASK:** Help pupils select suitable textile materials and construction methods for the intended function.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Link material and method choices to strength, appearance, durability, flexibility, joining, and user needs where relevant; use only methods pupils are expected to know or can safely learn.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Design requirements, material/method comparison, recommendation, construction considerations, evaluation questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Technically sound, feasible, age appropriate, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, product, textiles, tools, and taught techniques.

## MT-04 - Explore Joining and Fixing Methods
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and construction specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to decide how two or more materials should be joined in a product.
**TASK:** Compare appropriate joining methods and guide pupils towards a justified choice.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Explain what each method achieves; consider material compatibility, strength, appearance, durability, reversibility, and safety where relevant; do not recommend equipment outside the stated classroom provision.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Joining-method table, decision criteria, worked example, practical challenge, safety checks.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, feasible, curriculum appropriate, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide materials, tools, product, year group, and available joining techniques.

## MT-05 - Plan a Material Cutting and Construction Sequence
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher supporting pupils before practical making.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have a design requiring several material preparation and construction stages.
**TASK:** Turn the design into a clear, manageable making sequence.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Put stages in a sensible order; identify measuring, marking, cutting, joining, and checking points where relevant; include tool and material assumptions; make safety explicit without overwhelming the design learning.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Numbered making sequence, equipment list, quality checkpoints, safety reminders, pupil self-check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible, logically sequenced, safe, and suitable for the stated age group.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify product, materials, tools, year group, and lesson time.

## MT-06 - Investigate Strength, Stability and Durability
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher using practical evidence to develop material understanding.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to understand how material or construction choices affect a product's performance.
**TASK:** Design a practical investigation into strength, stability, or durability that is appropriate to the supplied product context.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the performance criterion; keep the comparison fair; record evidence consistently; avoid implying that one material is universally strongest or best.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Investigation question, variables or comparison conditions, method, results table, conclusion, design implication.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence based, technically accurate, manageable, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group, materials, construction method, product, and equipment.

## MT-07 - Design with Material Constraints
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher helping pupils design within realistic constraints.
**CONTEXT:** A product must be designed using a restricted set of materials, dimensions, budget, or classroom resources.
**TASK:** Generate a design challenge and planning support that makes the constraints meaningful rather than arbitrary.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Keep the user need central; distinguish essential from desirable requirements; encourage creative problem solving; ensure the proposed solution is feasible with the stated materials.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Brief, constraints, specification, idea-generation prompts, selection criteria, reflection questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Purposeful, achievable, creative, inclusive, and technically realistic.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide product, user, material limits, budget, dimensions, and year group.

## MT-08 - Evaluate Material Choices in a Finished Product
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and product evaluator.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have completed a product and need to evaluate whether their material and construction choices worked.
**TASK:** Guide an evidence-based evaluation of material selection and construction.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Link evaluation to the original specification and user need; require observable evidence; identify strengths, limitations, and specific improvements; avoid purely aesthetic judgement unless appearance is part of the specification.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Specification review table, evidence prompts, strengths, improvements, final evaluation statement.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence led, specific, age appropriate, and connected to design intent.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide product, original specification, materials, test results, and year group.

## MT-09 - Assess Materials and Construction Understanding
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** Assess pupils' understanding of material properties, selection, joining, construction, and evaluation.
**TASK:** Create an assessment that tests both technical knowledge and design application.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include recall, explanation, comparison, application, and design-reasoning questions; avoid questions that depend only on memorised vocabulary; provide answers or indicative responses and misconception mapping.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Assessment, mark guidance, expected responses, misconception map, intervention suggestions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Valid, technically accurate, age appropriate, and aligned to taught content.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, materials, construction techniques, assessment duration, and difficulty.

## MT-10 - Adapt Materials and Construction Tasks for Inclusion
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and inclusive-practice specialist.
**CONTEXT:** A materials or construction activity must remain accessible to pupils with different physical, sensory, language, or processing needs.
**TASK:** Provide adaptations that preserve the intended DT learning while reducing unnecessary access barriers.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Separate access support from changes to the learning objective; consider tools, materials, grip, workspace, instructions, sequencing, communication, peer support, and recording; retain meaningful pupil choice and evaluation.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Learning demand, barriers, adaptation table, teacher prompts, pupil alternatives, assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Inclusive, practical, safe, curriculum aligned, and construct preserving.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide year group, activity, identified barriers, equipment, and intended learning outcomes.
