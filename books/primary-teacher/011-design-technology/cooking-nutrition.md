# Cooking & Nutrition

## CN-01 - Explain the Purpose of a Healthy Diet
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and food education specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are learning how food choices contribute to a healthy and varied diet.
**TASK:** Explain the key idea of a healthy and varied diet at the stated age level and connect it to food design.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Use accurate, age-appropriate nutrition language; avoid medical claims; distinguish broad dietary guidance from individual dietary advice; connect choices to the product brief where relevant.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Learning intention, explanation, food examples, pupil questions, misconception check, design application.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, age appropriate, inclusive, curriculum aligned, and suitable for classroom use.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, curriculum content, food context, and vocabulary.

## CN-02 - Identify Food Preparation Techniques
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and food-preparation specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to understand and apply food preparation techniques appropriate to their age and classroom provision.
**TASK:** Explain the supplied techniques and their purpose in a practical food task.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Give clear sequential guidance; identify appropriate tools; include relevant hygiene and safety precautions; do not introduce techniques beyond the stated equipment or age level.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Technique table, step-by-step guidance, tool list, safety and hygiene checks, pupil self-check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, practical, safe, and accessible.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide year group, techniques, ingredients, tools, and lesson duration.

## CN-03 - Plan a Food Product
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher supporting pupils through food product design.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have a user and purpose for a food product and need to turn it into a practical design.
**TASK:** Develop a food product brief and specification that pupils can use to plan and make their product.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include user, purpose, ingredients, sensory expectations, nutritional considerations where appropriate, constraints, preparation methods, and evaluation criteria; account for allergies and dietary requirements only as supplied by the teacher.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Design brief, specification, ingredient plan, preparation sequence, testing criteria, evaluation prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible, age appropriate, safe, inclusive, and linked to user need.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, user, purpose, ingredients, equipment, constraints, and dietary requirements.

## CN-04 - Compare Ingredients by Function
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and food materials specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to understand why different ingredients may be selected in a food product.
**TASK:** Compare the supplied ingredients by their relevant properties and role in the product.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Focus on observable or well-established food properties; distinguish function from preference; avoid unsupported nutritional or allergen claims; link choices to the product specification.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Ingredient comparison table, function explanations, substitution considerations, design decision questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, purposeful, age appropriate, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide product, ingredients, year group, and taught food vocabulary.

## CN-05 - Teach Food Hygiene and Safety
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher responsible for safe food preparation.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are preparing food in a classroom or school kitchen environment.
**TASK:** Create a clear age-appropriate hygiene and safety routine for the stated activity.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Cover relevant personal hygiene, workspace hygiene, safe handling, tool use, storage, and cleaning; distinguish general principles from school-specific procedures; never assume an allergen-control process that has not been supplied.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before, during, and after checklist; teacher safety briefing; pupil self-check; key warning points.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical, clear, proportionate, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide year group, ingredients, tools, cooking method, and school safety rules.

## CN-06 - Adapt a Recipe to Meet a Specification
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and recipe-development specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to modify a supplied recipe so it better meets a stated user need or product specification.
**TASK:** Identify sensible modifications and explain the design reasoning behind them.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve the basic feasibility of the recipe; identify effects on ingredients, method, texture, flavour, appearance, or other relevant criteria; do not make unsupported claims about nutritional or dietary suitability.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Original specification, proposed changes table, reasoning, revised method, testing criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible, technically plausible, age appropriate, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Supply the recipe, specification, user, available ingredients, and equipment.

## CN-07 - Evaluate a Food Product
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and food product evaluator.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have made a food product and need to judge how well it meets its specification.
**TASK:** Create an evidence-based evaluation of the finished product.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Use sensory and functional criteria appropriate to the product; compare results with the specification; distinguish observation from opinion; identify specific improvements.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Specification review table, sensory test prompts, evidence summary, strengths, improvements, final evaluation.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence based, age appropriate, respectful, safe, and linked to design intent.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide product, specification, test results, and year group.

## CN-08 - Investigate Taste, Texture and Appearance
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher designing a controlled sensory investigation.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to compare food products or variations using agreed sensory criteria.
**TASK:** Design a fair classroom investigation that produces useful evidence for product development.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define criteria before testing; keep comparisons consistent; use age-appropriate recording; respect allergies, dietary restrictions, sensory differences, and pupil choice; do not pressure pupils to taste food.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Investigation question, criteria, method, recording sheet, analysis prompts, design implication.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Fair, inclusive, practical, safe, and suitable for the stated age group.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify products, sensory criteria, year group, and testing conditions.

## CN-09 - Assess Cooking and Nutrition Understanding
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** Assess pupils' understanding of nutrition, ingredients, preparation methods, hygiene, safety, and food product design.
**TASK:** Create an assessment that measures both technical knowledge and practical design reasoning.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include recall, explanation, application, sequencing, evaluation, and design decisions; avoid assessing pupils solely on memorised terminology; provide answers or indicative responses and misconception mapping.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Assessment, mark guidance, expected responses, misconception map, intervention suggestions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Valid, technically accurate, age appropriate, safe, and aligned to taught content.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, content, duration, assessment format, and difficulty.

## CN-10 - Adapt Cooking Tasks for Inclusion
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and inclusive-practice specialist.
**CONTEXT:** A cooking or nutrition activity needs adaptations for pupils with different physical, sensory, language, cultural, processing, or access needs.
**TASK:** Provide adaptations that preserve the intended food technology and design learning.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Separate access adaptations from changes to the learning objective; consider tools, grip, workspace, instructions, sequencing, communication, ingredients, sensory demands, and recording; follow supplied school food safety and allergy procedures.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Learning demand, barriers, adaptation table, teacher prompts, pupil alternatives, assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Inclusive, practical, safe, culturally respectful, and construct preserving.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide year group, activity, identified barriers, equipment, ingredients, and intended learning outcomes.
