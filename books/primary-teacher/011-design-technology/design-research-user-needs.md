# Design, Research & User Needs Workflows

**IDs:** DR-01 to DR-10

## DR-01 — Research the User
**ROLE:** Primary design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product brief and intended user supplied. **TASK:** Create an age-appropriate user research activity. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use questions that can influence design decisions; avoid collecting unnecessary personal data. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Research questions, recording grid, design implications. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Research connects to the brief. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DR-02 — Identify User Needs
**ROLE:** Design teacher. **CONTEXT:** User information and product purpose supplied. **TASK:** Separate user needs, wants and assumptions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Label unsupported assumptions clearly. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Needs table with evidence and implications. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Needs are relevant to function and context. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group users.

## DR-03 — Create Design Criteria
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief, user needs and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Turn evidence into measurable design criteria. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include function, user, materials, size or other relevant constraints only where justified. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Prioritised specification. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria are testable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-friendly version.

## DR-04 — Analyse Existing Products
**ROLE:** Design & Technology teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product type and available product descriptions/images supplied. **TASK:** Guide pupils to analyse function, materials, construction and user experience. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish observation from inference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Analysis grid and discussion questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Claims are grounded in supplied evidence. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** With/without images.

## DR-05 — Compare Product Solutions
**ROLE:** DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Two or more existing solutions supplied. **TASK:** Compare strengths and limitations against stated criteria. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid personal taste as the sole judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison matrix and recommendation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Judgements reference criteria. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## DR-06 — Generate Design Ideas
**ROLE:** Primary design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Brief and specification supplied. **TASK:** Generate varied starting ideas pupils can develop. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not present one AI-generated design as the correct answer; explain trade-offs. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Idea set with annotated decisions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Ideas satisfy the brief while allowing pupil agency. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Drawing prompts.

## DR-07 — Improve a Design Idea
**ROLE:** Design coach. **CONTEXT:** Pupil design description and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Identify one or more high-value improvements. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve the pupil's ownership; explain why each change matters. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, improvement options, decision prompt. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Suggestions relate to criteria. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Verbal/written feedback.

## DR-08 — Choose Between Designs
**ROLE:** DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil concepts and specification supplied. **TASK:** Structure a reasoned selection process. **REQUIREMENTS:** Compare function, feasibility and user needs. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Decision matrix and justification sentence stems. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Choice is evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/team.

## DR-09 — Investigate Material Suitability
**ROLE:** Primary DT teacher. **CONTEXT:** Product, candidate materials and required properties supplied. **TASK:** Compare materials against functional requirements. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use only supplied or established properties; flag uncertainty. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Property comparison and recommendation. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No invented material claims. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Practical testing included.

## DR-10 — Turn Research into Design Decisions
**ROLE:** Design teacher. **CONTEXT:** Research findings and brief supplied. **TASK:** Convert evidence into explicit design decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Link each decision to evidence and specification. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence-to-decision table. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unsupported leaps. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil reflection sheet.
