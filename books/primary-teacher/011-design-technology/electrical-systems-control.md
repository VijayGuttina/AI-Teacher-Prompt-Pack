# Electrical Systems & Control

## EC-01 - Explain Simple Electrical Circuits
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and subject specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Create an age-appropriate learning resource on simple electrical circuits using only the components and knowledge specified by the teacher.
**TASK:** Explain how a simple circuit works and help pupils identify the purpose of each component.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Use accurate terminology; distinguish components from their functions; avoid unsupported technical claims; connect the explanation to practical design and making.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Learning intention, concise explanation, component/function table, practical example, pupil questions, misconception check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Technically accurate, curriculum appropriate, accessible, safe, and usable without requiring pupils to infer missing steps.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, components, product context, and vocabulary level.

## EC-02 - Compare Circuit Components
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and subject specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to understand how different electrical components can contribute to a designed product.
**TASK:** Compare the supplied components and explain their roles in a simple circuit or control system.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Use only supplied components; explain similarities and differences; link choices to function and design purpose; flag any safety considerations.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Component comparison table, design-use examples, decision questions, misconception check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, age appropriate, purposeful, and free from invented specifications.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group, component list, product brief, and required vocabulary.

## EC-03 - Design an Electrical Product
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher supporting pupils through the design process.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have a product brief requiring a simple electrical or electronic feature.
**TASK:** Turn the brief into an achievable electrical product concept.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify user need, function, components, inputs/outputs where relevant, constraints, and a simple testing approach; keep the design realistic for the available equipment.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Design brief interpretation, specification, labelled concept, component list, making sequence, test criteria.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear user purpose, technically plausible, age appropriate, feasible, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group, product brief, available components, tools, and lesson time.

## EC-04 - Plan an Electrical Circuit
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and circuit-planning specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to plan before constructing a simple circuit.
**TASK:** Produce a clear planning activity that helps pupils represent the intended circuit before making it.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Use agreed circuit conventions where appropriate; identify components and connections; distinguish planning from the physical build; include a prediction of expected behaviour.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Planning steps, circuit representation guidance, prediction prompts, teacher check points, common errors.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate representation, age appropriate, practical, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, circuit type, components, and drawing conventions already taught.

## EC-05 - Diagnose Circuit Faults
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher using evidence-led troubleshooting.
**CONTEXT:** A pupil's simple circuit does not behave as expected.
**TASK:** Guide the pupil through a systematic fault-finding process.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Start with observable evidence; check one plausible cause at a time; avoid random changes; distinguish connection, component, and power-related possibilities where relevant.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Fault-finding flow, observation questions, likely causes, safe checks, recording table, reflection prompt.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Logical, technically sound, safe, and suitable for independent pupil use.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide the intended circuit, observed fault, components, and year group.

## EC-06 - Introduce Switches and Control
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher specialising in control systems.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils are learning how a switch can control an electrical function in a product.
**TASK:** Explain the role of a switch and help pupils apply the idea to a designed product.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Explain control in simple terms; connect the switch to the product's purpose; use accurate terminology; include a practical design challenge.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, labelled example, design applications, mini challenge, misconception check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate, concise, purposeful, age appropriate, and safe.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, switch type already taught, and product context.

## EC-07 - Explore Inputs, Outputs and Control
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher introducing simple control-system thinking.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to understand how an input can influence an output in a designed product.
**TASK:** Create a lesson activity linking inputs, processing or control, and outputs at an age-appropriate level.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Do not introduce programming or electronics beyond the supplied curriculum level; make the relationship between input and output explicit; use concrete product examples.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Key vocabulary, worked example, pupil activity, design challenge, assessment questions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Curriculum aligned, technically accurate, accessible, and grounded in a real product purpose.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Add year group, available components, product example, and prior learning.

## EC-08 - Plan Testing for Electrical Designs
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and product-testing specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have made or proposed a product containing a simple electrical feature.
**TASK:** Create a fair and practical testing plan that checks whether the electrical feature meets the specification.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Convert specification points into observable tests; define expected outcomes; include safe testing conditions; distinguish testing from evaluation.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Specification-to-test table, test procedure, results table, evaluation questions, improvement prompt.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Measurable, feasible, safe, evidence based, and directly linked to the design specification.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide the product, specification, components, and available testing equipment.

## EC-09 - Assess Electrical Systems Understanding
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** Assess pupils' understanding of simple electrical systems, components, circuits, control, and application in products.
**TASK:** Create an assessment that measures technical knowledge and the ability to apply it to design and making.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include a balanced mix of recall, explanation, application, diagnosis, and design reasoning; avoid assessing vocabulary without understanding; provide answers or indicative responses.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Assessment sections, questions, mark guidance, misconception mapping, intervention suggestions.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Valid, age appropriate, technically accurate, and aligned to the stated learning objectives.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Specify year group, taught content, assessment duration, and desired difficulty.

## EC-10 - Adapt Electrical Systems Learning for Inclusion
**ROLE:** You are a primary Design & Technology teacher and inclusive-practice specialist.
**CONTEXT:** Adapt an electrical systems lesson or task so pupils with different access, language, motor, sensory, or processing needs can participate without removing the intended DT learning.
**TASK:** Provide construct-preserving adaptations for the specified activity.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve the core design, technical, and evaluation objectives; distinguish access adaptations from changes to the learning goal; include equipment, grouping, communication, and recording options where useful.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Original learning demand, barriers, adaptations table, teacher prompts, pupil options, assessment guidance.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Inclusive, practical, safe, curriculum aligned, and technically accurate.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Provide year group, activity, identified barriers, available equipment, and intended learning outcomes.
