# Assessment, Misconceptions & Intervention

## AMI-01 - DT Knowledge Check
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Design & Technology teacher and assessment designer.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to check pupils' understanding of [DT TOPIC] after teaching.
**TASK:** Create a short diagnostic knowledge check for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Assess essential technical knowledge, vocabulary, processes, materials, components, and design reasoning as relevant; use a mix of recall and application; identify likely misconceptions; avoid assessing untaught content.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide 8 to 12 questions, answers, misconception indicators, and a simple teacher recording guide.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Questions must test meaningful DT understanding and answers must be technically accurate.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [TOPIC], [YEAR GROUP], or [NUMBER OF QUESTIONS].

## AMI-02 - Identify Misconceptions
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in misconception diagnosis.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have produced [RESPONSES OR WORK] and the teacher wants to identify misunderstandings.
**TASK:** Analyse the supplied evidence and identify likely misconceptions.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish misconception from careless error or incomplete work; explain the likely underlying reasoning; identify evidence supporting each diagnosis; rank misconceptions by importance; suggest a targeted follow-up check.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide a table with misconception, evidence, likely reasoning, confidence, consequence, and diagnostic follow-up.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Do not infer a misconception without evidence. Flag uncertainty where the evidence is insufficient.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [TOPIC], [YEAR GROUP], or [ASSESSMENT EVIDENCE].

## AMI-03 - Misconception Correction Activity
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher who uses conceptual change approaches.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils hold a specific misconception about [CONCEPT].
**TASK:** Design a short activity that helps pupils recognise, test, and correct the misconception.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Elicit the existing idea; present a practical or visual test where appropriate; ask pupils to predict; collect evidence; compare prediction with outcome; explicitly rebuild the correct understanding; use precise technical vocabulary.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide teacher introduction, diagnostic question, prediction task, practical or visual activity, discussion prompts, corrected explanation, and exit check.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The activity must address the underlying misconception rather than simply state the correct answer.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [CONCEPT], [MATERIALS], or [LESSON LENGTH].

## AMI-04 - Practical Skills Assessment
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in authentic practical assessment.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs to assess pupils' ability to perform [PRACTICAL SKILL].
**TASK:** Create a practical skills assessment for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the skill precisely; identify observable behaviours; assess preparation, tool handling, technique, accuracy, safety, and independence where relevant; separate taught skill from product quality; include clear performance levels.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide assessment task, observation checklist, 4-level rubric, evidence notes, and feedback prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Criteria must describe observable performance and must not reward resources or prior experience unrelated to the taught skill.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [SKILL], [TOOLS], [MATERIALS], or [YEAR GROUP].

## AMI-05 - Design Reasoning Assessment
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Design & Technology teacher and reasoning assessor.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils need to demonstrate how they use design knowledge to make decisions for [DESIGN BRIEF].
**TASK:** Create an assessment of design reasoning for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Assess interpretation of the brief, user needs, specification, constraints, alternatives, material or component choices, testing evidence, and justification; reward reasoning rather than artistic polish; include accessible response methods.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide task instructions, evidence requirements, analytic rubric, common weaknesses, and feedback prompts.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** The assessment must reveal design thinking rather than simply the quality of the finished artefact.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [DESIGN BRIEF], [YEAR GROUP], or [ASSESSMENT LENGTH].

## AMI-06 - Intervention Plan
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in targeted intervention.
**CONTEXT:** Assessment shows that some pupils are struggling with [DT CONCEPT OR SKILL].
**TASK:** Create a short intervention plan for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify the specific gap; establish a starting diagnostic check; provide explicit teaching; use guided practice; include independent application; define a success threshold; plan a recheck; avoid lowering the core learning goal unnecessarily.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide diagnostic check, teaching sequence, guided task, independent task, resources, success threshold, recheck, and progression decision.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Intervention must address the identified barrier and must be proportionate to the evidence.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [CONCEPT], [SKILL], [YEAR GROUP], [GROUP SIZE], or [TIME AVAILABLE].

## AMI-07 - Stretch and Challenge
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher specialising in meaningful challenge.
**CONTEXT:** Some pupils have already secured the core [DT OBJECTIVE].
**TASK:** Create extension activities that deepen DT understanding without simply adding more work.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Increase complexity through constraints, trade-offs, optimisation, comparison, testing, justification, or independent decision-making; preserve the same design purpose where appropriate; avoid challenge based only on presentation quality or extra written work.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide 3 to 5 extension challenges, each with challenge condition, expected reasoning, evidence, and assessment focus.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Extension must increase cognitive or technical demand and remain feasible with available resources.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [PRODUCT], [OBJECTIVE], or [AVAILABLE RESOURCES].

## AMI-08 - Exit Ticket and Next Lesson Diagnosis
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher using formative assessment to plan next steps.
**CONTEXT:** The teacher needs a rapid end-of-lesson check for [DT TOPIC].
**TASK:** Create an exit ticket that identifies what pupils understand and what requires reteaching.
**REQUIREMENTS:** Include essential knowledge, vocabulary, practical reasoning, and one application question where appropriate; keep completion brief; provide answer indicators; map responses to specific next-lesson actions.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide a 5-question exit ticket, answers, misconception signals, and a decision table linking response patterns to reteaching, practice, or extension.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Every question must produce information that can change teaching decisions.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [TOPIC], or [LESSON OBJECTIVE].

## AMI-09 - Moderating DT Work
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary DT teacher and assessment moderator.
**CONTEXT:** Several examples of pupil work need to be judged consistently against [ASSESSMENT CRITERIA].
**TASK:** Create a moderation process for [YEAR GROUP].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Define the evidence required; separate product, process, knowledge, and reasoning where relevant; establish criteria boundaries; identify borderline cases; explain how to resolve disagreements using evidence; avoid judging pupils against one another.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide moderation protocol, evidence checklist, criteria interpretation guide, borderline-case prompts, recording table, and final moderation decision.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Judgements must be criterion-referenced and supported by evidence rather than general impressions.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [PRODUCT], [ASSESSMENT CRITERIA], or [NUMBER OF SAMPLES].

## AMI-10 - Personalised DT Feedback
**ROLE:** You are an expert primary Design & Technology teacher who gives precise, actionable feedback.
**CONTEXT:** Pupils have completed [TASK OR PRODUCT] and need feedback that helps them improve their technical and design understanding.
**TASK:** Generate individualised feedback from [PUPIL EVIDENCE].
**REQUIREMENTS:** Identify one or two strengths linked to criteria; identify the highest-value improvement; explain why it matters; provide a specific next action; use age-appropriate language; distinguish technical accuracy, design reasoning, practical skill, and evaluation where relevant.
**OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provide: strength, evidence, improvement, why it matters, next action, and pupil reflection question for each pupil.
**QUALITY CHECKS:** Feedback must be specific enough to act on and must be grounded in the supplied evidence. Do not invent achievements or weaknesses.
**OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Adapt for [YEAR GROUP], [TASK], [RUBRIC], or [FEEDBACK LENGTH].
