# English Grammar, Punctuation and Spelling Workflows

This module contains compact reusable workflows for primary English grammar, punctuation and spelling. It inherits from the global, master, primary-teacher and English frameworks.

## GPS-01 — Grammar lesson sequence

**Purpose:** Build a focused lesson around a stated grammar concept.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Grammar, Lesson Planning

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Grammar concept
- Learning objective
- Lesson duration
- Prior knowledge
- Scaffold level

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary English teacher and grammar specialist.

### TASK
Create a coherent grammar lesson moving from explanation and modelling to guided practice and independent application.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Define the grammar concept accurately.
- Use age-appropriate terminology.
- Include examples and non-examples where useful.
- Address one or two likely misconceptions.
- Include a short assessment check.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Objective and success criteria
2. Prior knowledge
3. Explicit teaching
4. Examples / non-examples
5. Guided practice
6. Independent practice
7. Assessment check
8. Misconceptions
9. Resources

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every example must genuinely illustrate the stated grammatical concept. Terminology must be accurate and appropriate to the year group.

## GPS-02 — Grammar explanation for pupils

**Purpose:** Explain a grammar concept in language pupils can understand.

**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Output:** Short  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Grammar

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Grammar concept
- Existing misconception
- Example context

### ROLE
Act as a primary English teacher explaining grammar directly to pupils.

### TASK
Give a concise, pupil-friendly explanation of the supplied grammar concept, followed by examples and a quick check.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Explain the meaning and function, not just the label.
- Use accurate terminology.
- Avoid unnecessary linguistic jargon.
- Include at least one non-example where useful.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Simple explanation
2. Examples
3. Non-example and correction
4. Quick pupil check
5. Teacher note

## GPS-04 — Grammar misconception diagnosis

**Purpose:** Diagnose a likely misconception from pupil responses.

**Difficulty:** Advanced  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Grammar, Assessment

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Grammar objective
- Pupil responses
- Previous teaching

### ROLE
Act as a primary grammar assessment specialist.

### TASK
Identify observable patterns in the responses, suggest plausible misconceptions as hypotheses, and propose a quick diagnostic check.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Separate evidence from inference.
- Do not label or diagnose the pupil.
- Prioritise the most important misconception.
- Provide a targeted teaching response.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
| Observed pattern | Possible misconception | Evidence | Diagnostic check | Teaching response |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present an inferred cause as fact.

Create an explicit spelling lesson using the supplied pattern or rule, with practice and application.

## GPS-09 — Grammar, punctuation and spelling assessment

**Purpose:** Create a focused assessment covering specified GPS objectives.

**Difficulty:** Advanced  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, GPS, Assessment

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Objectives
- Number of questions
- Difficulty
- Assessment duration

### ROLE
Act as a primary English assessment specialist.

### TASK
Create a balanced assessment that measures the supplied objectives without allowing one objective to dominate unintentionally.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Map each question to an objective.
- Include a mixture of recognition and application where appropriate.
- Provide answers and marking guidance.
- Flag any item where more than one answer could reasonably be accepted.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Assessment paper
2. Objective map
3. Answer key
4. Marking guidance
5. Common error analysis

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every item must have a defensible answer or clearly stated acceptable responses.

## GPS-10 — Adapt GPS practice for SEND / EAL

**Purpose:** Adapt a GPS activity to remove unnecessary access barriers while retaining the target concept.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Output:** Short  
**Tags:** Primary, English, GPS, SEND, EAL, Differentiation

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Original activity
- Target objective
- Access barrier
- Desired level of independence

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt the supplied activity for the stated access need while preserving the core grammar, punctuation or spelling objective.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Identify the barrier being addressed.
- Use appropriate scaffolds such as reduced cognitive load, examples, visual structure or oral rehearsal.
- Do not assume a diagnosis from the stated need.
- Include a route toward greater independence.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Barrier addressed
2. Adapted activity
3. Teacher guidance
4. Independence-release option

### QUALITY CHECKS
The adaptation must change access demands rather than quietly replacing the intended learning objective.

## GPS-11 — Edit pupil writing for GPS features

**Purpose:** Analyse a supplied piece of writing for selected grammar, punctuation and spelling features.

**Difficulty:** Advanced  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, GPS, Assessment, Editing

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Target GPS features
- Pupil writing
- Assessment criteria

### ROLE
Act as a primary English assessment support assistant.

### CONTEXT
Use only the supplied writing and criteria. Do not infer personal characteristics about the pupil.

### TASK
Identify accurate examples, errors and opportunities for improvement relating to the specified GPS features.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Quote only the minimum text needed as evidence.
- Separate correction from optional stylistic improvement.
- Do not mark a legitimate variation as an error without justification.
- Distinguish missing evidence from evidence of non-mastery.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
| Feature | Evidence | Status | Suggested teaching point |
|---|---|---|---|

Then provide two priority next steps.

### QUALITY CHECKS
All judgements must be traceable to the supplied writing and criteria.
