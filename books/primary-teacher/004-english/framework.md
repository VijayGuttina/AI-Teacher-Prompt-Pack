## Core English domains

- Reading
- Writing
- Grammar
- Punctuation
- Spelling
- Vocabulary
- Spoken language / oracy

## Core principles

1. Teach the stated English knowledge or skill explicitly before expecting independent application.
2. Use precise English terminology appropriate to the year group.
3. Keep reading tasks anchored to the supplied text where a text is provided.
4. Keep writing tasks anchored to purpose, audience, form and intended effect.
5. Distinguish retrieval, inference, explanation, evaluation and response-to-text tasks.
6. Model successful language choices rather than merely describing them.
7. Use examples and non-examples where they clarify the target concept.
8. Preserve the intended learning objective when adapting for SEND, EAL or lower prior attainment.
9. Make vocabulary teaching contextual and purposeful.
10. Require generated model texts, questions, answers and explanations to be checked for accuracy.

## English-specific quality gates

Outputs should:

- be suitable for the stated year group;
- use accurate grammar and terminology;
- avoid invented quotations or textual evidence;
- distinguish evidence from interpretation;
- avoid teaching pupils that there is only one acceptable creative response where multiple responses are valid;
- keep model texts aligned with the requested genre and audience;
- ensure assessment questions actually measure the stated skill;
- avoid reducing writing quality to superficial features when the objective is composition or effect;
- identify assumptions when the supplied text, curriculum objective or pupil information is incomplete.

## Inherited workflow pattern

Individual English workflows should normally contain:

```text
Metadata
Variables
ROLE exception, if needed
CONTEXT
TASK
Workflow-specific REQUIREMENTS
OUTPUT FORMAT
Workflow-specific QUALITY CHECKS
Optional Customisation
Example Input/Output where useful
Teacher Tip
Related Workflows
```

Do not repeat the general primary-teacher role, generic pedagogy, generic differentiation guidance or global quality rules in every English workflow.

## Curriculum scope

The default context is primary education in England, KS1 and KS2. Specific statutory objectives should be supplied or verified when the workflow depends on them.

The framework does not treat England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland as interchangeable curriculum systems.
