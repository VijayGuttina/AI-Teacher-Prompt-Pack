# English Vocabulary and Oracy Workflows

This module contains compact reusable workflows for primary vocabulary development, speaking and listening, discussion and oral rehearsal. It inherits from the global, master, primary-teacher and English frameworks.

## VO-01 — Vocabulary teaching sequence

**Purpose:** Teach selected vocabulary through meaning, context, retrieval and application.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Vocabulary

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Target words
- Topic / text
- Lesson duration
- Prior knowledge
- Scaffold level

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary English teacher and vocabulary specialist.

### TASK
Create a practical vocabulary teaching sequence that moves from meaning and pronunciation to contextual use and retrieval.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Give clear pupil-friendly meanings.
- Use context rather than isolated dictionary-style definitions alone.
- Include pronunciation guidance where useful.
- Include examples and non-examples where useful.
- Include retrieval and application.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Target vocabulary
2. Pupil-friendly meanings
3. Context examples
4. Guided practice
5. Application task
6. Retrieval check
7. Common misconceptions

### QUALITY CHECKS
Definitions and examples must be semantically accurate and appropriate to the year group.

## VO-06 — Structured discussion and debate

**Purpose:** Develop pupils' ability to discuss, justify and respond to ideas.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Oracy, Discussion

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Proposition / question
- Curriculum context
- Group size
- Duration
- Scaffold level

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary teacher and oracy practitioner.

### TASK
Create a structured discussion or age-appropriate debate with clear roles, evidence expectations and opportunities to respond to other viewpoints.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Make the central question genuinely discussable.
- Require reasons or evidence rather than unsupported opinions.
- Include respectful response structures.
- Provide scaffolds where useful.
- Avoid presenting a complex or sensitive issue as though there is only one legitimate viewpoint unless the evidence clearly establishes it.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Discussion question
2. Background / stimulus
3. Ground rules
4. Roles
5. Prompt questions
6. Sentence stems
7. Teacher assessment focus
8. Reflection

## VO-10 — Vocabulary misconception diagnosis

**Purpose:** Identify likely misunderstandings of word meaning or usage from pupil responses.

**Difficulty:** Advanced  
**Output:** Medium  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Vocabulary, Assessment

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Target vocabulary
- Pupil responses
- Context / text

### ROLE
Act as a primary vocabulary assessment specialist.

### TASK
Analyse the supplied responses, identify observable misunderstanding patterns, and propose a quick diagnostic activity followed by targeted teaching.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Distinguish evidence from inferred cause.
- Check whether the issue is meaning, context, morphology, pronunciation or usage where evidence permits.
- Avoid labelling the pupil.
- Prioritise the highest-impact misconception.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Observed pattern
2. Possible misunderstanding
3. Evidence
4. Diagnostic check
5. Teaching response
6. Follow-up retrieval

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present an inferred cause as fact.

## VO-12 — Adapt vocabulary or oracy activity

**Purpose:** Adapt an activity to reduce unnecessary language or participation barriers while preserving the intended learning.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Output:** Short  
**Tags:** Primary, English, Vocabulary, Oracy, SEND, EAL

### Editable Variables
- Year group
- Original activity
- Learning objective
- Access barrier
- Existing support

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary teacher specialising in adaptive teaching and language development.

### TASK
Adapt the supplied activity to address the stated access barrier while retaining the core objective and meaningful pupil participation.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Identify the barrier being addressed.
- Use practical supports such as visuals, rehearsal, reduced language load or structured turns where appropriate.
- Do not assume a diagnosis.
- Include a route toward increasing independence.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Barrier
2. Adapted activity
3. Teacher guidance
4. Independence-release option

### QUALITY CHECKS
The adaptation must improve access without removing the intended cognitive or communicative demand.
