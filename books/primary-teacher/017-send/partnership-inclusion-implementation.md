# SEND Partnership, Inclusion & Implementation

## Family purpose

This family helps primary teachers turn SEND information, pupil voice, family knowledge, staff collaboration and school processes into practical, inclusive classroom implementation. The prompts focus on partnership, communication, review, consistency and sustainable implementation while keeping professional boundaries clear.

## Family-wide controls

1. Start with the pupil, intended learning, participation goal and observed evidence.
2. Treat pupils, families, teachers, SENDCOs, specialists and other staff as partners with different knowledge and responsibilities.
3. Do not diagnose, prescribe therapy, interpret specialist evidence beyond what is supplied or make statutory decisions.
4. Do not invent EHCP content, reasonable adjustments, school policy, safeguarding procedures, specialist recommendations or local authority processes.
5. Use supplied pupil, family, professional and school information as controlling context.
6. Protect pupil dignity, privacy, agency and confidentiality.
7. Do not require a pupil or family to disclose unnecessary personal, medical or sensitive information.
8. Distinguish classroom observations from professional or medical conclusions.
9. Use strengths-based, precise and respectful language.
10. Focus on practical implementation rather than producing generic SEND statements.
11. Ensure agreed support is realistic for the classroom and does not create unnecessary adult dependency.
12. Preserve curriculum ambition and intended learning constructs.
13. Make ownership, actions, review points and evidence explicit where implementation is being planned.
14. Avoid family blame, deficit framing and assumptions about home circumstances.
15. Where current statutory or local guidance matters, flag the requirement for authoritative verification.
16. Do not use an em dash character.
