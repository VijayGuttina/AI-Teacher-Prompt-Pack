# Religion, Worldviews, Identity & Community

This family supports primary teachers in exploring religion, worldviews, identity and community through accurate, evidence-led enquiry. Prompts should distinguish belief, practice, identity, culture, ethnicity, nationality and community. They should represent internal diversity and non-religious worldviews without stereotyping or tokenism.

Every workflow protects pupil dignity. Pupils must not be required to disclose their religion, worldview, family background, identity or personal experiences, and pupils must not be positioned as representatives of a religion or culture. First-person accounts should be treated as evidence of a particular lived experience, with provenance and appropriate context.

### EXAMPLE INPUT
