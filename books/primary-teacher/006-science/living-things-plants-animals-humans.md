# Living Things, Plants, Animals and Humans Workflows

## LH-01 — Living things concept lesson
**Purpose:** Teach a stated living-things concept at the appropriate year level.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; examples; prior knowledge.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Design a concept lesson with clear scientific explanations, examples and application.

### REQUIREMENTS
Use observable evidence and precise vocabulary. Address the most relevant misconception.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; retrieval; explanation; examples; guided task; independent task; assessment; misconception; answers.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check every biological statement for accuracy and age appropriateness.

## LH-02 — Plant structure and function
**Purpose:** Connect plant parts to their functions.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; plant parts; context; representations.

### ROLE
Act as a primary biology specialist.

### TASK
Create teaching and reasoning tasks linking structure with function.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Key structures; functions; model; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not overstate functions or introduce secondary-school mechanisms unnecessarily.

## LH-03 — Plant growth investigation
**Purpose:** Plan an appropriate investigation into plant growth or requirements.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; factor; plant; duration; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK

### OUTPUT FORMAT

## LH-04 — Animal classification

## LH-09 — Adaptation and survival
**Purpose:** Develop age-appropriate reasoning about how features help organisms survive.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; organism; habitat; feature.

Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK

### OUTPUT FORMAT

### QUALITY CHECKS

## LH-10 — Living-things misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose misconceptions from pupil responses.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; pupil responses; objective; recent teaching.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science diagnostic specialist.

### TASK
Identify observable errors, plausible explanations, discriminating questions and a focused teaching response.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed error; hypothesis; evidence; diagnostic question; teaching response; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Separate evidence from inference and do not infer fixed ability from limited evidence.
