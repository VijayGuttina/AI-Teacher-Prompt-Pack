Analyse `[PUPIL_RESPONSES]` from `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupils working on `[OBJECTIVE]`. Consider `[RECENT_TEACHING]` and `[REPRESENTATIONS_USED]` when interpreting the evidence.

### QUALITY CHECKS

### Example Output
Test whether pupils are relying on habitat rather than biological characteristics by presenting contrasting examples and asking them to identify the feature that determines the classification.
