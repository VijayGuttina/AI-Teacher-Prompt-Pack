# Light, Sound, Electricity and Energy Workflows

## LE-01 — Light concept lesson
**Purpose:** Teach a stated primary light concept.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; examples; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary physics teacher.

### TASK
Create a concept sequence using accurate models and observable examples.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; model; demonstration; questions; answers; misconception; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Use accurate language about sources, reflection, shadows or visibility as appropriate.

---

## LE-02 — Shadows investigation
**Purpose:** Investigate how shadows change.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; light source; object; variable; measurement.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science enquiry specialist.

### TASK
Design a feasible investigation of shadow size, direction or position.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; variables; method; data table; conclusion; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the method actually changes and measures the named variable.

---

## LE-03 — Reflection investigation
**Purpose:** Explore reflection using suitable materials.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; surfaces; light source; question.

### ROLE
Act as a primary physics teacher.

### TASK
Create a practical comparison of reflective surfaces.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; method; observations; recording; conclusion; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid describing reflection as light being simply "bounced back" without suitable context when greater precision is needed.

## LE-07 — Conductors and insulators

**Editable Variables:** Year group; materials; circuit; prediction task.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; method; results table; conclusion; safety.

## LE-08 — Energy in everyday contexts
**Purpose:** Develop age-appropriate understanding of energy use and transfer.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; context; devices; objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science specialist.

### TASK
Create an accurate classroom explanation without introducing unnecessary secondary-level formalism.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Concept; examples; diagram description; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS

## LE-10 — Adapt physics practical
**Purpose:** Adapt a practical science task while preserving the physics.

**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt presentation, equipment access, recording or language without removing the intended scientific reasoning.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adaptation; support; preserved demand; independence route; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The adaptation must preserve the scientific variable, observation or reasoning target.
