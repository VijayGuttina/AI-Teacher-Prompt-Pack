# Assessment, Diagnosis and Intervention Workflows

## ADI-01 — Science exit ticket
**Purpose:** Assess a stated science objective quickly.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; number of questions; recent teaching.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science assessment specialist.

### TASK
Create a concise check sampling scientific knowledge and, where appropriate, reasoning or evidence use.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; questions; answers; what each item assesses; interpretation; next step.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every item must align to the objective and every closed answer must be scientifically correct.

---

## ADI-02 — Science hinge question
**Purpose:** Create a diagnostic multiple-choice question for live teaching.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; misconception; response options.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science assessment specialist.

### TASK
Create a hinge question whose distractors represent plausible scientific misconceptions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; options; correct answer; distractor interpretations; teacher response.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Distractors must be scientifically plausible and diagnostically meaningful.

---

## ADI-03 — Science objective assessment
**Purpose:** Build a balanced assessment for a science objective or small unit.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objectives; coverage; item count.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science assessment designer.

### TASK
Create a varied assessment covering recall, application, explanation and evidence interpretation where appropriate.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Blueprint; questions; answers; marking guidance; interpretation; next actions.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check factual accuracy, coverage and whether each question measures what it claims to measure.

---

## ADI-04 — Analyse pupil science work
**Purpose:** Analyse supplied pupil responses for strengths, errors and next teaching needs.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; pupil work; recent teaching.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science assessment and diagnostic specialist.

### CONTEXT
Treat explanations for errors as hypotheses unless directly established.

### TASK
Identify observable evidence and recommend the smallest useful next teaching step.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Strengths; observable errors; pattern; hypotheses; evidence; diagnostic check; next step; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer fixed ability, effort or misconception from insufficient evidence.

---

## ADI-05 — Science misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Test whether a suspected misconception is genuinely present.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; misconception; evidence; topic.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science diagnostic specialist.

### TASK
Create discriminating questions or demonstrations that distinguish the hypothesis from alternative explanations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Hypothesis; diagnostic items; expected responses; interpretation; teaching response.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Each diagnostic item must provide new evidence rather than repeat the original question.

---

## ADI-06 — Targeted science intervention
**Purpose:** Design a short intervention for a clearly evidenced conceptual gap.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; gap; evidence; prerequisites; sessions.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science intervention teacher.

### TASK
Design a focused sequence using explanation, representation, retrieval, application and checking.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Need; prerequisites; session sequence; representations; practice; diagnostic checks; exit criterion.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not build an intervention around an unverified diagnosis.

---

## ADI-07 — Science retrieval planner
**Purpose:** Create purposeful retrieval for prior science knowledge.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; current topic; prerequisite knowledge; number of items.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create retrieval questions that reactivate knowledge needed for the current learning.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Retrieval set; answers; prerequisite link; common error; re-teach note.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Questions must test relevant prior knowledge rather than generic science trivia.

---

## ADI-08 — Adapt science task
**Purpose:** Adapt a science task for access without reducing the scientific objective.

**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; objective; support available.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt language, visuals, recording, equipment access or sequencing while preserving scientific thinking.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adapted task; support; preserved demand; independence route; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm the same science concept, observation or reasoning remains under assessment.

---

## ADI-09 — Science evidence summary
**Purpose:** Convert assessment or enquiry evidence into a concise teaching summary.

**Editable Variables:** Data; objective; pupil/group information; next lesson constraints.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science subject leader.

### TASK
Summarise what the evidence establishes, what it does not establish and what should happen next.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Secure understanding; developing understanding; errors; evidence limits; priority actions; diagnostic follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every recommendation must be traceable to supplied evidence.

---

## ADI-10 — Unit review and reteach planner
**Purpose:** Use end-of-unit evidence to plan retrieval, reteaching and future sequencing.

**Editable Variables:** Unit objectives; assessment results; misconceptions; next unit; available time.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science subject leader.

### TASK
Identify secure knowledge, unresolved gaps and high-value reteaching priorities without overreacting to isolated errors.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence summary; secure learning; gaps; priority misconceptions; reteach plan; retrieval schedule; future sequencing notes.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish cohort patterns from individual anomalies and keep claims proportionate to the evidence.
