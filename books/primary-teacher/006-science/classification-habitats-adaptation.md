# Classification, Habitats and Adaptation Workflows

## CH-01 — Habitat concept lesson
**Purpose:** Teach how habitats provide conditions and resources for living things.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; habitat; organisms; objective.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary biology teacher.

### TASK
Create a concept sequence linking habitat conditions to the needs of organisms.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; habitat model; examples; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not imply that habitats are only large geographical areas; include microhabitats where appropriate.

---

## CH-02 — Local habitat survey
**Purpose:** Plan a manageable field observation.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; location; organisms; equipment; time.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science enquiry specialist.

### TASK
Design a structured habitat survey using observation, classification and recording.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; sampling/observation plan; recording sheet; identification guidance; analysis; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure observations can be made safely and do not require unsupported species identification.

---

## CH-03 — Classification key builder
**Purpose:** Build a simple classification or identification key.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; organisms/objects; characteristics.

### ROLE
Act as a primary biology specialist.

### TASK
Create a branching key based on observable characteristics.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Characteristics; key; pupil task; answers; discussion.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every branch must lead consistently to the intended group or identification.

---

## CH-04 — Food-web reasoning
**Purpose:** Extend simple food chains into interconnected feeding relationships where appropriate.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; habitat; organisms; difficulty.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary biology teacher.

### TASK
Create a reasoning task showing how changes to one organism can affect others.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Food web; scenario; reasoning questions; answers; evidence notes.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check every feeding relationship and avoid deterministic claims where multiple outcomes are possible.

---

## CH-05 — Environmental impact case study
**Purpose:** Examine evidence about a change to a habitat.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; habitat; change; evidence set.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create an evidence-led case study requiring pupils to identify effects and possible responses.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; questions; conclusions; limitations; action options.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Separate observed effects from inferred causes and value judgements.

---

## CH-06 — Adaptation comparison
**Purpose:** Compare structural or behavioural adaptations in different environments.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; organisms; habitats; features.

### ROLE
Act as a primary biology specialist.

### TASK
Create a comparison task linking features to survival functions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Organisms; features; habitat; function; questions; answers.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid teleological explanations suggesting organisms consciously choose adaptations.

---

## CH-07 — Microhabitat investigation
**Purpose:** Investigate how a microhabitat supports different organisms.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; local microhabitat; observations; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Plan a safe observation and comparison enquiry.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; method; observations; table; analysis; conclusion; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Use non-destructive observation methods and appropriate safeguarding of living organisms.

---

## CH-08 — Classification reasoning
**Purpose:** Require pupils to justify classifications rather than merely name groups.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; organisms; classification system; difficulty.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science assessment specialist.

### TASK
Create classification statements and justification questions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Statements; classification; evidence; justification; answers.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Each classification must be supported by the stated characteristics.

---

## CH-09 — Habitat/classification misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose errors about habitats, classification or interdependence.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; pupil responses; objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary biology diagnostic specialist.

### TASK
Analyse responses and create discriminating diagnostic questions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed error; hypothesis; evidence; diagnostic task; teaching response; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not treat a vocabulary error as proof of conceptual misunderstanding without further evidence.

---

## CH-10 — Adapt habitat investigation
**Purpose:** Adapt a fieldwork or classification task while preserving scientific observation.

**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; location/equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt access, recording or observation procedures without changing the scientific question.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adaptation; support; preserved demand; independence route; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the adapted task still generates comparable scientific evidence.
