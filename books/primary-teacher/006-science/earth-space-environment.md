# Earth, Space and Environmental Science Workflows

## ES-01 — Earth and space concept lesson
**Purpose:** Teach a stated primary Earth or space concept.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objective; prior knowledge; representations.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a concept lesson using accurate models and accessible explanations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; model; explanation; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check terminology and distinguish observations from model-based explanations.

---

## ES-02 — Solar system model
**Purpose:** Use a model to teach relationships among objects in the Solar System.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objects; model type; scale constraints.

### ROLE
Act as a primary astronomy specialist.

### TASK
Create a model-based activity and explicitly identify useful simplifications and limitations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model; representation; pupil task; questions; answers; model limitations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present not-to-scale classroom models as physically proportional.

---

## ES-03 — Earth rotation and day/night
**Purpose:** Develop an accurate primary explanation of day and night.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; model; locations; questions.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Use a suitable model to explain the apparent movement of the Sun and the role of Earth's rotation.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model; explanation; demonstration; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not reinforce the misconception that the Sun physically travels around Earth each day.

---

## ES-04 — Moon phases
**Purpose:** Teach an age-appropriate model of Moon phases.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; model; phase set; observation context.

### ROLE
Act as a primary astronomy specialist.

### TASK
Create a model and observation sequence that explains changing visible illumination.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model; stages; observation task; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid explaining phases as Earth's shadow except for eclipses.

---

## ES-05 — Seasonal change investigation
**Purpose:** Observe or analyse seasonal patterns.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; local observations; time period; data available.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Design an observation-over-time investigation linking local evidence to seasonal patterns.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; observation schedule; data; representation; conclusion; limitations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Separate observed seasonal patterns from broader causal explanations.

---

## ES-06 — Rocks and soils
**Purpose:** Teach or investigate properties and formation of rocks or soils.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; samples; properties; objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary Earth-science specialist.

### TASK
Create a classification, comparison or observation activity using appropriate properties.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; samples; criteria; activity; questions; answers; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check rock and soil terminology and avoid unsupported identification from appearance alone.

---

## ES-07 — Environmental change
**Purpose:** Explore how environments change and how this can affect living things.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; environment; change; evidence.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create an evidence-led case study or investigation without turning complex environmental causation into unsupported certainty.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Context; evidence; impacts; questions; answers; limitations; action discussion.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish evidence, inference and value judgement.

---

## ES-08 — Sustainability science task
**Purpose:** Connect scientific knowledge to an age-appropriate sustainability decision.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; issue; evidence; constraints.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a decision task requiring pupils to use supplied scientific evidence rather than simply express opinions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Scenario; evidence; options; decision criteria; questions; model answer; limitations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present contested or value-based claims as simple scientific facts.

---

## ES-09 — Earth and space misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose common misconceptions in Earth and space science.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; pupil responses; objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary astronomy diagnostic specialist.

### TASK
Identify observable misconceptions and create discriminating model-based questions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed response; possible explanation; evidence; diagnostic model/question; teaching response; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check that the diagnostic question distinguishes the stated misconception from plausible alternatives.

---

## ES-10 — Adapt Earth/space task
**Purpose:** Improve access to an Earth, space or environmental science task.

**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; objective.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt language, representation, model or recording without reducing the intended scientific reasoning.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adapted task; support; preserved demand; independence route.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm that the same scientific concept or evidence-based decision remains the target.
