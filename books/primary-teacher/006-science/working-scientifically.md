# Working Scientifically Workflows

## WS-01 — Scientific question builder
**Purpose:** Turn a broad curiosity into a testable primary science question.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; starting observation; available equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create several increasingly precise scientific questions from the supplied starting point and identify the strongest question for the stated year group.

### REQUIREMENTS
Match the question to a feasible enquiry type and avoid questions that cannot be answered with the available evidence.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question options; recommended question; enquiry type; why it fits; required evidence.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm the question is scientifically meaningful and practically answerable.

---

## WS-02 — Enquiry type selector
**Purpose:** Select an appropriate scientific enquiry for a question.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; topic; constraints.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science enquiry specialist.

### TASK
Determine whether observing over time, pattern seeking, classification, comparative/fair testing or secondary research is most appropriate.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Recommended enquiry; rationale; what pupils will observe/measure/find; alternative if constraints change.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not force a fair test where the question is better answered another way.

---

## WS-03 — Fair test planner
**Purpose:** Plan a simple comparative or fair test.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; variable to change; outcome to measure; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Design a fair test that identifies the independent variable, dependent variable and relevant control variables.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; variables; equipment; method; recording table; conclusion prompt; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the proposed measurement actually answers the question and the controls are realistic.

---

## WS-04 — Observation-over-time planner
**Purpose:** Plan a longitudinal observation enquiry.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; phenomenon; observation period; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science specialist.

### TASK
Create an observation schedule that captures meaningful change over time.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; schedule; observations; recording format; expected patterns; conclusion prompts.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure observations are feasible at the stated intervals and do not imply unsupported outcomes.

---

## WS-05 — Pattern-seeking investigation
**Purpose:** Design an investigation looking for relationships in observations or data.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; topic; variables; data available.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a pattern-seeking investigation and explain what would count as evidence of a pattern.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; data to collect; method; table/graph; pattern prompts; limitations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not imply that correlation automatically establishes causation.

---

## WS-06 — Classification investigation
**Purpose:** Build a classification activity using observable characteristics.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objects/living things; characteristics.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a classification task and, where appropriate, a simple identification key.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Criteria; groups; key; pupil task; answers; discussion questions.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every classification decision must follow the stated characteristics.

---

## WS-07 — Secondary-source research planner
**Purpose:** Help pupils answer a science question through reliable secondary sources.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; source types; lesson time.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science and information-literacy specialist.

### TASK
Create a research workflow that distinguishes source information from pupil inference.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; search terms; source checklist; note-taking structure; synthesis questions; citation guidance.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not invent source findings or citations.

---

## WS-08 — Data recording template
**Purpose:** Design an age-appropriate way to record science observations or measurements.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; enquiry; measurements; expected data volume.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a recording table or other suitable format that makes the evidence easy to analyse.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Teacher instructions; pupil table; units; example entry; analysis prompts.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check headings, units and number of columns against the enquiry.

---

## WS-09 — Conclusion and evidence builder
**Purpose:** Help pupils draw a conclusion from supplied science data.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; data; prediction.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science assessment specialist.

### TASK
Guide pupils from data to a conclusion while distinguishing evidence from explanation.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
What the data show; evidence; conclusion; relation to prediction; limitation; next question.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every conclusion must be supported by the supplied data.

---

## WS-10 — Enquiry evaluation and improvement
**Purpose:** Evaluate a completed investigation and identify a meaningful improvement.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; question; method; results; limitations.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Analyse the enquiry for strengths, limitations and one or more feasible improvements.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Strengths; limitations; evidence; improvement; expected effect; further question.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not recommend changes that alter the question unless explicitly identified as a new investigation.
