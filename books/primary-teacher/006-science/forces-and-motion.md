# Forces and Motion Workflows

## FM-01 — Forces concept lesson
**Purpose:** Teach an age-appropriate concept about forces.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; force type; examples; objective.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary physics teacher.

### TASK
Create a concept lesson linking forces to observable effects.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; prior knowledge; explanation; demonstrations; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Use scientifically accurate force language appropriate to the year group.

---

## FM-02 — Pushes and pulls
**Purpose:** Develop understanding of pushes and pulls as forces.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; contexts; objects.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create classification and reasoning activities using familiar examples.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Examples; classification; explanation; questions; answers.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid implying that only moving objects experience forces.

---

## FM-03 — Friction investigation
**Purpose:** Plan an investigation into friction.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; surfaces; object; measurement method.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Design a fair or comparative test of friction with clear variables and recording.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; variables; equipment; method; table; conclusion; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the measured outcome is a reasonable proxy for the friction being investigated.

---

## FM-04 — Magnets investigation
**Purpose:** Investigate magnetic attraction and material properties.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; objects; magnets; question.

### ROLE
Act as a primary physics specialist.

### TASK
Create an investigation that distinguishes magnetic from non-magnetic materials and develops pattern reasoning.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; method; data table; conclusion; vocabulary; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not describe all metals as magnetic.

---

## FM-05 — Balanced and unbalanced forces
**Purpose:** Develop an appropriate primary model of balanced and unbalanced forces.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; contexts; representations.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary physics teacher.

### TASK
Create examples and reasoning tasks showing how forces affect motion or equilibrium.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Model; examples; diagrams; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Avoid claiming that balanced forces mean no forces are acting.

---

## FM-06 — Air and water resistance
**Purpose:** Investigate resistance forces.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; object designs; variable; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science enquiry specialist.

### TASK
Plan a comparative investigation with a measurable outcome and fair-test controls.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; prediction; variables; method; results table; conclusion; improvement; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check that the design difference actually changes the intended variable.

## FM-07 — Levers, gears and pulleys
**Purpose:** Explore simple mechanisms and how forces can be transferred or changed.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; mechanism; materials; task.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science and design specialist.

### TASK
Create a practical investigation linking mechanism structure to observable effect.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Mechanism; model; practical task; observations; questions; answers; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not overstate mechanical advantage beyond what the supplied evidence supports.

---

## FM-08 — Force-data reasoning
**Purpose:** Develop interpretation of data from a forces investigation.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; data set; variables; graph type.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science assessment specialist.

### TASK
Create questions requiring pupils to identify patterns, compare results and draw evidence-based conclusions.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Data; graph/table; questions; answers; conclusion guidance.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Verify all calculations, scales and conclusions.

---

## FM-09 — Forces misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose misconceptions about forces and motion.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; pupil responses; objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary physics diagnostic specialist.

### TASK
Identify observable errors and create questions that distinguish likely explanations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed response; possible explanation; evidence; diagnostic question; teaching response; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer a misconception from a single ambiguous response.

---

## FM-10 — Adapt forces investigation
**Purpose:** Improve access to a forces practical without removing the science.

**Editable Variables:** Original investigation; year group; access barrier; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Adapt instructions, recording, grouping or equipment use while preserving the scientific question and evidence requirement.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adaptation; support; preserved enquiry demand; independence route.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm that pupils still make the same scientific decision or observation.
