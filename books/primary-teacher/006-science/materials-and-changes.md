# Materials and Changes Workflows

## MC-01 — Materials properties lesson
**Purpose:** Teach properties of common materials.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; materials; properties; context.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a lesson that links observable properties to suitable uses.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; vocabulary; examples; comparison task; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Use precise property language and distinguish observable properties from inferred composition.

---

## MC-02 — Material classification
**Purpose:** Group materials using specified properties.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; material set; properties.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science specialist.

### TASK
Create a classification activity requiring pupils to justify groupings.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Materials; criteria; groups; reasoning; answers.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Every grouping must follow the stated criteria.

---

## MC-03 — Solids, liquids and gases
**Purpose:** Develop an age-appropriate model of states of matter.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; examples; representations.

### ROLE
Act as a primary chemistry specialist.

### TASK
Explain particle-level ideas only to the extent appropriate to the stated year group and connect them to observable behaviour.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Concept; model; examples; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not present simplified particle models as literal photographs of matter.

---

## MC-04 — Changes of state
**Purpose:** Teach melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation or related changes.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; change; examples; practical context.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create a concept and observation sequence explaining the named change.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Vocabulary; explanation; observation; questions; answers; misconception.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish changes of state from changes that create new substances.

## MC-05 — Reversible and irreversible change
**Purpose:** Compare changes and classify them appropriately.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; examples; curriculum objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary chemistry specialist.

### TASK
Create a classification and reasoning activity around reversible and irreversible changes.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Examples; classification; reasoning; answers; caveats.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check that each example genuinely fits the intended classification at the stated level.

## MC-06 — Dissolving and solutions
**Purpose:** Teach an appropriate primary concept about dissolving.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; solute; solvent; temperature; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Create teaching and practical work around dissolving, including appropriate observations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; model; method; recording table; questions; answers; safety.

## MC-07 — Separation techniques
**Purpose:** Teach a suitable method for separating mixtures.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; mixture; available equipment; method.

### ROLE
Act as a primary chemistry specialist.

### TASK
Select and explain a suitable separation method and the property it exploits.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Mixture; method; scientific principle; procedure; questions; answers; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the proposed method is physically capable of separating the supplied mixture.

---

## MC-08 — Materials investigation
**Purpose:** Plan a fair comparison of materials for a stated use.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; materials; property; use; equipment.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher.

### TASK
Design a comparative test and recording plan.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Question; variables; method; data table; conclusion; safety.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The measured property must be relevant to the stated use.

---

## MC-09 — Materials misconception diagnosis
**Purpose:** Diagnose misconceptions about properties, states or changes.

**Editable Variables:** Year group; pupil responses; objective.

### ROLE
Act as a primary science diagnostic specialist.

### TASK
Analyse responses and design a discriminating diagnostic sequence.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed error; hypothesis; evidence; diagnostic task; teaching response; follow-up.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not infer the cause of an error without evidence.

---

## MC-10 — Adapt materials task
**Purpose:** Adapt a materials activity while preserving the science.

**Editable Variables:** Original task; year group; access barrier; objective.

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary science teacher specialising in adaptive teaching.

### TASK
Reduce unnecessary language, visual or procedural barriers while preserving the intended scientific thinking.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; barrier; adapted task; support; preserved demand; independence route.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Confirm the adaptation has not changed the scientific question or evidence required.
