# Inclusion, Progression & Classroom Implementation Workflows

**IDs:** IP-01 to IP-10

## IP-01 — Identify Access Barriers in an Art Task
**ROLE:** Inclusive primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Activity, learning objective, materials and known access information supplied. **TASK:** Identify barriers that may arise in participation, perception, communication, motor access, planning or sensory experience. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not infer needs that are not evidenced; distinguish barriers created by the task from assumptions about the pupil. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, evidence, adaptation options, retained learning. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specific and proportionate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## IP-02 — Adapt Response Modes Without Lowering the Construct
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Intended artistic learning and access barrier supplied. **TASK:** Offer alternative ways to record, communicate or demonstrate learning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve the underlying visual, technical or creative decision wherever possible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Original construct, barrier, adaptation, equivalent evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Access adaptation is distinguished from reduced demand. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.

## IP-03 — Scaffold an Art Process and Plan Fade-Out
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Task, pupil starting point and intended independence supplied. **TASK:** Design temporary scaffolds and explicit conditions for reducing them. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid permanent over-support or replacing pupil decisions with adult decisions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Starting evidence, scaffold, practice, fade trigger, independence check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency increases over time. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/small group.

## IP-04 — Preserve Creative Agency During Support
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil needs support with generating, recording or developing ideas. **TASK:** Create support that expands access while keeping meaningful choices with the pupil. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate help with execution from ownership of artistic decisions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, support, pupil decisions retained, review question. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No substitute authorship. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## IP-05 — Plan Vocabulary Support in Art
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** New artistic vocabulary, year group and activity supplied. **TASK:** Embed vocabulary in looking, making and discussion. **REQUIREMENTS:** Prioritise usable understanding over isolated word memorisation; provide accessible explanations without distorting meaning. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Term, pupil-friendly meaning, visual/practical encounter, reuse prompt, check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Subject meaning retained. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** EAL/KS1/KS2.

## IP-06 — Map Progression Across an Art Unit
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Starting knowledge, intended end point and unit content supplied. **TASK:** Sequence development in knowledge, technique, decision-making and reflection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not assume progression is simply producing more detailed or realistic outcomes. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Starting point, intermediate evidence, end evidence, progression checks. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Progression is observable and relevant. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Unit length.

## IP-07 — Increase Challenge Through Artistic Depth
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupils are secure with a basic technique or process. **TASK:** Increase challenge through constraints, comparison, intentionality, experimentation or justification. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not use extra volume of work as the default definition of challenge. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Secure learning, deeper challenge, evidence, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Greater depth, not merely more work. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium/year group.

## IP-08 — Organise Practical Art Classroom Routines
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Class size, materials, workspace and activity supplied. **TASK:** Plan setup, distribution, making, cleanup and transition routines. **REQUIREMENTS:** Keep routines proportionate so logistics do not overwhelm artistic learning; include realistic contingencies. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Before, during, transition, cleanup, contingency. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible in an ordinary classroom. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** One-off/unit.

## IP-09 — Plan Group or Collaborative Art-Making
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Shared outcome, group size and artistic objective supplied. **TASK:** Structure collaboration while preserving individual contribution and decision-making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid allocating purely decorative roles; define how disagreements and artistic alternatives are handled. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Shared purpose, roles/process, decision protocol, evidence, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Meaningful participation. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## IP-10 — Review Classroom Implementation Evidence
**ROLE:** Primary subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Lesson observations, pupil voice, artwork/process evidence and teacher reflections supplied. **TASK:** Identify supported strengths, barriers and next actions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate evidence from interpretation and avoid generalising from isolated examples. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, finding, confidence, next action, evidence to collect. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Proportionate conclusions. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Class/year-group/whole-school.
