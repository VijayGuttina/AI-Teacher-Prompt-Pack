# Curriculum Planning, Exhibitions & Subject Leadership Workflows

**IDs:** CL-01 to CL-10

## CL-01 — Map an Art Curriculum Sequence
**ROLE:** Primary art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Year groups, curriculum expectations, existing units and local priorities supplied. **TASK:** Map coherent progression across knowledge, techniques, materials, artists and creative decision-making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish planned coverage from demonstrated learning and avoid treating progression as increasing realism alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Sequence, progression statements, dependencies, evidence points, review questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherent and manageable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Whole-school/key stage.

## CL-02 — Audit Curriculum Coverage and Balance
**ROLE:** Primary art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Current curriculum map supplied. **TASK:** Identify supported strengths, gaps, repetition and imbalance. **REQUIREMENTS:** Consider breadth of media, cultural contexts, disciplinary knowledge and creative processes without imposing artificial quotas. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, finding, confidence, implication, priority. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unsupported conclusions. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CL-03 — Design a Coherent Art Unit
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group, duration, artistic focus, prior learning and resources supplied. **TASK:** Build a sequence from introduction and exploration to application, reflection and evidence of learning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include genuine pupil choice and purposeful technical development. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** End point, lesson sequence, vocabulary, assessment evidence, resources, adaptations. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible and progressive. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 3/6/8 lessons.

## CL-04 — Build a Progression Model for Skills and Knowledge
**ROLE:** Primary subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Technique, concept or process across multiple year groups supplied. **TASK:** Define what changes over time in understanding, control, independence, decision-making and reflection. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid vague labels such as “basic” or “advanced” without observable evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Starting evidence, progression steps, secure indicators, common confusions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Observable and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium/discipline.

## CL-05 — Plan an Exhibition With Educational Purpose
**ROLE:** Primary art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Pupil work, audience, venue and constraints supplied. **TASK:** Plan selection, presentation and interpretation of work. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid selecting only conventionally polished outcomes; represent process, experimentation and diverse artistic approaches where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Purpose, selection principles, display plan, pupil voice, practical logistics, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Achievable and equitable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Class/year-group/whole-school.

## CL-06 — Curate Pupil Work Ethically
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Proposed display or publication and pupil work supplied. **TASK:** Establish transparent criteria for inclusion, attribution, permissions and contextualisation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Protect pupil ownership and dignity; do not publicly label work by perceived ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, permissions, attribution, contextual notes, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Fair and respectful. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Internal/public display.

## CL-07 — Conduct an Art Subject Review
**ROLE:** Primary subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Evidence from planning, lesson observation, pupil work, pupil voice and staff feedback supplied. **TASK:** Produce an evidence-led review of curriculum implementation and impact. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate evidence from inference and identify missing evidence before making broad judgements. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence base, findings, confidence, strengths, risks, priorities. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Proportionate conclusions. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Termly/annual.

## CL-08 — Create an Art Improvement Plan
**ROLE:** Primary subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Review findings, capacity, timescale and priorities supplied. **TASK:** Turn evidence into a focused improvement plan. **REQUIREMENTS:** Limit initiatives to what can realistically be implemented and evaluated. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Priority, rationale, actions, owner, evidence of progress, review point. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specific and measurable without false precision. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Term/year.

## CL-09 — Plan Staff Development in Art
**ROLE:** Primary subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Identified staff development need and available time supplied. **TASK:** Design practical professional learning that improves teacher knowledge or classroom implementation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include rehearsal or application and evidence of changed practice; do not rely on one-off information delivery alone. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Need, learning objective, activity, classroom application, follow-up evidence. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Realistic and transferable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Staff meeting/INSET/coaching.

## CL-10 — Prepare an Evidence-Based Subject Report
**ROLE:** Primary art subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Collected evidence and intended audience supplied. **TASK:** Summarise current position, demonstrated strengths, risks and next priorities. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish evidence, interpretation and recommendation; state uncertainty where evidence is limited. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Executive summary, evidence, findings, priorities, next review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Concise, defensible and actionable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Senior leaders/governors.
