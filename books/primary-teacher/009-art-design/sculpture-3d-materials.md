# Sculpture, 3D & Materials Workflows

## SM-01 — Plan a 3D Form Exploration

**Prompt Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Sculpture, 3D, Form, Materials

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[THEME]` | `[MATERIALS]` | `[TIME]` | `[OBJECTIVE]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher specialising in three-dimensional practice.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` are exploring `[THEME]` using `[MATERIALS]` for `[TIME]`. The intended learning is `[OBJECTIVE]`.

### TASK
Design a practical exploration of three-dimensional form that prioritises material and structural decisions over producing a prescribed object.

### REQUIREMENTS
Include a short demonstration, exploratory making, vocabulary, reflection and practical checks. Allow varied solutions. Distinguish what the material permits from what is an artistic preference.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; preparation; demonstration; exploration; teacher prompts; pupil choices; assessment; reflection; adaptations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Check feasibility, safety, age appropriateness and genuine variation in outcomes.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for KS1, KS2, individual or collaborative making.

### Example Input
Year 4; theme: shelter; card, paper and masking tape; 45 minutes; objective: explore how form can create enclosed and open spaces.

### Example Output
Pupils test folded, rolled and joined card forms before selecting a structure that creates a chosen balance of enclosed and open space. Assess the relationship between material decisions and form, not resemblance to a model.

## SM-02 — Explore Form, Mass and Space

**Prompt Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Sculpture, Form, Mass, Space

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[MATERIALS]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[TIME]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary Art & Design teacher with practical sculpture expertise.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` have `[MATERIALS]` available and are learning `[OBJECTIVE]` in `[TIME]`.

### TASK
Create an investigation into positive/negative space, mass and form using observable examples and practical tests.

### REQUIREMENTS
Use age-appropriate language first and introduce specialist terminology only when useful. Include comparison between different forms and require pupils to explain an observation.

Objective; demonstration; investigation; questions; application; assessment; reflection.

## SM-03 — Select Materials for a Sculptural Intention

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, Sculpture, Materials, Design Decisions

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[INTENDED_EFFECT]` | `[AVAILABLE_MATERIALS]` | `[CONSTRAINTS]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary sculpture teacher.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` need to achieve `[INTENDED_EFFECT]` using `[AVAILABLE_MATERIALS]` within `[CONSTRAINTS]`.

### TASK
Compare material properties and guide pupils towards an evidence-based material choice.

### REQUIREMENTS
Consider stiffness, flexibility, weight, joining, texture, durability and other relevant properties. Separate measurable/practical properties from aesthetic preference. Keep the final choice with the pupil where appropriate.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Intention; criteria; material comparison; quick tests; choice; justification; review.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Material claims must be accurate and relevant to the actual resources.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for clay, card, wire or recycled materials.

### Example Input
Year 5; intended effect: lightweight curved structure; card, foil and wire available; 50 minutes.

### Example Output
Pupils test bendability, rigidity and joining before selecting a material. They justify the choice in relation to the intended curve rather than choosing solely because they prefer its appearance.

**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, Sculpture, Construction, Joining, Materials

`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[MATERIALS]` | `[CONSTRUCTION_OBJECTIVE]` | `[TIME]`

### ROLE

### CONTEXT

### REQUIREMENTS

## SM-05 — Develop Balance and Stability

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, Sculpture, Balance, Stability, Experimentation

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[MATERIALS]` | `[FORM]` | `[TIME]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary sculpture teacher.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` are building `[FORM]` using `[MATERIALS]` for `[TIME]`.

### TASK
Design a prediction-test-refine sequence exploring how base, mass distribution and support affect stability.

### REQUIREMENTS
Require pupils to test rather than memorise a single rule. Record observations and use them to make one structural change at a time where practical.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Prediction; build; test; evidence; refinement; reflection; assessment.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Distinguish evidence from generalisation and recognise that different forms may require different solutions.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for KS1 or KS2.

### Example Input
Year 5; card tower; pupils have 30 minutes.

### Example Output
Ask pupils to predict which base will resist tipping, test two versions and record what changed. Use the results to guide a single purposeful modification.

## SM-07 — Plan Sculptural Iteration

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, Sculpture, Prototyping, Iteration, Reflection

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PROTOTYPE]` | `[INTENDED_EFFECT]` | `[TIME]` | `[CRITERIA]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary Art & Design teacher who uses iterative making and reflection.

### CONTEXT
Pupils have `[PROTOTYPE]` and are trying to achieve `[INTENDED_EFFECT]` within `[TIME]`. Relevant criteria are `[CRITERIA]`.

### TASK
Build a sequence of test, evidence, decision and modification.

### REQUIREMENTS
Make the evidence for changing the work observable. Keep pupil decisions central and allow retaining an existing feature when evidence supports it.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Baseline; test; evidence; decision; modification; reflection; assessment.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Iteration must have a reason. Avoid prescribing change merely for the sake of improvement.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual or group work.

### Example Input
Year 6; card sculpture prototype; intended effect: appear balanced but asymmetrical; 60 minutes.

### Example Output
Photograph or sketch the baseline, identify one stability and one visual criterion, test the form and decide whether modification is warranted. The pupil explains why the chosen change should improve the intended effect.

## SM-08 — Diagnose a 3D Construction Problem

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, Sculpture, Diagnostic Assessment, Construction

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[MATERIAL]` | `[OBSERVED_PROBLEM]` | `[STRUCTURE]` | `[RECORDED_EVIDENCE]`

### ROLE
Act as a diagnostic primary Art & Design teacher.

### CONTEXT
A `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupil's `[STRUCTURE]` made from `[MATERIAL]` shows `[OBSERVED_PROBLEM]`. Evidence available is `[RECORDED_EVIDENCE]`.

### TASK
Identify plausible material, structural and process causes and design a discriminating test.

### REQUIREMENTS
Separate observation from hypothesis. Consider joints, material behaviour, load, support, sequence and tool use. Do not assume carelessness or lack of ability.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; hypotheses; discriminating test; intervention; review criterion.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The diagnosis must be testable and proportionate to the evidence.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual or group troubleshooting.

### Example Input
Year 5; card tower repeatedly collapses at the same joint; card and tape; photographs show the joint bends before collapse.

### Example Output
The repeated location supports a joint-related hypothesis, but test it by reinforcing only that joint in a small comparison build. If stability improves, reteach joining rather than assuming the whole design is flawed.

## SM-09 — Critique Sculpture Without Prescribing Taste

**Prompt Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 400–700 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS2, Sculpture, Critique, Feedback, Artistic Agency

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[INTENTION]` | `[MATERIAL_CHOICES]` | `[CONSTRUCTION_CHOICES]` | `[CRITERIA]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary Art & Design teacher and critique facilitator.

### CONTEXT
A `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupil has made material choices `[MATERIAL_CHOICES]` and construction choices `[CONSTRUCTION_CHOICES]` in pursuit of `[INTENTION]`, assessed against `[CRITERIA]`.

### TASK
Generate critique that focuses on observable form, material and construction decisions and proposes a useful next investigation.

### REQUIREMENTS
Do not substitute teacher taste for the pupil's intention. Identify evidence and ask a question that returns decision-making to the pupil.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; effective decision; question; next experiment; reflection.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Be specific, respectful and agency-preserving.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Provide peer, self-critique or teacher-conference versions.

### Example Input
Year 6; asymmetrical wire sculpture; intended effect: tension; criteria include balance and visible line.

### Example Output
Comment on how the asymmetry and line create or fail to create the intended tension, then ask what small structural or spatial change the pupil would test next.

## SM-10 — Adapt 3D Art for Inclusion

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Sculpture, Inclusion, SEND, Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[TASK]` | `[ACCESS_BARRIER]` | `[CORE_OBJECTIVE]` | `[AVAILABLE_RESOURCES]`

### ROLE
Act as an inclusive primary Art & Design teacher with practical 3D teaching experience.

### CONTEXT
A `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupil is completing `[TASK]`. The access barrier is `[ACCESS_BARRIER]`; the core objective is `[CORE_OBJECTIVE]`; resources are `[AVAILABLE_RESOURCES]`.

### TASK
Design proportionate adaptations involving materials, scale, positioning, tools, timing or collaboration while preserving meaningful sculptural decision-making.

### REQUIREMENTS
Distinguish access support from changing the artistic construct. Offer more than one option where appropriate and retain pupil choice. Include relevant safety considerations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; retained objective; adaptation options; pupil choice; evidence of learning; independence route; safety; review point.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not replace the pupil's creative decisions with adult decisions. Ensure adaptations are practical and safe.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for fine-motor, physical, visual, communication or recording barriers.

### Example Input
Year 4; card construction; difficulty manipulating small joins; objective: explore balance and form; larger card pieces and pre-cut tabs available.

### Example Output
Offer larger components and pre-cut joining tabs while retaining the pupil's decisions about arrangement, balance and form. Assess those decisions rather than precision of cutting. Review whether support can be reduced as independence increases.
