# Sculpture, 3D & Materials Workflows

**IDs:** SM-01 to SM-10

## SM-01 — Plan a 3D Form Exploration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme, year group, materials and time supplied. **TASK:** Design an exploration of three-dimensional form. **REQUIREMENTS:** Allow varied solutions and focus on material/form decisions rather than a prescribed finished object. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective, exploration, vocabulary, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SM-02 — Explore Form, Mass and Space
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Sculpture materials and objective supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils investigate positive and negative space, mass, balance and form. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use observable examples and avoid unnecessary specialist terminology. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, investigation, questions, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Conceptual clarity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SM-03 — Select Materials for a Sculptural Intention
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Intended visual/structural effect and available materials supplied. **TASK:** Compare material properties relevant to the intention. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish material properties from aesthetic preference. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, comparison, choice, justification. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Material claims are accurate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Clay/card/wire/recycled materials.

## SM-04 — Teach Joining and Construction Techniques
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Materials and construction objective supplied. **TASK:** Sequence appropriate joining techniques. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe handling and account for material compatibility. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, guided practice, construction sequence, checks. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible and safe. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Materials.

## SM-05 — Develop Balance and Stability
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Freestanding sculpture task supplied. **TASK:** Explore how base, mass distribution and support affect stability. **REQUIREMENTS:** Test claims through practical comparison rather than presenting one rule as universal. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Prediction, build/test, evidence, refinement. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## SM-06 — Explore Texture in 3D Work
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Sculptural material and theme supplied. **TASK:** Create experiments with surface texture. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish physical surface texture from visual suggestion. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiments, vocabulary, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Material feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Clay/paper/found materials.

## SM-07 — Plan Sculptural Iteration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Prototype and intended effect supplied. **TASK:** Build a sequence of test, reflection and modification. **REQUIREMENTS:** Keep pupil decisions central and identify observable criteria for change. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Baseline, test, evidence, modification, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Iteration is purposeful. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## SM-08 — Diagnose a 3D Construction Problem
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil sculpture and observed problem supplied. **TASK:** Identify plausible material, structural or process causes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate evidence from hypothesis and avoid assuming carelessness or ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, discriminating test, intervention. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## SM-09 — Critique Sculpture Without Prescribing Taste
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Sculpture, intention and criteria supplied. **TASK:** Generate critique focused on material choices, form, construction and intended effect. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not equate teacher preference with artistic quality. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, question, next experiment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specific and agency-preserving. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Peer/self critique.

## SM-10 — Adapt 3D Art for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Sculpture activity and access need supplied. **TASK:** Remove barriers through materials, scale, positioning, tools or collaborative options. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve meaningful sculptural decision-making and safety. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained objective, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency and construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
