# Digital Art & Cross-Curricular Integration Workflows

**IDs:** DC-01 to DC-10

## DC-01 — Plan a Digital Art Exploration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group, available digital tools, artistic objective and time supplied. **TASK:** Design an exploration using digital media as an artistic medium. **REQUIREMENTS:** Start with visual and creative intent rather than the novelty of the software. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective, tool use, exploration sequence, pupil choices, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical and tool-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DC-02 — Compare Digital and Physical Media
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** A visual objective and available physical/digital media supplied. **TASK:** Create an investigation into what each medium enables, constrains or changes. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid presenting digital media as inherently superior or merely as a substitute. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison criteria, experiments, observations, choice, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Medium-specific reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Drawing/image manipulation/animation.

## DC-03 — Teach Layers, Selection and Revision
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Digital tool and artistic task supplied. **TASK:** Sequence a practical activity using layers, selection or reversible editing where supported. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect technical operations to visual decisions and revision. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, guided practice, independent choices, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Tool instructions match the stated capability. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Software/device.

## DC-04 — Plan a Digital Image Manipulation Enquiry
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Source image, year group and learning objective supplied. **TASK:** Design an enquiry into cropping, colour, layering, scale or other permitted manipulation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Address authorship, permissions and representation where relevant; do not imply unrestricted reuse of images. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Source context, experiment, ethical checks, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Legally and developmentally appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## DC-05 — Explore Animation or Sequential Visual Storytelling
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme, available tools and lesson time supplied. **TASK:** Plan an activity using sequence, timing and visual change. **REQUIREMENTS:** Keep artistic planning distinct from technical button-following. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Planning, frames/scenes, making sequence, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Achievable within constraints. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Stop-motion/digital animation.

## DC-06 — Integrate Art With Another Subject Meaningfully
**ROLE:** Primary curriculum designer. **CONTEXT:** Art objective and another subject objective supplied. **TASK:** Design an integrated activity that advances both disciplines where appropriate. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not use decorative artwork as evidence of unrelated subject learning; state what evidence belongs to each subject. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Dual objectives, authentic connection, activity, evidence, boundaries. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Neither subject is diluted. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** History/science/geography/English.

## DC-07 — Use Visualisation to Support Subject Learning
**ROLE:** Primary teacher. **CONTEXT:** Non-art concept and visual-art objective supplied. **TASK:** Create a visualisation or making task that supports conceptual understanding. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate artistic quality criteria from subject-concept criteria. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Concept, visual strategy, making task, evidence checks. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct boundaries explicit. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## DC-08 — Diagnose Digital Art Access or Process Problems
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil process, tool, observed difficulty and intended learning supplied. **TASK:** Identify plausible causes involving tool access, technical understanding, visual decision-making or task design. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not assume a technical problem reflects low artistic understanding. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, discriminating check, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## DC-09 — Evaluate Digital Art Ethically and Critically
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Digital artwork or image practice supplied. **TASK:** Create age-appropriate questions about authorship, editing, representation, ownership or audience. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid simplistic claims that editing is inherently deceptive or that digital work is inherently less authentic. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, questions, evidence, discussion boundaries, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Nuanced and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## DC-10 — Adapt Digital and Cross-Curricular Art for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Activity, intended learning, available technology and access barrier supplied. **TASK:** Remove barriers while preserving meaningful artistic and, where relevant, disciplinary decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Provide alternatives when technology itself becomes the barrier; do not lower the core construct by default. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained objective, equivalent evidence, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Access and construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
