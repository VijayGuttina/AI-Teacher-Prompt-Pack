# Assessment, Critique, Misconceptions & Intervention Workflows

## AC-01 — Create an Objective-Aligned Art Assessment

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Assessment, Art, Construct Validity

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[LEARNING_OBJECTIVE]` | `[MEDIUM]` | `[AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE]` | `[ASSESSMENT_PURPOSE]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher and assessment specialist.

### CONTEXT
The assessment is for `[YEAR_GROUP]`. The intended learning is `[LEARNING_OBJECTIVE]`, using `[MEDIUM]`. Available evidence is `[AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE]`; the purpose is `[ASSESSMENT_PURPOSE]`.

### TASK
Design an assessment that captures the intended artistic construct and specifies what evidence can and cannot support.

### REQUIREMENTS
Assess the stated learning rather than neatness, speed, compliance, confidence, quantity of work or resemblance to a teacher exemplar unless these are explicitly part of the objective. Include practical evidence collection.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Construct; evidence criteria; collection method; interpretation rules; confidence limits; feedback use; adaptations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the evidence is construct-valid, proportionate and feasible in a classroom.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for formative, summative, individual or whole-class assessment.

### Example Input
Year 4; objective: use line variation to communicate movement; drawing; sketchbook experiments and final outcome; formative assessment.

### Example Output
Assess evidence of purposeful line choices and the pupil's ability to explain or demonstrate how variation affects movement. Do not infer achievement from neatness or drawing realism.

## AC-02 — Create a Diagnostic Hinge Question

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 400–700 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Diagnostic Assessment, Misconceptions

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[CONCEPT_OR_TECHNIQUE]` | `[LIKELY_MISCONCEPTIONS]` | `[CONTEXT]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary Art & Design teacher designing diagnostic assessment.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` are learning `[CONCEPT_OR_TECHNIQUE]`. Likely misconceptions are `[LIKELY_MISCONCEPTIONS]`; the classroom context is `[CONTEXT]`.

### TASK
Create one hinge question or short task that distinguishes competing understandings and identifies the appropriate next teaching move.

### REQUIREMENTS
Include plausible response patterns, not merely one obviously incorrect answer. Explain what each response suggests and what evidence would still be needed before intervention.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Prompt; expected responses; diagnosis; teacher interpretation; next teaching move; follow-up check.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The task must genuinely discriminate understanding rather than measure memory or confidence alone.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for KS1, KS2, oral, visual or practical response.

### Example Input
Year 5; concept: overlap can create depth; misconceptions: all overlapping objects are automatically closer; classroom: collage.

### Example Output
Present contrasting arrangements and ask pupils to select which visual relationships create depth and explain why. Use the explanation to distinguish recognition from a memorised rule.

## AC-03 — Analyse Pupil Art Evidence

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Assessment, Evidence, Diagnostic Analysis

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PUPIL_WORK]` | `[PROCESS_EVIDENCE]` | `[STATED_OBJECTIVE]` | `[ADDITIONAL_CONTEXT]`

### ROLE
Act as a diagnostic primary Art & Design teacher.

### CONTEXT
A pupil in `[YEAR_GROUP]` has produced `[PUPIL_WORK]`. Process evidence is `[PROCESS_EVIDENCE]`; the objective is `[STATED_OBJECTIVE]`; additional context is `[ADDITIONAL_CONTEXT]`.

### TASK
Analyse what the available evidence supports, what remains uncertain and what additional check would improve confidence.

### REQUIREMENTS
Separate direct observation from inference. Avoid labels such as “untalented,” “careless” or “lacks creativity.” Consider whether missing evidence prevents a reliable conclusion.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observations; possible interpretations; confidence; missing evidence; next check; teaching implications.

### QUALITY CHECKS
No claim about understanding, motivation or ability may exceed the available evidence.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual, group or whole-class evidence.

### Example Input
Year 3; painting shows repeated colour mixing; process notes show several tests; objective: explore warm and cool colour relationships.

### Example Output
Identify observable colour relationships and experimentation. State separately whether the evidence is sufficient to conclude secure conceptual understanding, then propose a brief explanation or comparison task if needed.

## AC-04 — Diagnose a Technical Misconception

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Technique, Misconceptions, Diagnostic Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PUPIL_STATEMENT_OR_RESULT]` | `[MEDIUM_OR_TECHNIQUE]` | `[EXPECTED_LEARNING]` | `[ACCESS_CONTEXT]`

### ROLE
Act as a diagnostic primary Art & Design teacher with practical material knowledge.

### CONTEXT
A pupil in `[YEAR_GROUP]` shows or says `[PUPIL_STATEMENT_OR_RESULT]` while using `[MEDIUM_OR_TECHNIQUE]`. Expected learning is `[EXPECTED_LEARNING]`; access context is `[ACCESS_CONTEXT]`.

### TASK
Identify plausible conceptual, procedural, material or access-related explanations and design a short discriminating check.

### REQUIREMENTS
Do not assume misunderstanding before considering material properties, tool limitations, prior instruction or access barriers. Keep the check small and proportionate.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; hypotheses; discriminating task; expected outcomes; response; reassessment.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The diagnostic task must test a meaningful difference between hypotheses.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for drawing, painting, print, textiles or 3D work.

### Example Input
Year 4; repeated patchy prints; foam printing; expected learning: control ink loading and pressure; pupil has reduced hand strength.

### Example Output
Consider pressure, ink loading and access as separate hypotheses. Compare supported pressure with controlled ink loading before deciding whether reteaching or adaptation is required.

## AC-05 — Build a Critique Protocol

**Prompt Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Critique, Peer Assessment, Reflection

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[ARTWORK_STAGE]` | `[INTENDED_LEARNING]` | `[GROUP_SIZE]` | `[TIME]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher facilitating constructive critique.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` are at `[ARTWORK_STAGE]`. The intended learning is `[INTENDED_LEARNING]`; critique will occur in groups of `[GROUP_SIZE]` over `[TIME]`.

### TASK
Create a structured peer or self-critique that moves from observation to interpretation and response.

### REQUIREMENTS
Establish norms, evidence-based language and psychological safety. Do not require agreement about artistic value or impose a single aesthetic judgement. Capture one usable next action.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Purpose; norms; observation prompts; interpretation prompts; response prompts; next-action capture; teacher role; assessment.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure pupils can participate at the stated age and that comments remain specific to evidence.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for KS1, KS2, pair critique or silent gallery critique.

### Example Input
Year 6; mid-project mixed-media work; intended learning: composition communicates emphasis; groups of four; 15 minutes.

### Example Output
Begin with “What do you notice?” before “What effect might this create?” and finish with the maker choosing one idea to test, reject or retain.

## AC-06 — Give Evidence-Based Feedback

**Prompt Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 400–700 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Feedback, Formative Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[ARTWORK_OR_PROCESS_EVIDENCE]` | `[OBJECTIVE]` | `[CURRENT_STAGE]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher providing concise formative feedback.

### CONTEXT
A `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupil has evidence `[ARTWORK_OR_PROCESS_EVIDENCE]`. The objective is `[OBJECTIVE]`; the current stage is `[CURRENT_STAGE]`.

### TASK
Generate feedback linked to observable evidence and one actionable next experiment or decision.

### REQUIREMENTS
Avoid generic praise, talent labels and prescriptive statements based solely on teacher taste. Preserve the pupil's authorship.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence; significant strength or decision; why it matters; question; next experiment; review point.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Feedback must be specific, actionable and linked to the intended learning.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Provide written, verbal-conference or peer-feedback versions.

### Example Input
Year 5; collage uses strong overlap around one focal area; objective: create emphasis through composition; mid-project.

### Example Output
Reference the observable overlap, explain its effect on attention and ask the pupil to test whether simplifying another area strengthens or weakens the intended emphasis.

## AC-07 — Plan a Targeted Intervention

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Intervention, Assessment, Inclusion

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE]` | `[SPECIFIC_BARRIER]` | `[INTENDED_LEARNING]` | `[TIME_AVAILABLE]`

### ROLE
Act as a primary Art & Design teacher planning a targeted, evidence-led intervention.

### CONTEXT
Diagnostic evidence is `[DIAGNOSTIC_EVIDENCE]`. The specific barrier is `[SPECIFIC_BARRIER]`; intended learning is `[INTENDED_LEARNING]`; time available is `[TIME_AVAILABLE]`.

### TASK
Create a short intervention that addresses the identified barrier while retaining the intended artistic learning.

### REQUIREMENTS
Define entry evidence, the intervention itself, a check for improvement and an exit or fade-out condition. Avoid unnecessary reduction in creative demand.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Need; evidence; intervention; pupil task; reassessment; exit condition; fade-out; next step.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The intervention must address the diagnosed barrier rather than reteaching the whole topic by default.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for individual or small-group intervention.

### Example Input
Year 3; pupils can identify texture but do not vary marks intentionally; intended learning: select marks for surface effect; 15 minutes.

### Example Output
Use a brief comparison of three mark samples, require pupils to match and explain surface effects, then immediately retest the original objective in independent work before withdrawing support.

## AC-08 — Create a Retrieval and Re-teach Cycle

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Retrieval, Re-teaching, Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PREVIOUS_CONCEPT]` | `[CURRENT_EVIDENCE]` | `[TIME_AVAILABLE]` | `[MEDIUM]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher planning efficient retrieval and targeted re-teaching.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` previously learned `[PREVIOUS_CONCEPT]`. Current evidence is `[CURRENT_EVIDENCE]`; available time is `[TIME_AVAILABLE]`; medium is `[MEDIUM]`.

### TASK
Design retrieval, interpretation, a re-teach trigger, targeted practice and a recheck.

### REQUIREMENTS
Do not assume failure to recall immediately equals absence of understanding. Include more than one possible explanation for weak retrieval where evidence warrants it.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Retrieval task; response patterns; interpretation; re-teach trigger; targeted practice; recheck; next decision.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Keep the cycle efficient and evidence-led rather than automatically reteaching all content.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for a 10- or 20-minute sequence.

### Example Input
Year 4; previous concept: warm and cool colour; current evidence: pupils inconsistently label colours; 15 minutes; painting.

### Example Output
Use a visual sorting or mixing task before reteaching. If pupils can apply but not verbalise the distinction, address vocabulary separately from conceptual understanding.

## AC-09 — Produce an Evidence-Based Progress Summary

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 400–700 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Progress, Assessment, Reporting

### Editable Variables
`[PUPIL_OR_GROUP]` | `[EVIDENCE_SET]` | `[LEARNING_OBJECTIVES]` | `[AUDIENCE]` | `[TIME_PERIOD]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher synthesising evidence of development.

### CONTEXT
The subject is `[PUPIL_OR_GROUP]`. Evidence across `[TIME_PERIOD]` is `[EVIDENCE_SET]`; objectives are `[LEARNING_OBJECTIVES]`; audience is `[AUDIENCE]`.

### TASK
Summarise demonstrated development, secure learning, uncertainty and proportionate next priorities.

### REQUIREMENTS
Base claims on evidence across process and outcome where available. Avoid ranking pupils by innate ability or treating one finished piece as sufficient proof of broad capability.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Evidence base; demonstrated development; secure learning; uncertainty; next focus; confidence statement.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Claims must be proportionate to the evidence and suitable for the specified audience.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for pupil, parent or teacher-facing reporting.

### Example Input
Year 6 pupil; three sketchbooks and two final pieces; objectives: composition and material experimentation; audience: parent; one term.

### Example Output
Describe specific demonstrated changes across the evidence, identify where evidence remains limited and state one or two next priorities without using fixed talent labels.

## AC-10 — Adapt Assessment for Inclusion

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Inclusion, SEND, EAL, Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[ASSESSMENT_OBJECTIVE]` | `[ACCESS_BARRIER]` | `[CURRENT_EVIDENCE_METHOD]` | `[AVAILABLE_SUPPORTS]`

### ROLE
Act as an inclusive primary Art & Design teacher and assessment specialist.

### CONTEXT
The assessment objective for `[YEAR_GROUP]` is `[ASSESSMENT_OBJECTIVE]`. The access barrier is `[ACCESS_BARRIER]`; current evidence is collected through `[CURRENT_EVIDENCE_METHOD]`; supports available are `[AVAILABLE_SUPPORTS]`.

### TASK
Adapt evidence collection or response mode while preserving the assessed construct wherever possible.

### REQUIREMENTS
Distinguish access adaptation from lowering the learning being assessed. Consider alternative communication, demonstration, annotation, oral response or adapted tools where relevant.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; retained construct; adaptation options; evidence standard; comparability considerations; review point.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the adaptation improves access without measuring a different construct unintentionally.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for SEND, EAL, communication, motor or sensory access needs.

### Example Input
Year 5; objective: explain how composition creates emphasis; barrier: extended writing; current method: written paragraph; supports: oral recording and annotated image.

### Example Output
Retain the requirement to identify and justify compositional evidence while allowing oral explanation or image annotation. Assess the explanation of the construct, not writing stamina.
