# Assessment, Critique, Misconceptions & Intervention Workflows

**IDs:** AC-01 to AC-10

## AC-01 — Create an Objective-Aligned Art Assessment
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Learning objective, medium, year group and available evidence supplied. **TASK:** Design an assessment that captures the intended artistic learning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Assess the construct rather than neatness, speed, compliance or resemblance to an exemplar. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence criteria, prompts, collection method, interpretation boundaries. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Construct-valid and practical. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Formative/summative.

## AC-02 — Create a Diagnostic Hinge Question
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Specific concept or technique and likely misconception supplied. **TASK:** Create one question or short task that distinguishes competing understandings. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include plausible distractors or response patterns and teacher actions. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Prompt, expected responses, diagnosis, next teaching move. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Actually discriminates understanding. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AC-03 — Analyse Pupil Art Evidence
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil artwork, process evidence and stated objective supplied. **TASK:** Analyse what the evidence can and cannot support. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate observation from inference and identify missing evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Observations, possible interpretations, confidence, next check. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unsupported pupil labelling. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/class.

## AC-04 — Diagnose a Technical Misconception
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil statement or repeated process outcome supplied. **TASK:** Identify a likely conceptual or procedural misconception and design a short discriminating check. **REQUIREMENTS:** Consider material properties and access barriers before concluding misunderstanding. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, diagnostic task, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable and proportionate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium.

## AC-05 — Build a Critique Protocol
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Age group, artwork stage and intended learning supplied. **TASK:** Create a structured peer or self-critique. **REQUIREMENTS:** Move from observation to interpretation and response; do not require pupils to agree on artistic value. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Norms, prompts, sequence, next-action capture. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specific and psychologically safe. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AC-06 — Give Evidence-Based Feedback
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork/process evidence and objective supplied. **TASK:** Generate concise feedback linked to observable evidence and an actionable next experiment. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid generic praise, talent labels and prescriptive aesthetic judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, question, next step. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Actionable and agency-preserving. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Written/verbal.

## AC-07 — Plan a Targeted Intervention
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Diagnostic evidence identifies a specific barrier. **TASK:** Create a short intervention that addresses the barrier while retaining the intended artistic learning. **REQUIREMENTS:** Define entry evidence, intervention, check for improvement and exit condition. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Need, evidence, intervention, reassessment, fade-out. **QUALITY CHECKS:** No unnecessary reduction in creative demand. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/small group.

## AC-08 — Create a Retrieval and Re-teach Cycle
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Previously taught visual or technical concept and current evidence supplied. **TASK:** Design retrieval followed by diagnosis and targeted re-teaching. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not assume failure to recall equals absence of understanding. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Retrieval, interpretation, re-teach trigger, practice, recheck. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Efficient and evidence-led. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** 10/20 minutes.

## AC-09 — Produce an Evidence-Based Progress Summary
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Multiple pieces of artwork and process evidence supplied. **TASK:** Summarise demonstrated development, secure learning and next priorities. **REQUIREMENTS:** State confidence limits and avoid ranking pupils by innate ability. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, development, uncertainty, next focus. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Proportionate claims. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil/parent/teacher-facing.

## AC-10 — Adapt Assessment for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Assessment objective and access barrier supplied. **TASK:** Adapt evidence collection or response mode while preserving the construct where possible. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish access adaptation from lowering the assessed learning. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained construct, evidence standard. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Fair and valid. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
