# Art History, Artists & Cultural Context Workflows

**IDs:** AH-01 to AH-10

## AH-01 — Introduce an Artist Through Evidence
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artist, artwork and year group supplied. **TASK:** Build an evidence-led introduction. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish documented facts from interpretation and avoid invented quotations or biography. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, looking prompts, evidence, discussion, activity. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Factual restraint. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AH-02 — Analyse an Artwork Without Guessing Intention
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork image/details supplied. **TASK:** Guide pupils from observation to interpretation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Label visual observations separately from plausible interpretations; do not state artist intention without evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Observe, analyse, interpret, questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence discipline. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AH-03 — Compare Artists or Artworks
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Two artists/artworks supplied. **TASK:** Create a purposeful comparison. **REQUIREMENTS:** Compare identifiable features, processes, contexts or ideas rather than ranking artistic worth. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Comparison criteria, questions, pupil task, synthesis. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Fair comparison. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Period/theme.

## AH-04 — Teach Art in Cultural Context
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork/tradition and cultural context supplied. **TASK:** Explain relevant historical and cultural context accurately. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid treating a diverse culture as homogeneous and avoid presenting sacred or culturally significant practices as generic craft motifs. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, evidence, respectful questions, activity boundaries. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Cultural accuracy and respect. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Teacher-supplied sources.

## AH-05 — Check an Artist Biography for Reliability
**ROLE:** Primary subject leader. **CONTEXT:** Biography text supplied. **TASK:** Identify claims requiring verification. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not invent sources or silently correct uncertain facts. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Claim, confidence, evidence needed, safe classroom wording. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear uncertainty. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Pupil-facing/teacher-facing.

## AH-06 — Teach Historical Change in Art
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artists/artworks from different periods supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils identify continuity and change in materials, purposes, techniques or visual conventions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid simplistic progress narratives in which later art is automatically better. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Timeline, comparison, enquiry questions, synthesis. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Chronological accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## AH-07 — Connect Artist Context to Making
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artist, technique and classroom materials supplied. **TASK:** Create a making activity informed by the artist's practice without requiring imitation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve pupil agency and distinguish influence from copying. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, technique investigation, pupil choices, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Authentic connection. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium/theme.

## AH-08 — Diagnose Misconceptions About Art History
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil statement about an artist, period or artwork supplied. **TASK:** Identify the misunderstanding and design a corrective enquiry. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate factual error from interpretation or value judgement. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, misconception, diagnostic question, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence-based diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## AH-09 — Discuss Controversial or Difficult Art Contexts
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork, historical context and age group supplied. **TASK:** Frame a balanced, age-appropriate discussion. **REQUIREMENTS:** Provide historical context without endorsing harmful views; identify when content requires safeguarding or school policy consideration. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Context, vocabulary, questions, boundaries, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Accurate and proportionate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS2.

## AH-10 — Adapt Art-History Learning for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork/context task and access need supplied. **TASK:** Provide access routes to looking, discussing and interpreting art. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not reduce intellectual demand merely because the response mode changes. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained objective, response options. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Access and disciplinary integrity. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
