# Art & Design Representative Verification Test Suite

**Purpose:** Repeatable execution-QA fixtures for the 100 Art & Design workflows.

A fixture defines what to test. It is **not** evidence of a successful execution. Record actual executions in `VERIFICATION_REGISTER.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

Outputs should be teacher-usable, faithful to the supplied context, specific, practical, age-appropriate, technically responsible, inclusive without automatically lowering the construct, protective of pupil artistic agency, and explicit about uncertainty. Diagnostic and interpretation workflows must distinguish observation from inference. Historical/cultural workflows must not invent facts, quotations or artist intentions.
