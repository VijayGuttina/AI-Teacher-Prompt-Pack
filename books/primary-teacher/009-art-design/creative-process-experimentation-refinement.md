# Creative Process, Experimentation & Refinement Workflows

**IDs:** CP-01 to CP-10

## CP-01 — Plan an Open-Ended Creative Starting Point
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme, year group, media and time supplied. **TASK:** Create a starting activity that generates multiple possible visual directions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid prescribing a single finished outcome or aesthetic. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Provocation, materials, prompts, exploration sequence, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Genuine pupil choice. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CP-02 — Generate Productive Constraints
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Creative objective and available materials supplied. **TASK:** Design constraints that focus experimentation without dictating the final image or object. **REQUIREMENTS:** Explain what learning each constraint is intended to surface. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Constraint, rationale, task, release/fade condition. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Constraint supports rather than replaces agency. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Drawing/painting/3D.

## CP-03 — Plan an Artistic Experiment Sequence
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Technique, media and intended learning supplied. **TASK:** Sequence small experiments before a larger application. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include comparison and decision points; do not assume experimentation must converge on one “best” result. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiments, variables, evidence, choices, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Process is purposeful. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** One/two lessons.

## CP-04 — Turn Mistakes Into Enquiry
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Unexpected visual or material result supplied. **TASK:** Reframe the outcome as an investigation where appropriate. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not romanticise every error; distinguish accidental discovery from a process failure requiring correction. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Observation, possible responses, test, decision. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Evidence and artistic intention. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium.

## CP-05 — Support Artistic Decision-Making
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil has several possible directions and an intended effect. **TASK:** Provide questions that help the pupil compare options and make a reasoned choice. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not select the option for the pupil without a stated teaching reason. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Criteria, questions, comparison, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency retained. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## CP-06 — Plan Reflection During Making
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Practical project and lesson sequence supplied. **TASK:** Insert short reflection points that inform the next artistic decision. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use observable evidence and avoid generic “what went well” prompts only. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Checkpoint, evidence prompt, decision question, next action. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Reflection changes process. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## CP-07 — Teach Iteration and Refinement
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Initial outcome and intended visual effect supplied. **TASK:** Design a cycle of review, experiment and refinement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Clarify that refinement can involve simplification, replacement or retention, not only adding detail. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Baseline, review, experiment, decision, next version. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Meaningful iteration. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Medium.

## CP-08 — Diagnose Stalled Creative Process
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil process evidence and stated difficulty supplied. **TASK:** Identify plausible barriers such as idea generation, technique, decision overload or access. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not infer laziness, low ability or lack of motivation without evidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, diagnostic prompt, targeted support. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Hypotheses remain testable. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## CP-09 — Give Feedback on Process, Not Just Outcome
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Sketchbook/process evidence and intended objective supplied. **TASK:** Give feedback on experimentation, choices and refinement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Reference specific evidence and avoid generic praise or ranking. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, effective decision, question, next experiment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Actionable and specific. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Written/verbal.

## CP-10 — Adapt Creative Process for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Creative task and access need supplied. **TASK:** Remove barriers to idea generation, experimentation, decision-making or reflection while retaining meaningful artistic choices. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not confuse reduced output volume with reduced intellectual or creative demand. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained learning, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency and construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
