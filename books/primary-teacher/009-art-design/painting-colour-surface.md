# Painting, Colour & Surface Workflows

**IDs:** PC-01 to PC-10

## PC-01 — Plan a Colour Exploration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Year group, theme and media supplied. **TASK:** Design an experiment with colour relationships. **REQUIREMENTS:** Pupils make and compare choices rather than reproduce a single model. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective, experiment, vocabulary, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Age-appropriate and practical. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## PC-02 — Teach Primary, Secondary & Mixed Colours
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Existing pupil knowledge and paint media supplied. **TASK:** Build a practical colour-mixing investigation. **REQUIREMENTS:** Explain the relevant colour model simply and avoid claiming all pigments behave identically. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, experiment, observations, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Technically accurate and media-aware. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Paint type.

## PC-03 — Explore Warm and Cool Colour Effects
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Artwork/theme supplied. **TASK:** Investigate how warm/cool colour choices can influence visual effect. **REQUIREMENTS:** Present expressive effects as possibilities, not universal psychological rules. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiment, comparison, discussion prompts. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Avoid overclaiming. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## PC-04 — Teach Colour Value and Contrast
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Painting objective supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils identify and manipulate light/dark relationships. **REQUIREMENTS:** Connect choices to visibility, emphasis or composition where appropriate. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, practice, application, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear distinction between hue and value. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Paint/media.

## PC-05 — Explore Texture and Surface
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Available media and surface supplied. **TASK:** Create controlled experiments with texture. **REQUIREMENTS:** Distinguish actual texture from visual texture. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiments, vocabulary, design application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Material feasibility. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## PC-06 — Develop Brush and Paint Control
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Age, brushes and paint supplied. **TASK:** Sequence practice in loading, pressure, direction and mark-making. **REQUIREMENTS:** Adapt to the actual media and equipment available. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, drills, application, cleanup routine. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safe and practical. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Media.

## PC-07 — Plan Layering and Refinement
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Painting project and drying constraints supplied. **TASK:** Sequence layers and refinement decisions. **REQUIREMENTS:** Account for drying and avoid unnecessary irreversible steps. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Layer sequence, checkpoints, refinement questions. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible process. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** One/two lessons.

## PC-08 — Diagnose Colour-Mixing Problems
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil colour experiment and materials supplied. **TASK:** Identify plausible reasons for an unexpected colour result. **REQUIREMENTS:** Consider pigment/media properties before attributing error to pupil misunderstanding. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, discriminating experiment, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## PC-09 — Critique Painting Without Prescribing Style
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil painting and objective supplied. **TASK:** Generate specific feedback about choices, experimentation and technique. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not treat resemblance to a teacher exemplar as the sole criterion. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, question, next experiment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Artistic agency preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Written/verbal.

## PC-10 — Adapt Painting for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Painting activity and access need supplied. **TASK:** Remove barriers through tools, scale, positioning, timing or response alternatives. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve meaningful colour/surface decision-making where possible. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained objective, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency and construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
