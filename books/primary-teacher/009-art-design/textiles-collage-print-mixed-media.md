## TC-01 — Plan a Collage Exploration

**Prompt Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 500–800 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Collage, Composition, Mixed Media

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[THEME]` | `[MATERIALS]` | `[TIME]` | `[OBJECTIVE]`

### ROLE
Act as an experienced primary Art & Design teacher specialising in collage and material exploration.

### CONTEXT
Pupils in `[YEAR_GROUP]` are exploring `[THEME]` using `[MATERIALS]` for `[TIME]`. The intended learning is `[OBJECTIVE]`.

### TASK
Design a practical collage exploration that develops composition through selecting, arranging, layering and revising materials.

### REQUIREMENTS
- Begin with movable experiments before permanent attachment where practical.
- Include purposeful vocabulary and teacher prompts.
- Require pupils to make and explain at least one compositional decision.
- Allow multiple valid outcomes.
- Do not treat neatness or resemblance to an exemplar as the default success criterion.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Objective; preparation; demonstration; exploratory arrangement; pupil choices; application; reflection; assessment; adaptations.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Ensure the activity is feasible, age-appropriate and preserves artistic agency.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for KS1, KS2, recycled materials or a 20-minute session.

### Example Input
Year 2; local environment; coloured paper, magazines and natural textures; 40 minutes; objective: arrange shapes to create emphasis.

### Example Output
Pupils create two temporary arrangements before choosing which relationship to develop. The teacher asks what the eye notices first and why, then assesses intentional placement rather than tidiness alone.

## TC-08 — Diagnose Process Problems in Print or Textile Work

**Prompt Difficulty:** Advanced  
**AI Model Compatibility:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot  
**Expected Output Length:** 600–900 words  
**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Printmaking, Textiles, Diagnostic Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[PROCESS]` | `[MATERIALS]` | `[OBSERVED_RESULT]` | `[EXPECTED_RESULT]`

### ROLE
Act as a diagnostic primary Art & Design teacher.

### CONTEXT
A `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupil using `[PROCESS]` and `[MATERIALS]` produced `[OBSERVED_RESULT]` when `[EXPECTED_RESULT]` was intended.

### TASK
Identify plausible process, material and conceptual causes, then design a discriminating test before recommending a teaching response.

### REQUIREMENTS
Separate evidence from hypotheses. Consider pressure, loading, alignment, material condition, sequence, tool use and understanding where relevant. Do not assume pupil carelessness or lack of confidence.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Observed evidence; hypotheses; discriminating test; response; review criterion.

### QUALITY CHECKS
The proposed test must isolate a meaningful variable where possible and be feasible in the classroom.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for an individual, pair or whole-class issue.

### Example Input
Year 4; foam printing; patchy print; expected solid transfer; pupils used uneven ink and variable pressure.

### Example Output
Run a small comparison controlling ink loading while varying pressure. Use the results to determine whether loading or pressure is the stronger cause before reteaching.

**Curriculum Tags:** KS1, KS2, Mixed Media, Inclusion, SEND, Assessment

### Editable Variables
`[YEAR_GROUP]` | `[ACTIVITY]` | `[ACCESS_BARRIER]` | `[CORE_OBJECTIVE]` | `[AVAILABLE_RESOURCES]`

### ROLE
Act as an inclusive primary Art & Design teacher with practical knowledge of mixed-media activities.

### CONTEXT
A `[YEAR_GROUP]` pupil is completing `[ACTIVITY]`. The access barrier is `[ACCESS_BARRIER]`; the core objective is `[CORE_OBJECTIVE]`; resources available are `[AVAILABLE_RESOURCES]`.

### TASK
Design proportionate adaptations involving tools, material size, positioning, timing, preparation or response methods while preserving meaningful artistic choices.

### REQUIREMENTS
Distinguish access support from changing the construct. Offer alternatives rather than assuming one solution fits every pupil. Include relevant safety considerations.

### OUTPUT FORMAT
Barrier; retained objective; adaptation options; pupil choice; evidence of learning; independence route; safety; review point.

### QUALITY CHECKS
Do not replace the pupil's authorship or unnecessarily reduce artistic demand.

### OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION
Adapt for fine-motor, physical, visual, communication, language or recording barriers.

### Example Input
Year 3; collage; difficulty cutting small shapes; objective: arrange shapes intentionally; larger pre-cut materials and tearing tools available.

### Example Output
Offer larger pre-cut pieces or tearing as access routes while retaining the pupil's decisions about selection, placement, overlap and composition. Assess those decisions rather than cutting precision.
