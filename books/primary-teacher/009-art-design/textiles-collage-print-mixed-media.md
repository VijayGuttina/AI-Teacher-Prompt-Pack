# Textiles, Collage, Print & Mixed Media Workflows

**IDs:** TC-01 to TC-10

## TC-01 — Plan a Collage Exploration
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Theme, year group, materials and time supplied. **TASK:** Design an exploration of composition through collage. **REQUIREMENTS:** Allow pupils to select, layer and rearrange materials rather than reproduce a fixed composition. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Objective, experiment, vocabulary, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Practical and age-appropriate. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## TC-02 — Explore Shape, Layering & Composition
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Collage materials and visual objective supplied. **TASK:** Help pupils investigate how scale, overlap, placement and negative space affect composition. **REQUIREMENTS:** Treat compositional decisions as choices rather than rules. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Demonstration, experiment, questions, application. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Clear visual reasoning. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## TC-03 — Plan a Printmaking Experiment
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Printing process, materials and year group supplied. **TASK:** Sequence an experiment exploring repeat, pressure, registration or mark-making as appropriate. **REQUIREMENTS:** Adapt to the actual process and equipment available. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Setup, demonstration, experiment, application, cleanup. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Safe and feasible. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Relief/stamp/monoprint.

## TC-04 — Teach Positive and Negative Print Areas
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Printing process supplied. **TASK:** Make positive/negative relationships observable through a practical task. **REQUIREMENTS:** Use process-appropriate language and examples. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Explanation, demonstration, test, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Conceptual accuracy. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## TC-05 — Explore Pattern and Repetition
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pattern theme and media supplied. **TASK:** Investigate repetition, variation, rhythm and arrangement. **REQUIREMENTS:** Allow intentional irregularity and avoid presenting symmetry as mandatory. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiments, vocabulary, design application, review. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Artistic agency preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Print/textile/collage.

## TC-06 — Plan a Textile Surface Experiment
**ROLE:** Art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Fabric/materials and intended effect supplied. **TASK:** Explore joining, layering, texture or surface manipulation appropriate to the materials. **REQUIREMENTS:** Include safe tool handling and realistic material limitations. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Experiment, technique, application, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Feasible and safe. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** KS1/KS2.

## TC-07 — Combine Media Purposefully
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Existing artwork/process and available media supplied. **TASK:** Plan a mixed-media experiment where each medium contributes a deliberate visual or tactile effect. **REQUIREMENTS:** Avoid adding media merely for novelty. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Media choice, purpose, sequence, test, reflection. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Coherent artistic rationale. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Unit theme.

## TC-08 — Diagnose Process Problems in Print/Textile Work
**ROLE:** Diagnostic art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil outcome, process and materials supplied. **TASK:** Identify plausible causes of an unwanted result. **REQUIREMENTS:** Separate material/process variables from pupil understanding or confidence. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, hypotheses, discriminating test, response. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Testable diagnosis. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Individual/group.

## TC-09 — Critique Collage, Print or Textile Work
**ROLE:** Primary art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Pupil work, intention and objective supplied. **TASK:** Generate feedback on material choices, composition, process and intended effect. **REQUIREMENTS:** Do not equate neatness or conformity with artistic quality. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Evidence, strength, question, next experiment. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Specific and constructive. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** Peer/self critique.

## TC-10 — Adapt Mixed-Media Practice for Inclusion
**ROLE:** Inclusive art teacher. **CONTEXT:** Practical activity and access need supplied. **TASK:** Remove barriers through tools, material size, positioning, timing or response alternatives. **REQUIREMENTS:** Preserve meaningful artistic choices and safe participation. **OUTPUT FORMAT:** Barrier, adaptation, retained objective, independence route. **QUALITY CHECKS:** Agency and construct preserved. **OPTIONAL CUSTOMISATION:** SEND/EAL.
