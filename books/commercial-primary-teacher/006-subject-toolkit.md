# Part 6: Subject Toolkit

A curated selection of subject-specific workflows from the verified primary subject libraries.

---

## Commercial Prompt 087: English reading retrieval questions

**Best for:** Checking explicit understanding of a text.

**Difficulty:** Beginner

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Use the supplied text only. Create [NUMBER] retrieval questions for [YEAR_GROUP] pupils. Each question must require pupils to locate or recall information explicitly stated in the text. Provide an accurate answer and evidence location for each. Do not turn retrieval into inference. Vary where the information appears in the text.
```

**Teacher tip:** Use retrieval before inference so pupils have a secure evidence base.

**Related prompts:** 088, 089

## Commercial Prompt 088: English inference questions

**Best for:** Developing evidence-based inference.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Using only the supplied text, create [NUMBER] inference questions for [YEAR_GROUP]. For each, provide the inference, textual evidence, reasoning linking evidence to inference and any defensible alternative interpretation. Clearly distinguish what the text states from what the reader infers. Do not invent evidence or background information.
```

**Related prompts:** 087, 089

## Commercial Prompt 089: English vocabulary in context

**Best for:** Selecting and teaching high-value vocabulary from a text.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Analyse the supplied text for [YEAR_GROUP] and select the [NUMBER] highest-value words for teaching. Prioritise words that matter to understanding, future vocabulary development or precise discussion. For each word give its meaning in context, pupil-friendly explanation, example and a short application task. Do not select words merely because they look difficult. Check every meaning against the context.
```

**Related prompts:** 087, 088

## Commercial Prompt 090: English guided reading sequence

**Best for:** Planning a structured reading lesson around a supplied text.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create a [DURATION]-minute guided reading sequence for [YEAR_GROUP] using the supplied text. Include retrieval, vocabulary teaching, evidence-based inference, one explanation question and a final response task. Keep every question grounded in the text. Provide teacher prompts, model answers and likely misconceptions. Do not invent quotations.
```

**Related prompts:** 087, 088, 089

## Commercial Prompt 091: Mathematics lesson sequence

**Best for:** Teaching a primary mathematics objective coherently.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create a [DURATION]-minute mathematics lesson for [YEAR_GROUP] on [OBJECTIVE]. Identify prerequisite knowledge, model the mathematical structure using useful representations, provide guided practice, independent practice and one reasoning item. Include likely misconceptions and diagnostic questions. Use purposeful variation. Verify every calculation, notation, unit and answer key before presenting the lesson.
```

**Related prompts:** 092, 093

## Commercial Prompt 092: Mathematics misconception diagnosis

**Best for:** Understanding why a pupil's mathematical answer is wrong.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Analyse the supplied mathematics responses for [YEAR_GROUP] against [OBJECTIVE]. Separate what each response demonstrates from hypotheses about the pupil's thinking. Group meaningful error patterns, suggest one diagnostic question for each pattern and recommend a short teaching response. Do not infer SEND or ability from the responses. Verify every mathematical statement.
```

**Related prompts:** 091, 093

## Commercial Prompt 093: Mathematics reasoning problems

**Best for:** Creating problems that require reasoning rather than routine calculation.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create [NUMBER] mathematical reasoning problems for [YEAR_GROUP] on [OBJECTIVE]. Require pupils to explain, justify, compare, generalise or identify possibilities rather than simply calculate. Provide complete answers and reasoning. Include one common misconception and a teacher question that exposes it. Check every numerical value and answer independently.
```

## Commercial Prompt 094: Science practical enquiry plan

**Best for:** Planning a manageable scientific enquiry.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create a practical science enquiry for [YEAR_GROUP] on [QUESTION]. State the scientific idea, variables where relevant, equipment, method, safety considerations, prediction, observation/data collection, analysis and conclusion. Keep the procedure realistic for a primary classroom. Distinguish observation from inference and flag anything the teacher should verify before use.
```

**Related prompts:** 095, 096

## Commercial Prompt 095: Science explanation with misconception check

**Best for:** Explaining a science concept accurately.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Explain [SCIENCE_CONCEPT] to [YEAR_GROUP] pupils. Start from relevant prior knowledge, use one accurate example, identify two likely misconceptions and create short checks that distinguish correct understanding from the misconception. Use precise scientific language while keeping the explanation age appropriate. Do not simplify the science into an inaccurate statement.
```

**Related prompts:** 094, 096

## Commercial Prompt 097: History enquiry question

**Best for:** Building a historical lesson around a genuine enquiry.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Design a historical enquiry for [YEAR_GROUP] on [TOPIC]. Create a clear enquiry question, identify the historical thinking involved, sequence the evidence pupils need, and provide questions that develop chronology, causation, change, significance, interpretation or comparison as appropriate. Do not invent primary-source quotations or provenance. Clearly label claims that need verification.
```

**Related prompts:** 098, 099

## Commercial Prompt 098: History source analysis

**Best for:** Teaching pupils to use historical evidence.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Analyse the supplied historical source for [YEAR_GROUP]. Separate what the source directly shows or says from what pupils may infer. Identify provenance information supplied by the teacher, possible usefulness and limitations, and questions pupils should ask before using it as evidence. Do not invent provenance. End with a short source-analysis activity and model response.
```

**Related prompts:** 097, 099

## Commercial Prompt 099: History chronology and change

**Best for:** Developing chronology and change-and-continuity thinking.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create a [YEAR_GROUP] history sequence for [TOPIC] showing the essential chronology, key developments and important examples of change and continuity. Distinguish secure historical facts from interpretation. Include a timeline activity, comparison questions and one question requiring pupils to explain why a change mattered. Verify dates and names before presenting the material.
```

**Related prompts:** 097, 100
