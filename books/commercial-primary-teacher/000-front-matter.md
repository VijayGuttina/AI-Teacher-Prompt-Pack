## How to use this book

1. Find the teacher task you need help with.
2. Read **Best for** and **Use when**.
3. Replace the editable variables with your own information.
4. Copy the prompt exactly as shown.
5. Paste it into your approved AI assistant.
6. Review the result against your curriculum, pupils, resources and school expectations.
7. Adapt the final material before using it with pupils.

You should not need to rewrite the prompt itself for normal use.

## A simple rule for better results

Give the AI enough context to understand the job, but do not provide information that it does not need.

Useful context often includes:

- Year group
- Subject
- Topic
- Learning objective
- Lesson duration
- Prior learning
- Resources available
- Attainment range
- Accessibility requirements

Avoid unnecessary pupil-identifying information.

---

## Responsible use

AI can help with preparation. It should not make high-stakes professional decisions for you.

Always check:

- factual accuracy;
- curriculum alignment;
- mathematical, scientific and historical accuracy where relevant;
- age appropriateness;
- accessibility;
- safeguarding implications;
- whether the output genuinely serves the learning objective.

Where a prompt analyses pupil work, treat AI conclusions as hypotheses for professional consideration, not diagnoses.
