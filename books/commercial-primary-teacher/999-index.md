## Assessment coverage

The commercial edition deliberately concentrates assessment on the strongest currently completed workflows. Further assessment workflows remain part of the master library roadmap rather than a prerequisite for this product.

---

## Adaptation coverage

The Adapt section focuses on mainstream classroom use of SEND, accessibility, EAL and adaptive-teaching principles. It does not attempt to reproduce the full SEND specialist library.

---

## How to find a workflow

Search by the job you need to complete rather than by subject alone. Many workflows can be reused across several subjects by changing the variables.
