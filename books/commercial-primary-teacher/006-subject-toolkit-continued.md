# Part 6: Subject Toolkit continued

## Commercial Prompt 101: Geography place and location comparison

**Best for:** Comparing places through meaningful geographical concepts.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create a comparison of [PLACE_A] and [PLACE_B] for [YEAR_GROUP] using supplied or verified information. Organise it around location, physical geography, human geography, climate or another stated focus. Include evidence-based questions and one explanation task requiring pupils to account for a difference. Clearly flag information that requires verification.
```

**Related prompts:** 100, 102

## Commercial Prompt 102: Geography map and data interpretation

**Best for:** Teaching pupils to interpret geographical information.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create [NUMBER] geography questions from the supplied map, table, graph or dataset. Include retrieval, comparison, pattern identification and at least one explanation question. Every answer must be supported by the supplied evidence. Do not invent values, locations or trends. Provide an answer key and identify the geographical skill assessed.
```

**Related prompts:** 100, 101

## Commercial Prompt 103: Computing concept explanation

**Best for:** Explaining computing concepts without unnecessary technical language.

**Difficulty:** Intermediate

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Explain [COMPUTING_CONCEPT] to [YEAR_GROUP] pupils. Start from prior knowledge, define essential vocabulary, use one accurate analogy where helpful, give a worked example and identify one common misconception. Finish with three questions that check conceptual understanding before pupils begin practical work. Do not assume software features that have not been supplied or verified.
```

**Related prompts:** 104, 105

**Best for:** Teaching debugging and computational thinking.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text

Provide a short intentionally flawed example, explain the intended behaviour, ask pupils to identify and justify the error, and provide a corrected version with reasoning. Keep the code appropriate for the stated environment and do not assume unavailable features.

## Commercial Prompt 115: Religious Education enquiry lesson

**Best for:** Enquiry-led RE that represents beliefs and worldviews responsibly.

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Copy and paste:**
```text
Create an RE enquiry for [YEAR_GROUP] exploring [QUESTION/TOPIC]. Distinguish belief, practice, interpretation and lived experience. Where a tradition contains internal diversity, represent that diversity rather than presenting one view as universal. Include age-appropriate evidence, discussion questions and a reflective task. Do not invent quotations or claim that all members of a religion believe the same thing.
```
