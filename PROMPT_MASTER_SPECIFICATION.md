| Topic | Persuasive Writing |

| Lesson Duration | 60 minutes |

| EAL | One pupil new to English |

---

# Example Output (Excerpt)

> **Lesson Overview**
>
> Pupils explore the features of persuasive writing before jointly constructing a persuasive letter. The lesson concludes with independent writing supported by differentiated success criteria and teacher conferencing.

> **Key Vocabulary**
>
> persuade, audience, rhetorical question, emotive language, conjunction, evidence

> **Teacher Modelling**
>
> Model writing the opening paragraph while verbalising the decision-making process behind vocabulary choices and sentence construction.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Omitting the year group.
- Providing vague learning objectives.
- Forgetting SEND or EAL requirements.
- Requesting unrealistic lesson durations.
- Leaving the curriculum unspecified.
